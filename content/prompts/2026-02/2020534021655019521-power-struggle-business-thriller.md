  Shot 1 (Wide Shot, Static): Two partners, [Character A] and [Character B], stand far apart by a massive window. [Character A] paces restlessly and says loudly: '[Dialogue A, e.g., Fifty percent. That was the agreement!]' [Character B] stands still, back to him.
