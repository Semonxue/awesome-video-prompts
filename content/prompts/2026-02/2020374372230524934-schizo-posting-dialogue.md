description: |
  【@图1】是 A，【@图2】是 B，以下是他们的对话：【English Script】

  (Context: A is looking in a mirror or posing)

  A: "Someone told me I look like a model today."

  B (Without looking up from phone): "Who?"

  A: "The voices in my head."

  B: "Tell them to shut up."

  A: "They said they hate you."

  这是一个典型的美式"Schizo posting"（精神分裂梗）。A说"The voices in my head"时要表现得极度自豪。最后一句A怼回去的时候，B最好给一个**翻白眼**的镜头。，使用英文作为视频语言，使用幽默搞笑风格的数字人形象
