description: |
  CENE 1 — The "Hidden Observer" (Tension & Reveal)
  Best for: Horror, Drama, or Mystery. A character discovers something they shouldn't.

  Variables:

  [Observer]: The person watching (e.g., small boy, detective, spy).

  [Conflict]: The people arguing/fighting inside.

  [Dialogues]: What is being yelled?

  Prompt Template: Fixed: A continuous cinematic sequence in a dimly lit [Location, e.g., house/corridor]. Shot 1 (Medium Shot, Tracking): A [Observer, e.g., small 6-year-old boy] walks slowly down a dark hallway, rubbing his eyes. He hears muffled shouting and stops in front of a slightly ajar door. Shot 2 (POV Shot from door crack): The view shifts to the [Observer]'s perspective looking through the narrow vertical crack of the door. Inside a bright room, we see [Subject A & Subject B] arguing fiercely, shadows moving across the wall. We hear [Subject A] yell: '[Dialogue 1, e.g., It's all gone! What do we do now?]' Shot 3 (Inside Room, Medium Shot): Cut to inside the room. [Subject B] is crying/angry, yelling back: '[Dialogue 2, e.g., Stop shouting! You'll wake him up!]' Shot 4 (Slow Zoom Out Reveal): The camera slowly zooms out from the arguing couple. The door creaks open wider, revealing the [Observer] standing in the doorway illuminated by the light. The couple instantly stops fighting and looks at him in stunned silence. Technical Details: Warm indoor lighting contrasted with dark hallway, realistic POV effect, emotional shift.
