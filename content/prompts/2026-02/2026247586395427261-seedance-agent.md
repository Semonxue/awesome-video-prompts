You can throw images, videos, sounds, but you don't have to reference them specifically, the agent will try to figure it out and usually it works perfectly. One thing I wanted to see if it can grab a starting frame of a car, and apply a pre-defined camera movement on it even if the starting frame doesn't 100% match the 3D first frame. Used Blender+MCP+Claude to create the 3D scene and for the camera movement. It is not perfect - as you see the second half using difference blending mode, also for example the driver is not holding the wheel - but it's good enough to give you more control about your generations. #seedance2"
