Create a 15-second ultra-cinematic futuristic city transformation sequence.
Scene starts at night in a slightly old, realistic South Asian megacity with dim street lights, traffic, and dense urban chaos. The camera slowly flies forward between buildings in a smooth drone motion.
At 3–5 seconds, subtle technological changes begin: street lights shift into soft neon glow, digital holographic ads start appearing in the air, and building surfaces slowly upgrade into sleek smart glass and metallic structures.
At 6–10 seconds, the transformation accelerates dramatically. Roads become intelligent glowing pathways, AI interfaces appear in the environment, people interact with floating holograms, and autonomous flying transport vehicles move through the sky in smooth traffic patterns.
At 10–13 seconds, the entire city fully evolves into a futuristic mega-city: ultra-tall smart skyscrapers, neon cyberpunk lighting, floating transit highways in the sky, and clean, organized energy flow throughout the environment.
Final 2 seconds: wide aerial shot revealing the complete transformed city—alive, glowing, intelligent, and deeply connected with AI life. Subtle cinematic lens flare, volumetric lighting, ultra-detailed, hyper-realistic, 8K, smooth motion, sci-fi aesthetic, emotional awe feeling.
STYLE: cinematic realism, cyber-futuristic, ultra-detailed, smooth camera motion, dramatic lighting, high-end film quality.
