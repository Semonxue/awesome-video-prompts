Prompt 1 — "The Bunker Ending" (Single Continuous Shot, 15s)

A single continuous high-action shot on an overcast evening overlooking a massive bridge and ocean coastline. Wind blowing hard, distant thunder, cinematic disaster-movie atmosphere. Sound: crashing waves, rumbling sky, emotional shouting.

The shot starts close on a woman arguing with a man near a railing overlooking the water. Both emotional, tense.

She shoves him slightly and says:
"This is why we can't be together!"
As she says it, the camera subtly shifts behind them—

Far in the sky, a massive burning meteor streaks downward toward the ocean.

Neither notices at first.

A distant impact.

The horizon suddenly erupts—water shoots upward, shockwave rolling across the sea. The bridge begins shaking violently.

They both turn.

Silence for half a second.

The ocean starts rising unnaturally—a gigantic tsunami forming.

The woman whispers:
"I guess this is it…"

The man suddenly grabs her hand hard:
"No, baby—run!"
He pulls her uphill toward a hidden steel bunker hatch built into the hillside nearby. The ground trembles harder. Cars on the bridge begin crashing.

Behind them, the tsunami rapidly grows larger.
He reaches the hatch, spins the wheel lock open, shoves her inside first.

At the very last second, he dives in after her as the wave crashes toward them.

The bunker door SLAMS shut from the force.

Darkness.

End.

Prompt 2 — "The Smart Couple" (Single Continuous Shot, 15s)

A single continuous handheld-style shot inside a dim underground bunker after a global disaster. Emergency red lights glow softly. Water drips from pipes. Sound: distant muffled ocean impacts, metal creaking.

The shot starts on the man and woman sitting on the bunker floor, soaked, breathing heavily after barely surviving.

Behind them, through a thick circular reinforced window, the outside world is underwater. Pieces of the destroyed bridge float past.

A shark suddenly swims across the window.

Then another.

One bumps lightly into the glass.

The woman stares at it in disbelief.

The man looks at her, calm, slightly smug.

"You really thought I was gonna let us die like those other idiots making these apocalypse reels?"

He gestures proudly around the bunker.

"Nah… I actually had a plan for us, baby."

Outside the window, another shark drifts past awkwardly carrying debris from the bridge.

The woman starts laughing in relief.

He pulls her close.

They kiss while outside the bunker the flooded apocalypse continues silently.

The camera slowly drifts toward the bunker window as debris and sharks float by outside.

End.
