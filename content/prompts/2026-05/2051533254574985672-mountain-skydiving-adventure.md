Create a 15-second ultra-realistic cinematic vertical (9:16) action sequence.

Scene Style:
High-adrenaline cinematic adventure with breathtaking natural landscapes. Ultra-detailed visuals, dynamic camera movement, slow-motion aerial shots, and immersive sound design.

Sequence:

0–3s:
A helicopter flies above massive snow-covered mountains under a bright blue sky. Wind is strong, clouds moving fast below. Door opens, revealing a skydiver preparing to jump.

3–7s:
The skydiver leaps from the helicopter into open air. Camera follows in third-person cinematic tracking shot. Freefall through clouds with spinning mountain peaks below. Wind rushing, suit flapping realistically.

7–11s:
Parachute opens with a powerful snap. The character glides smoothly between snowy peaks, drifting toward a wide icy plateau. Golden sunlight reflects off snow and ice.

11–15s:
Soft landing on a frozen mountain lake. The character removes helmet, smiling. They begin sliding and playing on the ice, spinning and skating freely, joyfully interacting with the icy surface. Wide cinematic pull-back showing vast mountain landscape.
