15-SECOND CINEMATIC ACTION SEQUENCE — BATTLE ATOP THE SKY-TRAIN

0.0–2.0s: Sweeping aerial drone shot rockets through cathedral clouds, revealing the rust-streaked blue locomotive thundering across a colossal stone viaduct suspended between floating gothic citadels. Camera dives like a falcon toward the speeding roof.

2.0–4.5s: Whip-pan to roof level. The chrome-armored swordsman (white hair, mirror-polished cybernetic body, snow-white fur cloak) rises from a kneeling stance, drawing his glowing katana in a slow-motion bullet-time arc. Sparks and ash trail behind him as wind tears at his cape. Camera orbits 180° around him at hyper-speed.

4.5–7.0s: Hard cut to the rival — a faceless obsidian-plated phantom wrapped in massive billowing veils of translucent silver silk, gliding forward across the train cars like a ghost. Low-angle tracking dolly, silk ribbons whipping past lens, motion-blurred clouds streaking behind. He unsheathes a thin mirror-blade.

7.0–9.0s: Clash. Blades collide mid-air in an explosive shockwave — frame freezes for a split second, sparks frozen like diamonds, then snaps back into 120fps slow-motion. Camera spirals around them in a full 360° matrix-style rotation as the train banks hard around a tower.

9.0–11.5s: Rapid-fire exchange — three strikes, parry, kick. Handheld shaky cam slams between over-the-shoulder POVs. The phantom's silk veils slice apart and reform like liquid mercury. The swordsman dashes along the train's edge, footsteps denting steel, cloak snapping like a war banner.

11.5–13.5s: Crane shot pulls back violently as both warriors leap between train cars, blades trailing white plasma streaks. A floating cathedral looms ahead; the train roars beneath an arch, plunging the fight into strobing shadow and shafts of god-light.

13.5–15.0s: Final clash — extreme close-up on locked blades, reflections of both faces in the steel. Camera barrel-rolls upward into the sky as a sonic boom of light erupts outward, engulfing the screen in blinding white.

Style: Ghibli-meets-Blade-Runner, anime cinematic realism, volumetric clouds, lens flares, motion blur, 35mm anamorphic, hyper-detailed, orchestral taiko score, 24fps with selective 120fps slow-mo bursts.
