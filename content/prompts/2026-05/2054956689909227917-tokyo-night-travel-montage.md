Create a stunning 15-second cinematic travel montage through Tokyo at night and golden hour, featuring seamless transitions between the city's most iconic locations. Style should feel emotional, energetic, modern, and visually breathtaking — like a luxury travel commercial mixed with an anime-inspired urban dream.
Visual Direction
Ultra-cinematic photography, realistic lighting, smooth camera motion, rich neon reflections, atmospheric depth, vibrant but tasteful color grading, shallow depth of field, occasional slow motion moments, dynamic city energy, premium tourism-ad aesthetic.
Scene Sequence (fast-paced but smooth)
Aerial sunrise over the Tokyo skyline with Tokyo Tower glowing in warm light
Hyper-lapse crossing at Shibuya Crossing with crowds and neon billboards
Rainy neon alleyways in Shinjuku, reflections on wet streets
Peaceful cinematic shot of Senso-ji with lanterns and drifting smoke
Fast train transition featuring the Shinkansen rushing past
Evening skyline from Tokyo Skytree observation view
Cozy ramen shop close-up, steam rising, locals laughing
Final dramatic night aerial of Tokyo filled with neon lights and moving traffic, ending with glowing text: "TOKYO"
Camera & Motion
Drone shots, FPV fly-throughs, smooth gimbal movement, whip-pan transitions, cinematic zooms, speed ramps synced to music beats, realistic crowd motion.
Music Direction
Use emotionally uplifting Japanese-inspired electronic cinematic music with soft piano intro, energetic beat drop, modern synthwave and orchestral fusion. Music should build progressively with perfect beat-sync transitions between scenes. Add subtle city ambience: train sounds, crowd chatter, rain, distant announcements.

Duration: exactly 15 seconds

24fps film look
HDR lighting
Smooth motion blur
Highly detailed environments
Natural human movement
Seamless scene blending
No glitches, no distorted faces, no text artifacts except final "TOKYO" title
