Household Chores — Cinematic Sequence Prompt (Seedance 2.0)

Generate a single continuous cinematic video of a young woman performing a 16-step household chore routine in a clean, minimal indoor setting. The character must exactly match the provided reference image in face, skin tone, and proportions, maintaining consistent identity throughout. Natural makeup, soft expression, realistic look.

She wears a modest modern outfit: fitted crop top (non-revealing) with high-waisted straight-fit jeans, optional minimal sneakers or barefoot. Clothing should respond naturally to movement.

Structure & Flow
The video should feel like a smooth, choreographed routine with seamless transitions between actions (no hard cuts). Maintain spatial continuity within the same home environment. Include slight pauses for clarity and natural movement between tasks.

16-Step Sequence (in order)
Make bed → Tidy room → Dust surfaces → Vacuum → Sweep → Mop → Load laundry → Hang clothes → Fold clothes → Clean kitchen counter → Wash dishes → Take out trash → Water plants → Clean bathroom → Organize shelves → Final reset (subtle satisfied pose)

Motion Style
Fluid, rhythmic, intentional movements (lightly dance-like but realistic). Clear action readability with continuous flow between steps.

Camera & Cinematography
Mostly full-body framing, occasional medium shots for interaction. Slow tracking, subtle handheld feel. Use match cuts or fluid transitions only—no abrupt edits.

Visual Style
Natural color (no grayscale), soft daylight lighting, minimal modern interior, realistic textures and shadows.

Consistency Rules
Same character, same outfit, same environment throughout. No extra people, no identity drift.

Output
25–40 seconds, polished lifestyle-commercial quality, like an aesthetic home productivity vlog.
