Create a 15-second 16:9 animated instructional cooking video that follows the 10-shot sequence exactly. Preserve the same chef, kitchen, props, ingredients, and premium 3D stylized clean instructional aesthetic.

Rules: 
- Strictly follow the 10-shot sequence in order
- One shot per panel, about 1.5 seconds each
- Do not change the order or skip any step
- Keep rolling actions readable and elegant
- Maintain continuity across the entire sequence

Shot sequence: 
1. Show ingredient layout 
2. Wet hands and spread rice on nori 
3. Leave top edge clear 
4. Add fillings in a neat row 
5. Tuck and begin rolling 
6. Compress evenly for a tight roll 
7. Finish rolling and seal edge 
8. Slice into clean even pieces 
9. Arrange sushi with garnish 
10. Present finished sushi plate

Camera: Overhead for setup and spreading, 45-degree close-up for rolling actions, tight detail for slicing and plating, front-facing presentation at end.

Style: Bright clean kitchen light, precise, calm, elegant movement, premium 3D stylized realism, fast but readable instructional pacing.
