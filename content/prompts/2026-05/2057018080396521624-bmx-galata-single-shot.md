Create a hyper-cinematic extreme street BMX video. image1 = strict identity and outfit reference for the rider: keep her exact face, blue eyes, long straight black hair, body proportions, white sporty cropped top, airy white mesh skirt, white socks, white sneakers, and natural skin texture. Do NOT redesign the outfit, do NOT change her identity, do NOT make her look like a fashion model or superhero; she must feel like a real ordinary person unexpectedly performing insane BMX tricks. The mesh skirt stays light and airy, fluttering naturally with speed, jumps, manuals, bunny hops, landings, skids, and grinds; under it, keep a clean white fitted athletic underlayer / swim-bottom-like underlayer, visible only naturally during motion, athletic and tasteful, non-glamorous. Galata: steep historic Istanbul streets around Galata Tower at sunrise, narrow wet cobblestone road, warm golden light, visible Galata Tower, closed cafés, shutters, Turkish signage, old European-style façades, subtle graffiti, stone walls, slight morning humidity, reflective puddles; keep it raw, local, cinematic, believable, never touristy, not a static copy of the reference framing. One continuous real-time 15-second shot, no cuts. Opening shot: ultra-tight handheld close-up on her focused face while already riding a BMX manual with the front wheel raised, hair moving, breath visible in the cool air, strong sense of downhill speed. In a single move, the camera rapidly pulls back on a low 24–35mm lens to a wider low front three-quarter tracking shot revealing handlebars, lifted front wheel, her whole body, and the sunrise-lit street with Galata Tower behind. She rides straight toward camera, holding the manual, front wheel high but believable, skirt fluttering, tires vibrating on wet cobblestones. She passes extremely close beside the lens; the operator whip-pans with aggressive motion blur and swings into a rear chase position. From this moment the camera stays locked behind her at rear wheel level, immersive handheld, hugging the ground, dynamically reframing as she pedals hard downhill, briefly re-lifts the front wheel, then stands tall on the pedals in an attack stance to carve a tight downhill corner between stone walls and metal shutters, sparks and water droplets kicking from the tires. Mid-shot, she flows into a technical sequence: realistic bunny hop over a low curb, heavy compressed landing, visible body balance corrections, then an immediate fast corner. She spots a narrow stone side stairway branching off with a black metal handrail. Still in the same shot, she lines up, bunny hops onto the rail, both BMX pegs locking into a fast but controlled peg grind down the handrail, metal-on-metal friction, subtle sparks, skirt fluttering from the speed, body low and centered. The camera dives even lower, nearly scraping steps and railing, chasing just behind and slightly offset. At the base, she pops out of the grind with a whip-like rotation, lands hard but clean on wet cobblestones, suspension and legs compressing realistically. Immediately she throws a controlled rear-wheel skid, back tire sliding sideways across the slick stones to shed speed and redirect, a fine mist of water spraying outward, then she catches traction smoothly and drives forward again. She keeps full momentum, slightly lifting the front wheel one last time and pedaling aggressively away from Galata Tower down the street as the handheld chase camera stays glued to her rear wheel and back.
