15-second cinematic action sequence set inside a colossal stadium arena drowned in stark blue stadium floodlights, thick rolling smoke hugging a wet mirror-like concrete floor that reflects every beam of light.

0–3s: Ultra-low dolly-in shot skimming across the glossy floor, water droplets parting as the camera rockets toward the center. Two silhouettes ignite through the mist — on the left, the White Sword Maiden, raven-black ponytail whipping skyward, translucent silk robes billowing like phantom smoke, gleaming straight blade raised in a high guard stance. On the right, the Crimson Silver Sorceress, long platinum-white hair cascading, red-glass armor and silver tribal jewelry chiming, crouched low with swirling glass orbs and water ribbons orbiting her body.

3–6s: Whip-pan 360° orbit around both fighters as they explode forward. Time slows to 120fps. The swordswoman leaps, robes trailing comet-like vapor; the sorceress launches glass spheres that shatter into prismatic shards mid-air. Camera barrel-rolls between the projectiles, lens flares streaking across the frame.

6–9s: Hyper-speed crash zoom into the clash — sword meets a conjured silver gauntlet of liquid mercury. Sparks erupt, shockwave ripples outward parting the smoke and rattling the stadium lights. Camera snaps to a Dutch-angle close-up of locked eyes: cold determination versus crimson defiance.

9–12s: Bullet-time arc shot. The sorceress backflips, red silk pants flaring, hurling a spiraling vortex of water and glass. The swordswoman spins midair, her blade carving a luminous crescent that splits the vortex in two. Camera corkscrews upward through the falling debris.

12–15s: Crane pull-back rising fast above the arena as both warriors land back-to-back in opposing stances, smoke exploding outward in a perfect ring, stadium floodlights flaring into a blinding starburst. Final freeze on a god's-eye overhead shot — two tiny figures, one white, one red, at the heart of a glowing battlefield.
