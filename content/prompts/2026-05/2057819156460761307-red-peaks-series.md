A transparent water slide descends from the stratosphere with the Earth in the background. The character wears a t-shirt and swimsuit.

He speeds down the slide, enjoying the ride.

The character is seen hurtling towards Earth on the transparent water slide and says to the camera in English:

"Just by participating in this race, I earn 5 years' worth of cell regeneration credits. I have nothing to lose at my age."

He smiles at the camera.

He quickly approaches the slide, passing through a tropical rainforest with a fast-moving, turbulent river, and ends up riding in a speedboat at full speed.
