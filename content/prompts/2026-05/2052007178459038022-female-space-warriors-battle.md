Two opposing teams of female space-warriors. Each warrior wears sleek, form-fitting black tech-armor that combines tactical utility with high-fashion aesthetics. They have agile builds, glowing cybernetic accents on their suits, and distinct, ethereal appearances.

Scene: Deep space, dark void punctuated by distant, colossal, vibrant planets and nebulae. Two massive, glowing, spherical spacecraft hover in the background, casting rhythmic pulse-lighting onto the combatants.

Style: High-octane sci-fi, epic cinematic scale, high-contrast lighting, particle-heavy combat.

Lighting: Sharp, cold rim light from the spacecraft, fiery orange-yellow sparks from clashing blades, deep black shadows of the void.

Audio: Low, thrumming hum of spacecraft, metallic ringing of clashing swords, roaring jets of flame, distorted robotic voice commands through comms, silence of the vacuum (except for localized combat sounds).

[0-3s] Wide establishing shot of the vast, dark void. Two massive, glowing, spherical spacecraft drift slowly, their hull lights blinking rhythmically.

Camera: Slow push-in toward the space between the ships.

[3-6s] Dozens of warriors propel themselves from the hangar bays of both ships. They move with fluid, dance-like grace through zero-G.

Camera: Tracking shot following the converging groups as they draw glowing swords.

[6-9s] The two groups collide in the center. The warriors engage in high-speed, acrobatic combat. Every strike of their swords releases long, cinematic trails of fire and sparks.

Camera: Dynamic handheld orbit, rotating around the center of the fight.

[9-12s] Tight shots of individuals landing strikes. The warriors receive holographic tactical displays from their respective ships appearing near their ears. They exchange swift, lethal blows while dodging through the debris of combat.

Camera: Close-up tracking, fast-paced pans between opponents.

Vfx: Volumetric fire, lens flares, glowing UI interfaces, sparks flying into the void.

Audio: Metallic "clang" of swords, sizzling sound of fire in a vacuum, synthesized voice chatter, intense, fast-paced orchestral percussion.
