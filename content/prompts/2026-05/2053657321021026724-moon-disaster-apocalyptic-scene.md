Style: Ultra-cinematic apocalyptic realism, IMAX-scale destruction atmosphere, realistic VFX, volumetric moonlight, dramatic storm clouds, emotional human reactions, handheld realism mixed with massive aerial compositions, intense cinematic sound design, film-grade color grading, hyper-detailed environmental destruction.

0–3s — The Impossible Appears
Wide aerial shot over a crowded futuristic mega city at night. Traffic moves normally beneath heavy storm clouds until people suddenly stop and stare upward. Between towering skyscrapers, the moon appears impossibly massive and unnaturally close, slowly descending toward Earth. Thousands of phones rise to record the sky as distant emergency sirens begin echoing through the city.

3–6s — Fear Spreads Through the Streets
Rapid cinematic close-ups of terrified faces illuminated by pale moonlight. Windows tremble violently across nearby buildings. Helicopters circle dangerously close to skyscrapers while rooftop debris crashes into the streets below. A deep low-frequency bass rumble grows stronger, shaking the environment with invisible pressure.

6–10s — Collapse of Reality
Massive wide shot: the moon now dominates half the sky, detailed craters clearly visible across its surface. Its overwhelming glow floods the city with cold white light. Panic erupts everywhere—crowds run through intersections, cars collide, alarms scream, and papers spiral through the air in slow motion. The atmosphere feels heavy, surreal, and catastrophic.

10–13s — Humanity in the End
Quiet emotional close-up of a young child gripping their mother's hand while staring silently upward. Tears reflect the enormous glowing moon above. In the background, entire city blocks lose power one by one, darkness spreading across streets and skyscrapers like a wave consuming civilization.

13–15s — Final Descent
Ultra-wide IMAX-style skyline shot. The moon hangs terrifyingly close above the skyscrapers, surrounded by violently swirling storm clouds. Every sound suddenly cuts out into complete silence. One second later, an overwhelming white flash engulfs the entire frame, ending abruptly in pure light.
