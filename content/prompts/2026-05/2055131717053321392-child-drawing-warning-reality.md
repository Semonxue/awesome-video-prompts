A cinematic emotional mystery where imagination begins altering reality.

0–3s: A quiet child sits alone at a kitchen table during a rainy evening, drawing with crayons while thunder echoes outside. The drawing shows a city street covered in fire and chaos.

3–6s: Hours later — news broadcasts reveal the exact same street burning in real life. Parents stare in shock at the child's drawing while emergency sirens sound outside.

6–9s: The child continues drawing rapidly — airplanes falling from the sky, giant cracks in the ground, glowing lights above the city. Quick cinematic cuts show each event becoming reality moments later.

9–12s: Government agents and scientists rush into the child's home while televisions worldwide display increasingly impossible disasters matching the drawings perfectly. The child remains calm, silently sketching.

12–15s: Final emotional shot. The child slowly finishes one final drawing — Earth split apart beneath a dark cosmic sky. The camera pulls back as distant rumbling begins outside and the lights flicker out.
