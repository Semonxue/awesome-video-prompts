Crazy 1980s fantasy-action blockbuster energy at night.
A military Jeep races through a mountain forest road while a gigantic fire-breathing dragon hunts them from above and behind. Exploding trees, flaming roads, helicopter extraction energy, loud panic, giant practical fireballs, and a massive rocket-launcher payoff.

⸻

Shot 1 (0s–2s)

Ultra-wide NIGHT aerial shot.

A military Jeep speeds through a dark forest mountain road.

Suddenly the ENTIRE road behind it ERUPTS INTO FIRE.

A gigantic dragon dives low overhead, breathing a massive stream of fire directly toward the speeding vehicle.

Trees explode into flames instantly.

⸻

Shot 2 (2s–5s)

Inside the violently shaking Jeep.

Orange firelight flickers across terrified faces.

A muscular action-hero type man grabs the dashboard while yelling into a walkie-talkie.

A fearless woman driver drifts around burning wreckage.

In the back:
a panicking comic-relief guy screams:
"IT'S LITERALLY BREATHING FIRE! THAT SHOULD NOT EXIST!"

Behind them:
dragon claws smash through trees beside the road.

⸻

Shot 3 (5s–8s)

Cut to a rocky mountain ridge overlooking the canyon road.

A battle-hardened mercenary kneels beside a massive rocket launcher.

Wind whipping around him.

Dragon roars echo through the valley.

Walkie-talkie crackles:
"Bring it closer."

Below:
the Jeep bursts through flaming forest while the dragon glides directly above them.

⸻

Shot 4 (8s–11s)

The dragon swoops LOW behind the Jeep.

Its fire breath engulfs the road completely.

Flames nearly swallow the vehicle as the woman driver crashes through burning debris.

The dragon opens its jaws wide directly above the Jeep.

The muscular guy screams into the radio:
"TAKE THE SHOT NOW!"

Comic-relief guy:
"WE ARE COOKED!"

⸻

Shot 5 (11s–13s)

Slow-motion blockbuster insanity.

The rocket launcher FIRES from the mountain ridge.

Rocket streaks through smoke and fire.

The dragon lunges downward toward the Jeep mid-fire-breath.

DIRECT HIT into the inside of its glowing throat.

⸻

Shot 6 (13s–15s)

MASSIVE MID-AIR EXPLOSION.

The dragon detonates in a gigantic fireball above the canyon.

Burning wings tear apart through the night sky.

Shockwave flips the Jeep sideways through flaming trees while burning debris rains across the mountain road and the giant flaming dragon crashes into the forest behind them.

⸻

The dragon's fire breath engulfing the road directly behind the speeding Jeep in frame one.
