[0:00-0:15] The Hook The engine screamed—a mechanical howl that echoed off the canyon walls. Kael shifted into fifth gear, the desert blurred into a streak of orange and gold. Behind him, three black interceptors hovered just inches off the ground, their plasma cannons charging with a high-pitched whine.

[0:15-0:30] The Conflict "Package is hot, Kael! You've got a lock-on!" the radio barked. A red light flashed on his HUD. Beep. Beep. BEEP. He didn't flinch. He yanked the steering yoke, sending his sand-skipper into a violent 360-degree spin. A bolt of blue energy hissed past his ear, vaporizing a boulder where his head had been a second before.

[0:30-0:45] The Risk Ahead lay 'The Needle'—a gap in the rock barely wider than his ship. If he hit it at this speed, he was a dead man. If he braked, he was a prisoner. Kael's thumb hovered over the "Overload" button. "Gravity is just a suggestion," he muttered. He punched the thrusters. The world stretched. His vision tunneled.

[0:45-1:00] The Climax The ship scraped the canyon walls, throwing a shower of white-hot sparks that blinded the pursuers. He burst through the other side into the open salt flats, the sun setting behind him. One final surge of speed, and he was gone—leaving nothing but a trail of dust and the faint scent of ozone
