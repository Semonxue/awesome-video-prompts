Create a 30-second ultra-realistic cinematic lifestyle vlog in 16:9 format featuring a naturally beautiful young British woman in her early 20s with fair glossy skin, ocean-blue eyes, long balayage blonde hair, lip gloss, and acrylic nails.
Begin with a morning desk scene where she accidentally spills a small amount of coffee, laughs softly, and wipes it while saying in a British accent: "Oh, brilliant… that's exactly what I needed." Transition into fast cinematic getting-ready cuts showing her applying lip gloss, fixing her hair, putting on earrings, checking her nails, and grabbing her handbag, with a voiceover: "Right, let's try this again."
Cut to her walking through a busy city street while texting on her phone, moving confidently and naturally.
At 15 seconds, shift to a cozy café interior where she sits calmly, sips coffee, and works on her laptop with a relaxed smile, saying: "Not gonna lie, this coffee's actually brilliant."
End with a golden-hour rooftop scene. Wind gently moves her hair as she looks directly into the camera with a playful expression and says: "Looks completely real, doesn't it? This entire video was made with AI." Add on-screen text: "AI Life — Real & Relatable."
