Pure urban-disaster insanity. A gigantic armored Kaiju crashes through a mega shopping mall while civilians stampede through collapsing escalators and exploding storefronts. The creature moves like an angry animal trapped indoors — smashing through floors, glass ceilings, and neon signs while military helicopters attack from above.

⸻

Shot 1 (0s–2s) — THE HOOK

Inside a crowded mega mall.

The ENTIRE ceiling suddenly caves inward.

A gigantic Kaiju claw SMASHES through multiple floors at once while people scatter screaming.

⸻

Shot 2 (2s–5s)

Escalators collapse.

Glass storefronts explode outward.

The Kaiju forces its massive head through the structure roaring while helicopters fire missiles through the shattered ceiling.

⸻

Shot 3 (5s–8s)

People sprint through smoke-filled corridors while the Kaiju tail smashes through a food court behind them.

Tables and neon signs fly everywhere.

⸻

Shot 4 (8s–11s)

A survivor jumps onto a collapsing hanging banner as the floor below caves inward into darkness.

The Kaiju eye suddenly appears through smoke beside him.

⸻

Shot 5 (11s–15s) — PAYOFF

The entire mall collapses around the Kaiju while it bursts outward through the front entrance into the city street surrounded by fire and falling debris.

⸻

Key Visual Hook

The Kaiju claw smashing downward through multiple floors of the mall instantly.

⸻

Why It Will Go Viral

Confined-space giant-monster destruction inside a recognizable location feels fresh and instantly chaotic.
