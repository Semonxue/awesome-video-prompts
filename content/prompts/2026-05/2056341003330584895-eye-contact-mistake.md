Use the attached BARRY VS THE SEAGULL character sheet as the exact visual reference for Barry and the seagull throughout. Barry is a Pixar 3D man, mid 40s, large soft build, floral beach shirt, khaki shorts, white socks with sandals, sunscreen on nose, sunglasses on forehead. Every character — Barry, seagulls, background figures — full Pixar 3D render. No realistic humans or animals.
THE BEACH: Bright white sand, clear deep blue ocean, vivid blue sky, golden sunlight. One wooden bench facing the ocean. A few beach umbrellas and sunbathers in the far background. Maximum brightness and color throughout. No dark frames.
THE SANDWICH: Plain white bread, visible filling. In Barry’s hands until 0:10 when it flies into the air and disappears forever.
THE SEAGULL: White feathers, yellow beak, small black emotionless eyes. Zero expression. Zero remorse. The one seagull at the start is the exact same one on the bench at the end.
MUSIC: Peaceful gentle acoustic guitar and soft waves at the start. The moment forty seagulls appear at 0:07 music explodes into full chaotic percussion and horns. Cuts to complete silence the instant Barry hits the sand at 0:10.
0:00–0:02 — Medium shot. Barry on the bench facing the ocean. Unwraps his sandwich. Takes one slow happy bite. Completely at peace. Perfect day. SOUND: Gentle waves. Soft acoustic guitar.
0:02–0:04 — One seagull lands on the bench directly beside him. Barry notices. They make full eye contact. Neither moves. Complete silence. SOUND: Music fades. One ambient seagull cry. Wind.
0:04–0:06 — Barry grabs a piece of bread and throws it far down the beach. Both watch it land. Seagull looks at the bread. Looks back at Barry. Does not move. Barry slowly looks up at the sky. Empty blue. He exhales with relief. Takes one bite. SOUND: Silence. Barry chewing. One ominous low note.
0:06–0:07 — Wide shot. The sky turns white. Forty seagulls drop from directly above in a screaming diving formation. SOUND: One beat of silence then music EXPLODES — full chaos, horns, percussion.
0:07–0:10 — Wide tracking shot. Barry sprints full speed down the beach, sandwich held above his head with both arms, mouth open screaming. Seagulls swarming everywhere — diving at the sandwich, one on his head, one on each shoulder. Pure chaos. One sandal flies off mid-sprint. SOUND: Chaos music full volume. Seagulls screaming. Barry screaming.
0:10–0:12 — Barry trips. Falls face first into the sand in slow motion. Sandwich launches upward into the air. Every seagull vanishes instantly. Complete silence. SOUND: Slow motion whoosh. Heavy thud. Music cuts dead.
0:12–0:15 — Wide shot. Barry face down in the sand. Silence. He slowly lifts his head and looks back at the bench. The original seagull is sitting exactly where it started. Perfectly still. Staring at him. Barry puts his face back in the sand. Fade to black. SOUND: Soft acoustic guitar returns. Gentle waves. Fade to silence.
Camera: medium bench shot, medium two-shot Barry and seagull, medium bread throw, wide sky shot for the descent, wide tracking shot during sprint, slow motion fall, wide final bench shot.
