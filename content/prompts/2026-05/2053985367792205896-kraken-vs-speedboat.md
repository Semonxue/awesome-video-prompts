Pure military-creature-action madness. A heavily armed speedboat races through storm waves while a gigantic Kraken hunts them beneath the ocean. Machine guns firing, giant tentacles erupting everywhere, screaming engines, exploding water, and a final bullet-time bazooka shot inches away from death.

Shot 1 (0s-2s) - THE HOOK: Top-down storm shot. A military speedboat races through giant waves at insane speed. Suddenly a gigantic tentacle erupts upward behind the boat and smashes into the water beside it, nearly flipping it over.

Shot 2 (2s-5s): Inside the violently bouncing speedboat. Armed mercenaries fire machine guns wildly into the ocean. Another tentacle wraps around the back of the boat and starts dragging it sideways through the waves.

Shot 3 (5s-8s): The Kraken suddenly rises COMPLETELY out of the ocean behind them. Massive roaring mouth. Tentacles thrashing everywhere. Its sheer size towers over the speedboat while waves crash around it.

Shot 4 (8s-11s): One mercenary grabs a bazooka while another screams: "TAKE THE SHOT!" A tentacle whips toward the boat at insane speed. The speedboat launches off a giant wave directly toward the Kraken.

Shot 5 (11s-15s) - PAYOFF: BULLET TIME. The bazooka rocket spins through the air toward the Kraken while a giant tentacle almost reaches the boat. DIRECT HIT. The Kraken explodes in a massive underwater fireball just inches from grabbing them.

Style: Pure adrenaline - giant monster reveals, machine guns, storm waves, tentacle attacks, and a bullet-time bazooka kill shot all escalating nonstop.
