15s / ONE CONTINUOUS CINEMATIC SHOT / ultra-detailed floating ocean fortress under pirate siege. Bright golden sunlight, violent ocean spray, dense lived-in steampunk world. No dark apocalypse mood - colorful, chaotic, alive.

The camera rushes low across the ocean toward the massive floating fortress-city made from salvaged ships, turbines, pipes, cranes, sails, and stacked mechanical neighborhoods.

Suddenly alarm bells erupt across the city. From the distant horizon, a fleet of insane pirate warships appears. Each vessel is built from scavenged junk and war machines: rusted car frames welded into armor, spinning tractor wheels used as paddle engines, broken airplane wings bolted into sails, giant turbine propellers, hanging chains and scrap metal spikes, patched steel plates and burning engines.

The pirate fleet crashes through massive waves at full speed. The camera dives between incoming ships as explosive harpoons slam into the fortress walls.

Chaos erupts instantly. Floating market bridges collapse. Steam pipes explode. Citizens run across hanging walkways carrying crates and supplies.

Final escalation: A gigantic pirate flagship rises from the mist - half battleship, half mechanical sea monster made of welded industrial wreckage. It opens massive mechanical jaws.
