Hyper-realistic cinematic 15-second continuous video of a beautiful 
muscular Japanese young woman doing her morning Get Ready With Me gym 
routine inside a bright modern Japanese apartment. Follow the storyboard 
sequence strictly from panel 1 to panel 16 in exact order — waking up, 
morning stretch, face wash, skincare moisturizer application, tying hair 
into high ponytail, putting on sports bra, stepping into compression 
leggings, checking outfit in full mirror, drinking pre-workout shake, 
lacing up training sneakers, packing gym bag, inserting earbuds, 
flexing in mirror with a grin, pulling on oversized hoodie, grabbing 
bag and keys at apartment door, stepping outside into bright morning 
sunlight with a confident look back over shoulder. Luminous glowing 
shiny skin throughout. Warm golden morning light, smooth lifestyle 
cinematic transitions between each step. Photorealistic, fitness 
editorial quality, 60fps.

Storyboard Prompt
[VISUAL STYLE]
Hyper-realistic, full-color photographic storyboard.
Clean natural morning light, warm tones, high contrast skin detail.
Editorial fitness magazine quality across all panels.
[GRID]
4×4 layout (16 panels), evenly spaced with thin white dividers.
Panels identical in size, numbered 1–16.
Continuous GRWM flow: wake-up → gym-ready exit.
[CHARACTER]
Japanese woman, mid-20s.
Athletic, muscular, well-defined physique (arms, shoulders, back, legs).
Consistent face, proportions, and skin tone across all panels (no drift).
Skin: luminous, moisturized, radiant throughout.
Expression arc: Groggy → Energized → Confident → Ready.
[ENVIRONMENT]
Modern minimal Japanese apartment (consistent across panels):
Bright bedroom + bathroom, wooden floors, white walls.
Vanity with mirror + ring light, gym bag on bed.
Plants + motivational posters.
Soft sunlight through sheer curtains.
[PANEL FORMAT]
Top-left: Step # + EN/JP label
Center: full/mid-body action shot
Bottom-left: 3–4 short description lines
Mood tag: 1-word energy label
[SEQUENCE — 16 PANELS]
Wake Up / 目覚め — alarm, sitting up, messy hair, oversized shirt
Stretch / 朝ストレッチ — full overhead stretch, body visible
Face Wash / 洗顔 — splashing water, radiant skin
Skincare / スキンケア — applying moisturizer, dewy glow
Hair Tie / ヘアセット — high ponytail, strong shoulders
Sports Bra / スポブラ — putting on, back muscles visible
Leggings / レギンス — stepping in, compression fit
Mirror Check / 鏡チェック — outfit check, confident smirk
Pre-workout / プレワークアウト — shaking bottle, energized
Shoes / シューズ — lacing sneakers, seated
Gym Bag / バッグ準備 — packing essentials
Earbuds / イヤホン — inserting, focused
Pump Check / ポンプ — light flex, muscle definition
Jacket / アウター — hoodie mid-wear
Ready / 出発準備 — bag + keys at door
Exit / 出発 — stepping out, looking back, confident
[SKIN PROGRESSION]
1–2: soft natural matte
3–5: fresh post-wash glow
6–10: peak dewy luminosity
11–16: fully radiant, moisturized shine
[LIGHTING]
Warm sunlight (primary)
Bathroom: cool white
Mirror panels: reflected lighting
Final panel: strong natural sunlight, high contrast
[RENDER]
Ultra-detailed photorealism.
Sharp focus: skin texture, fabric compression, hair strands, environment.
Consistent warm color grade.
[RESTRICTIONS]
No anime/cartoon.
Accurate Japanese text.
Tasteful, fitness-focused framing.
Consistent character identity.
Focus: GRWM transformation → gym readiness.
