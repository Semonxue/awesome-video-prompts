Create an ultra-realistic cinematic video scene set in a huge open countryside football ground during cloudy weather. The ground is extremely wide, surrounded by green grass fields, broken fences, scattered glass pieces on one side of the field, and distant mountains visible far away. The atmosphere feels emotional, dramatic, and realistic like a movie scene. Dark storm clouds slowly move across the sky while strong wind blows through the field, moving the grass naturally.

A large group of children around 10 to 14 years old are playing an intense football match in the middle of the ground. The children are running fast, shouting, laughing, and passing the football with realistic movement and expressions. Some children are wearing colorful football jerseys while others are wearing simple village-style clothes. Their clothes become slightly dirty from playing on the rough ground.

Around the football field, several goats are peacefully grazing grass and moving naturally. Some goats are sitting while others walk slowly across the edge of the field. In the background, old electric poles, small village houses, trees moving in the wind, and broken glass scattered near one corner of the ground can be seen, creating a realistic rural environment.

As the football game becomes more intense, one child kicks the football very hard toward another player. Two boys run quickly toward the ball at the same time and accidentally collide with each other at high speed. Both children fall dramatically onto the ground in slow motion. Dust, mud, and grass fly into the air realistically. One child slides across the grass while the football rolls away slowly. Other children immediately stop playing and rush toward them with worried expressions.

The camera shows multiple cinematic angles:

* Wide aerial drone shot of the giant football ground and cloudy sky.
