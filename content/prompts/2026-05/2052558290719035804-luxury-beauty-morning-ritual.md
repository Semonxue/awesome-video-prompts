Style: Luxury beauty cinematic, golden-hour sunlight, soft warm tones, ultra-detailed close-ups, glowing skin textures, dreamy slow motion, elegant feminine atmosphere, premium lifestyle aesthetic.

0–3s — Sunlight Awakening
Golden sunlight pours gently through sheer curtains into a minimal luxury bedroom. A girl sits calmly at a vanity mirror wearing a silk robe. Floating dust particles shimmer in the warm light while soft ambient music begins.

3–6s — Beauty Details
Extreme close-up shots: makeup brushes gliding across radiant skin, foundation blending seamlessly, glossy lips reflecting sunlight. Slow-motion hair movement and delicate hand gestures create a smooth, elegant rhythm.

6–9s — Luxury Atmosphere
Cinematic beauty-detail cuts: perfume bottle reflections, sparkling jewelry, makeup palette opening slowly, sunlight flaring across the mirror. Camera movements remain soft and fluid with dreamy shallow depth of field.

9–12s — Confidence Glow
The final makeup look comes together naturally—soft glam, glowing skin, effortless confidence. She turns slightly toward the window as warm sunlight highlights every facial feature with a golden cinematic glow.

12–15s — Cinematic Finish
Slow-motion final shot: she stands beside the window holding a coffee cup, silhouette illuminated by golden light. Curtains move softly in a calm breeze as the camera slowly pulls back into a warm glowing fade.
