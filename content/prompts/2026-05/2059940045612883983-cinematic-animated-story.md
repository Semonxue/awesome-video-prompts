A small boy around 6 years old is playing football on a clean paved path beside a neat neighborhood park. The setting must feel bright, clean, safe, and tidy.

Nearby is a group of 4 teenage boys with a classic animated tough-kid vibe. They wear varsity jackets, hoodies, jeans, sneakers, and wristbands. One is taller and looks like the leader. Another has folded arms, one has messy hair, and one carries a skateboard. They look intimidating at first with stern expressions, confident body language, and slow approach, but they are not evil. Their behavior turns kind and protective.

Wide side-tracking shot. The little boy happily plays football alone on the clean park path, lightly dribbling and kicking the ball. Camera moves closer to the boy on the ground and the ball near the teen gang.
