Scene Style: Rustic fire-lit vibe, golden baked bread crusts, glistening fat, deep savory browns.

Sequence:

0–2s: Smartphone screen showing a menu of grilled items. Finger taps "Spicy Hawawshi." Satisfying click.

2–5s: Quick cuts montage ,spiced minced meat pressing into baladi bread, brushing the exterior with melted ghee, sizzling and bubbling in a blazing hot oven.

5–8s: Delivery moment , grease-spotted paper bag handed over, logo visible, heavy steam rising.

8–11s: Hero food shots ,  hands tearing the crispy bread in half, meat juices and melted cheese dripping, dipping a corner into thick tahini.

11–13s: First bite reaction ,loud crunch sound, person closes their eyes, enjoying the rich, smoky flavor.

13–15s: Final shot ,wooden board piled high with Hawawshi halves and pickles.

Text overlay: "Crunch Into the Crave." Fade out with a heavy drum beat.
