Create a premium 15 second vertical 9:16 motion graphics video using the uploaded Alfa Romeo concept supercar blueprint board as the main reference.

Turn the flat design board into a layered pseudo-3D automotive presentation. Separate the top sketches, central blue supercar render, bottom technical blueprints, dimension lines, suspension diagrams, wireframe studies, and callout panels into floating depth layers. Preserve the original aged paper texture, blue ink blueprint style, technical layout, and deep metallic blue car. Do not redesign the car, change the proportions, distort the wheels, grille, body shape, or add new logos/text.

0–3s:
Start with the full board front-facing. Slow cinematic push-in. The flat page begins to separate into multiple floating 3D layers. Blue construction lines glow softly and draw themselves like invisible technical ink.

3–6s:
Top sketch panels lift forward with parallax depth. Side-profile sketches, rear sketch, wheel guide circles, and aerodynamic arrows animate gently. The camera begins a smooth orbit around the floating blueprint layers. Subtle shadows create real 3D depth.

6–10s:
The central metallic blue supercar becomes the hero object and lifts slightly forward from the board. Perform a controlled 360-style cinematic orbit around the car and surrounding blueprint panels. Reflections sweep across the hood, windshield, side panels, roofline, and wheels. Headlights softly pulse once. Keep the car sharp, stable, realistic, and undistorted.

10–13s:
Move into the lower engineering section. Suspension diagrams, side elevation, front view, rear view, wireframe study, top view, underbody aero panels, and dimension lines float in 3D space. Blueprint lines extend smoothly, callouts appear with clean technical motion, panels rotate gently like museum display objects.

13–15s:
Final hero pullback. Show the full floating 3D automotive design composition with the blue supercar in front, surrounded by elegant blueprint panels and technical sketches. All motion settles into a polished premium concept-car presentation frame.
