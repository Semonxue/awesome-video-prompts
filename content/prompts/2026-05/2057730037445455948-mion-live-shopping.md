Create a 15-second 80s anime video.

Use @[char ref]  as the character reference.
Use @[storyboard ref] as the storyboard reference.

Follow the storyboard as sequential keyframes, not as one collage. Preserve panel order, shot rhythm, framing progression, product reveals, camera energy and comedic sales pacing. Do not show storyboard borders, panel lines, labels, notes, watermarks or text artifacts in the final video.

@[char ref]  is Mion Kasuga, a fast-paced speed live shopping host doing a smartphone livestream.

The video must feel like a real phone livestream, not a cinematic commercial.
Use a front-facing phone camera feel with slight handheld movement, casual framing, fast host energy and direct eye contact with the audience.

Show a retro-inspired livestream interface on screen:
LIVE badge,
viewer count,
floating hearts,
fast comment flow,
shopping bag icon,
pinned product cards,
limited-time deal popups,
quick price highlights.
Do not use any real app logos or brand names.

Setting:
A cozy 1980s city-pop shopping studio corner, retro boutique booth or colorful TV shopping set adapted for a phone livestream. The background should feel stylish, nostalgic and attractive but secondary to the host and products.

Action:
Mion rapidly presents cute, stylish and useful retro lifestyle products:
hair clips,
earrings,
necklaces,
watches,
mini makeup items,
compact perfume,
cute notebooks,
cassette-themed accessories,
phone charms,
fashion belts,
scarves,
small bags,
beauty tools,
retro gadgets,
limited-edition boutique items.

She talks very fast, reacts to comments, grabs new items quickly, lifts them close to the phone camera, rotates them, demonstrates them and points at pinned deals like a professional live selling host. She occasionally leans close to the camera, smiles, winks, gestures excitedly with her analog cue wand and sells with playful urgency.

Performance:
Quick-talking, cheerful, persuasive, clever, socially bright, expressive and warm.
She should feel like a charming 1980s anime live commerce influencer.
Her mouth movement should match fast speaking.
She reacts as if viewers are asking questions in real time.

Camera and pacing:
Mostly front phone camera perspective.
Occasional quick cuts to tighter product close-ups are okay, but always keep the overall feeling of a mobile livestream.
Fast pacing, quick product swaps, lively expressions, playful hand gestures and strong host presence.

Visual style:
1980s hand-painted cel anime look, clean ink linework, flat painted colors, classic cel shading, soft analog texture, subtle film grain, retro city-pop color palette, expressive anime face animation, nostalgic Japanese TV shopping energy.

Important:
Preserve the character's identity.
Do not redesign her face, hairstyle or outfit.
Make it clearly feel like a smartphone live broadcast.
No watermark.
No real platform branding.
No modern influencer fashion redesign.
