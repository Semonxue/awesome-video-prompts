  Massive ancient library at night. Towering bookshelves stretch infinitely upward. Moonlight pours through tall gothic windows, casting long beams across floating dust particles. The environment feels vast, quiet, and mysterious. Camera slowly pushes inward.

  SHOT 2 (1.00–2.00s) – Close-up, eye-level, static:
  Old wooden bookshelf filled with dusty books. Tiny glowing particles drift in the air. Subtle shimmer suggests hidden magic. Camera holds still for atmosphere.

  SHOT 3 (2.00–3.00s) – Medium close-up, eye-level, slight push-in:
  Young student holding a lantern. Soft warm light illuminates their face. Expression: cautious curiosity. Eyes scanning surroundings. Slight nervous breath.

  SHOT 4 (3.00–4.00s) – Medium shot, low-key lighting, slow pan:
  Mysterious elderly librarian stands in shadow between shelves. Only part of face visible. Sharp eyes watching silently. Camera pans slightly as figure emerges subtly.

  SHOT 5 (4.00–5.00s) – Close-up, eye-level, dynamic:
  Student pulls a glowing book from the shelf. As it slides out, golden light leaks from its pages. Fingers hesitate, then commit.

  SHOT 6 (5.00–6.00s) – Extreme close-up on book, dynamic motion:
  Book bursts open. Pages flip rapidly on their own. Bright magical light pulses outward. Energy hum builds. Camera shakes slightly from force.

  SHOT 7 (6.00–7.00s) – Wide shot, slight tilt, dynamic:
  Library begins shifting. Bookshelves subtly slide and rotate. Space distorts. Floor lines warp. Camera tilts slightly to emphasize disorientation.

  SHOT 8 (7.00–8.00s) – Medium shot, orbital camera move:
  Symbols and glowing glyphs begin floating around the student. Lantern flickers. Student looks around in awe as energy surrounds them.

  SHOT 9 (8.00–9.00s) – Wide shot, orbiting camera:
  Dozens of books lift into the air, orbiting in a circular motion around the student. Pages flutter. Motion accelerates smoothly.

  SHOT 10 (9.00–10.00s) – Close-up insert, static:
  Magical diagrams and symbols on pages. Intricate glowing geometry, shifting runes, and layered patterns. Light pulses rhythmically.

  SHOT 11 (10.00–11.00s) – Medium shot, eye-level, slight push-in:
  Student raises hand cautiously, experimenting. Floating books respond—slowing, aligning slightly. Expression shifts to focused determination.

  SHOT 12 (11.00–12.00s) – Wide chaotic shot, fast motion:
  Suddenly, control breaks. Books spiral wildly in all directions. Energy surges violently. Camera shakes. Student looks overwhelmed.

  SHOT 13 (12.00–13.00s) – Medium shot, low angle, slow motion:
  Librarian steps forward calmly, raising one hand. Motion slows dramatically. Books begin stabilizing mid-air. Calm authority radiates.

  SHOT 14 (13.00–14.00s) – Wide shot, centered composition, slow stabilization:
  Everything aligns. Books gently settle into orbit then return to shelves. Energy fades. Student stands still, now composed and confident.

  FINAL MOMENT (end) – Close-up, soft focus:
  Student’s eyes glow faintly with magical energy. In the blurred background, the librarian smiles knowingly. Subtle hint: this is only the beginning.
