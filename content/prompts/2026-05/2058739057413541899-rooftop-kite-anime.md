RIKU & KAITO

"The Rooftop Kite" (Friendship / Childhood Memory)

SUBJECTS
17-year-old boy named Riku, energetic personality, messy hair, oversized hoodie.
17-year-old boy named Kaito, quieter personality, black jacket, calm expression.

ENVIRONMENT
Old apartment rooftop during windy sunset overlooking a warm city skyline. Broken chairs, clotheslines, drifting birds, and colorful rooftops nearby.

MUSIC
Warm acoustic guitar mixed with nostalgic piano and soft cinematic ambience.

STYLE
Digital painting animation with simplified painterly forms, warm sunset shadows, soft hand-painted textures, emotional atmosphere.

SHOT 1
Hard cut opening. Wide shot, 35mm. Riku excitedly runs across the rooftop carrying a damaged kite while Kaito quietly watches. SFX: rooftop wind, distant city ambience.

SHOT 2
Continuous shot. Medium shot, 50mm. Both boys kneel together trying to repair the broken kite using tape and old paper. SFX: paper folding, quiet laughter.

SHOT 3
Cut. Wide cinematic shot, 28mm. They run together as the repaired kite finally lifts high into the sunset sky. SFX: wind intensifying, emotional acoustic swell.

SHOT 4
Continuous push-in. Medium close-up, 85mm. Riku laughs loudly while Kaito smiles quietly for the first time. SFX: distant birds, warm piano rise.

SHOT 5
Ending shot. Extreme wide shot, 24mm. The kite floats above the glowing skyline while both boys sit silently beside each other watching sunset. SFX: fading guitar melody, soft evening ambience.
