Prompt Part 1:
Shot 1: Opening Speed | 0–5s

Extreme low-angle highway shot.
A massive dark cargo truck loaded with illegal weapons tears down the road at dangerous speed.
Camera shakes slightly from the force as the truck blasts past.
Quick interior glimpse of the driver gripping steering wheel tightly, sweat on face.

Audio: Heavy diesel engine roar, turbo hiss, cinematic bass pulse.

Shot 2: Illegal Cargo Reveal | 5–9s

Fast interior cuts inside truck container.
Metal crates slide during sharp turns.
Open duffle bags reveal illegal rifles, pistols, ammo boxes.
Red emergency light flickers across weapons.

Driver checks side mirror nervously.

Audio: Metallic rattling, deep bass hit, radio static.

Shot 3: Police Detection | 9–15s

Rear aerial shot.
Three white police SUVs begin aggressive pursuit with flashing red-blue lights.
Police helicopter appears above highway and locks spotlight onto truck.

Police vehicles rapidly close distance from both lanes.

Police Radio:
“Target confirmed. Do not let the truck escape.”

Audio: Loud sirens, helicopter blades, rising tension music.

Prompt part 2:
Shot 4: Full Chase | 15–20s

Dynamic side tracking shot.
Police SUVs surround truck from left and right.
Truck swerves aggressively trying to escape.
One police SUV nearly gets pushed off-road.

Police Loudspeaker:
“STOP THE VEHICLE NOW!”

Audio: Tire screeches, sirens, engine strain.

Shot 5: Roadblock Impact | 20–25s

Front highway view.
A white police Bolero blocks the road sideways ahead.
Truck driver refuses to brake.
Truck smashes directly into Bolero.
Huge collision. Glass, sparks, smoke everywhere.
Truck spins slightly and stops hard.

Audio: Violent crash impact, twisting metal, alarms.

Shot 6: Final Action | 25–30s

Driver jumps from damaged truck and starts running across road.
Police officers move forward aiming weapons.
Helicopter spotlight follows him precisely.

A single controlled sniper shot fires from helicopter.
Driver gets hit and collapses instantly.

Police slowly approach while helicopter hovers above burning crash scene.

Audio: Sharp gunshot, helicopter blades, fading sirens.
