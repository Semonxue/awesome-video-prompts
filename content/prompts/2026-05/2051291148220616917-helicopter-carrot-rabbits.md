A vast, cinematic forest landscape during bright daylight, filled with enormous, towering trees that stretch high into the sky. The trees are dense and lush, forming a rich green canopy that can be seen from far away. Sunlight streams through the leaves, creating soft rays and natural shadows across the environment, giving the entire scene a vibrant and realistic feel.
In the middle of this massive forest, there is a wide open grassy field. The grass is fresh, slightly swaying with the breeze, and the space feels calm and untouched. In this field, a large group of more than two dozen rabbits is scattered around. The rabbits are small, fluffy, and highly detailed, moving naturally as they hop around and search for food. Some are sniffing the ground, others are gently digging or nibbling on grass, creating a peaceful wildlife moment.
Suddenly, a helicopter appears high in the sky. It slowly descends while hovering above the field, its blades spinning rapidly and creating a soft wind effect that ripples through the grass and nearby trees. The helicopter stands out against the clear blue sky.
From above, the helicopter begins dropping carrots down into the field. The carrots fall from the sky in multiple directions, landing across the grassy area. Some bounce slightly upon impact. The rabbits quickly notice and rush toward the falling carrots. They grab them eagerly, holding them with their front paws and happily eating. Their movements become more energetic and playful.
Among the rabbits, two or three stand upright and perform a humorous, human-like salute toward the helicopter, similar to a soldier's salute. This adds a light comedic and charming touch to the scene.
Nearby, on tree branches at the edge of the forest, a group of monkeys watches everything. They become excited and start gesturing toward the helicopter, waving their arms as if asking for food to be thrown in their direction as well.
In the far background of the field, large animals such as buffaloes and deer are peacefully grazing. They are spread out across the landscape, adding depth and realism to the scene. The entire environment feels alive, balanced, and full of natural wildlife activity.
The scene should be highly detailed, cinematic, and realistic, with smooth camera movements, wide-angle shots, soft natural lighting, and rich textures. The overall mood is peaceful, lively, slightly playful, and visually immersive.
