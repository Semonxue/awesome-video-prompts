  Six children (around 8–10 years old, diverse appearances) are sitting on chairs around the table, forming a circle.   The original children are still surprised, exchanging confused looks with each other, then slowly turning back to their identical versions.

  The glowing egg continues to pulse softly in the background, as if it has created something magical and alive.

A large, softly lit futuristic room with minimal design. In the center of the room, there is a big round table. On top of the table sits a large, egg-shaped computer device smooth, metallic, and slightly transparent. The egg is glowing from inside, emitting soft, radiant beams of light that pulse gently, creating a magical atmosphere in the room.

Six children (around 8–10 years old, diverse appearances) are sitting on chairs around the table, forming a circle. Their faces are filled with curiosity and excitement as they interact with the glowing egg. Some tap it lightly, others watch closely as light patterns move inside it. The room is quiet except for a soft humming sound coming from the egg.

Suddenly, the glowing intensifies. The light inside the egg becomes brighter and starts forming shapes. The children lean back slightly, confused and amazed.

From inside the glowing egg, six figures slowly begin to emerge — one by one. These figures step out of the light… and they are exact lookalikes of each child sitting around the table. Same faces, same expressions — but wearing different clothes.

The real children freeze in shock. Their eyes widen. They look at their identical versions sitting right in front of them.

The copies or duplicates look at the original children… and suddenly start smiling brightly. They seem happy, excited, and friendly. Then they begin to laugh joyfully, as if meeting their best friends.

The original children are still surprised, exchanging confused looks with each other, then slowly turning back to their identical versions.

The glowing egg continues to pulse softly in the background, as if it has created something magical and alive.

Final shot:
Wide angle showing all twelve children — six originals and six identical copies — sitting face to face around the glowing egg. The room is filled with soft light and laughter.

Style: cinematic, soft lighting, magical realism, high detail, emotional expressions, smooth camera movement
