A cinematic horror-fantasy sequence set in a forgotten amusement park after midnight.

0–3s: Wide shot of an abandoned theme park under a full moon. Rusted rides stand still in silence. Wind moves through broken ticket booths while distant carnival music crackles faintly from nowhere.

3–6s: Flickering lights suddenly turn on across the park one by one. The Ferris wheel begins to rotate slowly. Broken speakers emit distorted laughter as fog rolls across the ground.

6–9s: Close-up shots of animatronic characters inside shattered ride cabins. Their eyes light up red. Joints twitch as they begin moving again, slowly at first, then more synchronized.

9–12s: The entire park "awakens." Roller coasters start moving on their own, bumper cars drift without drivers, and animatronics step down from broken stages into the fog-covered walkways.

12–15s: Final cinematic wide shot. The abandoned park is now fully alive and glowing under eerie lights. Hundreds of animatronics stand motionless, all turning their heads toward the camera at once as the music abruptly cuts to silence.

Style: ultra cinematic horror, eerie neon lighting, fog-heavy atmosphere, realistic animatronic motion, slow tension buildup, jump-scare framing, film-grade color grading, suspenseful sound design feel, analog glitch aesthetic.
