Create a cinematic 15-second post-apocalyptic desert action sequence featuring Brax.
Use:
@BraxRef  as character reference
@braxstory2  as storyboard
STYLE:
stylized low-poly painterly
hand-painted 3D concept art
faceted polygonal illustration
modern mobile-game splash art aesthetic
painterly cinematic character rendering
strong silhouette readability
cinematic anime action
heavy environmental atmosphere
minimal but impactful detail
warm desert lighting
heat haze distortion
high visual clarity
IMPORTANT:
Brax is NOT a giant.
He is:
extremely athletic
explosively powerful
calm under pressure
heavy but agile.
His movement style should feel:
efficient
grounded
violent
unexpectedly fast.
Think: heavyweight martial artist with superhuman acceleration.
NOT flashy anime teleportation.
Every movement should still carry:
weight
momentum
impact.
ENVIRONMENT:
minimal desert setting
mostly blue sky
rock formations
floating dust
sand movement
heat distortion
small debris reacting to impacts
no large buildings
no crowded background
CAMERA LANGUAGE:
extreme low angles
fast cinematic cuts during action
slow moments before impact
dynamic handheld movement
motion blur on bursts of speed
strong scale and momentum framing
0:00 - 0:03
Raiders on scrap bikes aggressively chase a desert caravan.
Sand sprays everywhere.
Gunfire erupts.
Fast cinematic cuts.
Suddenly:
Brax stands alone in the middle of the path.
Wind blows through the straps on his shorts.
The raiders laugh and accelerate directly toward him.
0:03 - 0:05
Extreme close-up:
Brax calmly watching.
The lead raider charges at full speed.
At the very last second—
Brax instantly sidesteps.
Fast.
Clean.
Unexpected.
The bike barely misses him.
Camera whip-pan follows the movement.
0:05 - 0:08
Brax grabs the rider mid-motion.
Uses the momentum to violently slam him into the ground.
Massive sand explosion.
No wasted movement.
Brax immediately pivots into another attack.
Dust trails behind his movement.
0:08 - 0:11
Two raiders attack together.
Brax explodes forward with shocking speed.
Brutal grounded choreography:
elbow strike
dodge
spinning backfist
low sweep kick
Heavy impacts.
Bodies crash into sand.
Bike flips through the air.
Floating debris reacts to the force.
0:11 - 0:13
One raider tries escaping.
Brax sprints.
Leaps onto a rock.
Then launches himself downward like a missile.
Devastating impact destroys the bike.
Shockwave blasts sand outward.
Brief sound cutoff from impact force.
0:13 - 0:15
Silence.
Dust slowly settles.
Destroyed bikes scattered around the desert.
Brax stands calmly, breathing heavily.
The caravan stares at him in disbelief.
Close-up:
Brax tightening the wraps around his fists.
Then casually walking away into the heat haze beneath the endless blue sky.
