Prompt Part 1:

SHOT 1 — Midnight Run | 0–5s
Cinematic low-angle highway scene at night.
A giant armored cargo truck races through the empty road at extreme speed, headlights cutting through heavy fog. The camera vibrates from the power of the engine as sparks fly from the wheels during sharp turns.
Inside the cabin, the stressed driver glances around while gripping the wheel tightly.

Audio: Aggressive diesel engine, turbo pressure, dark cinematic bass.

SHOT 2 — Smuggled Cargo | 5–9s
Fast cuts inside the moving trailer.
Wooden and metal crates crash into each other as the truck swerves. Open cases reveal illegal firearms, ammunition, stacks of cash, and hidden contraband glowing under flickering emergency lights.
The driver quickly checks the mirror after hearing distant sirens.

Audio: Metal impacts, radio interference, deep suspense sound.

SHOT 3 — Pursuit Activated | 9–15s
Wide aerial drone shot above the highway.
Multiple police interceptors enter the chase with flashing red and blue lights.
A helicopter appears through the clouds and tracks the truck using a powerful spotlight.
Police vehicles rapidly surround the truck from different sides.

Police Radio:
"Suspect vehicle identified. Intercept immediately."

Audio: Sirens echoing, helicopter rotors, rising tension music.

Prompt Part 2:

SHOT 4 — High-Speed Chase | 15–20s
Side cinematic tracking shot.
The truck aggressively pushes through traffic while police SUVs attempt to trap it between lanes.
One interceptor nearly crashes after the truck swerves dangerously toward it.

Police Loudspeaker:
"Pull over now!"

Audio: Tire screeches, sirens, roaring engines.

SHOT 5 — Final Roadblock | 20–25s
Front highway cinematic shot.
A heavy police barricade blocks the road ahead with armed officers nearby.
Instead of stopping, the truck accelerates and smashes directly into the barricade.
Massive explosion of sparks, debris, shattered glass, smoke, and twisted metal fills the scene as the truck spins sideways.

Audio: Explosive crash, alarms, grinding metal.

SHOT 6 — Last Attempt Escape | 25–30s
The driver jumps from the wreckage and sprints across the dark highway.
Police officers move in tactically while the helicopter spotlight follows every movement.
A precise sniper shot echoes through the night.
The fugitive collapses instantly as police surround the burning crash site.

Final wide cinematic shot:
Flashing lights illuminate the destroyed highway while smoke rises into the night sky.
