Use the provided previs storyboard @[image] as the main visual and motion reference.

Create a 15-second cinematic horror sequence.

Read the storyboard panels as sequential shots, not as one image. Follow the panel order, camera logic, blocking, motion arrows and framing. Do not show storyboard panels, numbers, labels, text, red boxes or blue arrows in the final video. 

The woman begins seated on the dirty floor in a white dress with her face hidden by long dark hair. The commuter approaches from the platform distance. The transformation and fight must feel continuous, physical and cause-and-effect driven.

STYLE: Live-action cinematic horror, gritty subway realism, body-horror action, cold overhead fluorescent lighting, dirty tiled platform, deep shadows, sparks, handheld chaos during the fight.
ENVIRONMENT: A desolate, dimly lit underground transit station platform. Features dirty tiled walls, concrete floors, and flickering overhead fluorescent tubes.
MOOD: Claustrophobic dread escalating into high-speed, kinetic monstrosity and chaotic violence.
COLOR LOGIC: Cold Isolation Look
