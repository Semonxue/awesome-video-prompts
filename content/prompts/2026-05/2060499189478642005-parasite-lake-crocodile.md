Nightmare jungle island. Black water lake surrounded by twisted mangrove roots, giant ferns, strange glowing plants, thick fog hanging over the water. The island feels biologically wrong. Hyper-real creature-feature action. Chaotic handheld cinematography, aggressive speed ramps, crash zooms, rapid cut-tos. Sound: splashing water, crocodile roars, parasite screeches, frantic breathing. Multiple shots. Like a movie scene.

0–2s — HOOK

Handheld chase shot. A survivor is already sprinting along the muddy shoreline. Behind him — A gigantic crocodile bursts through shallow water. Forty feet long. Ancient scars. Massive jaws snapping shut inches behind him. Mud and water explode everywhere.

2–5s — ESCALATION

The survivor reaches the lake edge. No options left. The crocodile lunges. At the last second — He dives into the water. Cut underwater. The crocodile crashes into the lake behind him. A massive wall of bubbles and mud fills the frame.

5–8s — FALSE SAFETY

Underwater tracking shot. The survivor swims deeper. Looks behind. The crocodile is still searching. Then something changes. Small glowing worm-like creatures emerge from the darkness. Hundreds of them with large jagged teeth. Moving unnaturally fast.

8–11s — REVEAL

The parasites attach themselves to the crocodile. One. Ten. Fifty. Hundreds. The crocodile thrashes violently. Its body starts mutating almost immediately. Extra limbs push through its sides. Additional jaws emerge from its neck. New eyes open beneath its scales. The water turns chaotic.

11–13s — CHAOS

The survivor surfaces and pulls himself onto shore. Gasping for air. Relieved. Then the lake erupts. The mutated crocodile explodes upward. Twice as large. Covered in glowing parasites. Multiple jaws opening independently.

13–15s — SHOCKING PAYOFF

The creature roars. Then one of the extra jaws tears open. Inside — Another fully formed crocodile head begins pushing outward. Growing from inside the first. The survivor stares in disbelief. The second head opens its eyes. Hard cut to black.
