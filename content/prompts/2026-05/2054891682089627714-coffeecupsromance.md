A cozy aesthetic cafe in soft morning light. Two animated coffee cups sit on different tables every day. One cup is warm brown with cute steam shapes, the other is creamy white with soft pastel details. As the cafe fills with gentle background music, their steam subtly forms tiny hearts whenever they're near each other, hinting at a silent connection. Every morning, the cups are placed in different spots, always close but never together, as if fate keeps separating them. The camera follows their "feelings" through soft zoom-ins on the steam and warm lighting reflections. Days pass in a calming time-lapse. One rainy morning, the cafe rearranges its seating. Finally, both cups are placed side by side on the same table. Their steam rises together, forming one big glowing heart. The scene ends with a cozy close-up as the cafe lights softly blur in the background.
