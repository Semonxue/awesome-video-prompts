Create a 15-second ultra-realistic luxury beauty influencer vlog (16:9) featuring a young woman using Gisou haircare + lip oil.

Style: iPhone influencer GRWM + luxury beauty commercial

Look: warm natural daylight, soft handheld camera, glossy hair, realistic skin, shallow depth of field

NO UI overlays, NO floating icons, NO magic effects

CHARACTER

24–26, fair glowing skin, hazel-green eyes, long dark brown wavy hair with caramel highlights, soft pink lips, cream sleeveless top, minimal gold jewelry.

CUT SCENES

01 (0–2s) — INTRO TAP

Selfie shot. She gestures and taps in air (selection gesture).

Dialogue: "My Gisou routine today."

02 (2–4s) — HAIR MASK

She taps again → mask appears in hand.

Shows product to camera.

Dialogue: "This mask is my favorite."

03 (4–6s) — TEXTURE CLOSE-UP

Opens jar, dips fingers, rubs product between thumb & fingers to show creamy texture.

Dialogue: "Look at this texture."

04 (6–8s) — APPLY + COMB

Applies mask with both hands, then uses wide-tooth comb through hair.

Hair looks coated, smooth, shiny.

Dialogue: "It melts into my hair."

05 (8–10s) — HAIR OIL

She taps again → oil appears. Drops golden oil, smooths into ends, soft hair flip.

Dialogue: "Perfect salon shine."

06 (10–13s) — LIP OIL

She taps again → lip oil appears. Applies slowly, glossy lips close-up.

Dialogue: "My everyday glow essential."

07 (13–15s) — FINAL

Selfie wrap-up, hair glossy and finished.

Dialogue: "My hair has never looked better. Bye 💛"

RULES

Tap gesture = product selection (no UI shown)

Realistic product appears after each tap

No magic effects

No extra hands

Close-up hair + product realism required
