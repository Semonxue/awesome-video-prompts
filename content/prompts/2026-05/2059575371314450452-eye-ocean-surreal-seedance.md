Extreme macro shot of a human eye under soft natural light. Tiny reflections visible across the iris surface.

At the 2-second mark, the pupil begins expanding unnaturally.

Inside the eye, an entire ocean becomes visible.

Massive waves crash beneath floating clouds contained entirely within the iris.

The camera dives into the pupil and enters the ocean world.

The environment feels physically impossible:

— giant whales swim through clouds
— rain falls upward
— moonlight refracts beneath the water surface
— colossal waves freeze mid-motion before collapsing again

Human eyelashes become giant black structures on the horizon.

Velocity ramp: a wave crashes toward the camera, freezing into suspended crystal water droplets reflecting the original human face.

Final moment: the camera exits the eye again — now the entire room around the person is flooded silently with ocean water.
