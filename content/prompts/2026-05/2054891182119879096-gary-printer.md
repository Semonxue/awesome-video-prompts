Gary is a Pixar 3D man, early 40s, light brown messed hair, plain blue office shirt top button undone sleeves rolled up, reading glasses pushed up on forehead, dark trousers. Bright clean office throughout. NO dark frames.
THE OFFICE: White walls, bright fluorescent lighting, single desk with keyboard, monitor showing a document, coffee mug. Standard grey and white office laser printer on a low cabinet beside the desk. Plain grey carpet. No other people.
THE PRINTER: Grey and white rectangular office laser printer. Display screen on front always facing camera — shows PAPER JAM throughout, changes to READY TO PRINT at 0:12. Has four openable doors — front access door hinges downward, left side door swings left, right panel slides right, back panel opens outward, plus front paper input tray. Green cylindrical drum visible inside when open. Printer never moves. No paper visible inside at any point — the jam does not exist visibly. That is the joke.
THE TORN PAPER: At 0:09 Gary pulls one single tiny torn corner of white paper — 2 centimetres by 2 centimetres, torn on all edges — from deep inside the printer. The only paper in the entire video. No full sheets anywhere.
