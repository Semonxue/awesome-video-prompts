15-second Mirinda Blueberry commercial.

Young Southeast Asian woman, purple glam makeup, double space buns, oversized purple fluffy fur coat.

Emotional story arc:

Scene 1 - she sits upset, arms crossed, dim cool lighting.

Scene 2 - she spots a Mirinda can, eyes widen, grabs it in slow motion.

Scene 3 - extreme close-up slow-mo sip, eyes close, warm light washes over her face.

Scene 4 - she relaxes, blissful smile, sparkles bloom around her.

Scene 5 - she suddenly jumps up dancing energetically, spins, holds can in air, confetti explodes, freeze frame winking at camera with Mirinda logo.

4K, cinematic, K-pop meets luxury beverage ad energy, deep purple studio backdrop.
