Cinematic dark fantasy action A winged figure stands motionless in a storm-ravaged landscape. Suddenly — the ground CRACKS beneath them. Fissures spread outward in all directions. The figure's wings EXPLODE open — violent, powerful, feathers scattering like shrapnel. Camera WHIPS around the figure in a fast 360° orbit (3 seconds). Lightning doesn't just flash — it STRIKES the figure directly. Multiple bolts. Rapid succession. The figure's body convulses with power. Eyes SNAP open, glowing white-blue. They LAUNCH upward — wings fully extended, ascending into the storm. Camera follows in a rapid upward push, handheld shake, motion blur. The figure reaches the peak of their ascent and STOPS — suspended in mid-air. They look DOWN at the camera. One moment of absolute stillness. Then — EXPLOSION of light. White-out. Screen goes black. Color: Deep blue-gray → electric white-blue → black void. Mood: Violent, powerful, apocalyptic, unstoppable force. Texture: Feathers in motion, ground deformation, energy discharge, real physics. Pacing: Slow build (0-3s) → rapid action (3-12s) → power moment (12-15s). No dialogue. Only environmental SFX and energy sounds.
