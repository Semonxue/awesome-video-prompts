14-second 3D Pixar-style animated comedy short, ultra-detailed, vibrant colors, cinematic lighting, smooth 24fps animation, exaggerated expressions and physics, emotional and funny tone.

SHOT 1 (0.00–1.00s) – Wide establishing shot, eye-level, slow push-in: Elegant luxury restaurant interior with chandeliers and oil paintings. Tall male chef (dark beard, white uniform, tall hat) stands beside a table, smiling proudly while lifting a silver cloche to serve an elderly lady seated at a white-tablecloth table. The tiny elderly lady (fluffy light-blue bun, huge gold glasses, pearl jewelry, white cardigan) looks up at him with a knowing smirk. Other diners visible in soft background. Camera gently dollies in.

SHOT 2 (1.00–2.00s) – Tight close-up on cloche: The chef dramatically unveils the dish with a flourish. A perfectly seared piece of protein rests center on an artfully arranged plate. The elderly lady's eyes widen with anticipation. Soft steam rises.

SHOT 3 (2.00–3.00s) – Medium shot, eye-level: The elderly lady takes her first bite. Her eyes bulge comically wide. She chews with an exaggerated look of pure, transcendent delight. A subtle blush appears on her cheeks. Background diners occasionally glance over, smiling warmly.

SHOT 4 (3.00–4.00s) – Medium close-up, slightly low angle: The elderly lady sets down her fork with surgical precision, folds her hands calmly, and stares directly at the chef with an amused, unimpressed expression. A long silence. She slowly raises one eyebrow. Music drops to dead silence.

SHOT 5 (4.00–5.00s) – Close-up, eye-level: The chef's confident smile freezes, then slowly falters. He glances at the dish, then back at the lady. His brow furrows in confusion, then concern. Sweat drop appears on his forehead.

SHOT 6 (5.00–6.00s) – Medium two-shot, eye-level: The elderly lady slides the plate back toward the chef with one finger. Her expression unchanged. The chef looks at the plate, utterly bewildered. An awkward silence hangs.

SHOT 7 (6.00–7.00s) – Medium shot, over-the-shoulder: The chef slowly picks up a fork and cuts a precise piece of the dish. He takes a bite. His face instantly cycles through shock, confusion, disbelief, and finally... shame. He looks down at his own dish. The golden perfection of his own cooking stares back.

SHOT 8 (7.00–8.00s) – Wide shot, high angle, slow push-in: A second identical dish arrives, presented by a younger sous chef. The elderly lady points at the new dish with a knowing smile, then back at the chef. She crosses her arms. The chef stands frozen, completely exposed. Other diners whisper and giggle.

SHOT 9 (8.00–9.00s) – Close-up on chef's face: Realization hits. He looks at his own dish, then at the new dish. His entire worldview crumbles. He takes off his tall hat and places it slowly on the table, a gesture of defeat. Subtle sad violin music begins.

SHOT 10 (9.00–10.00s) – Medium close-up in kitchen, eye-level: Elderly lady intensely stirs a sizzling copper pan with a large ladle, steam rising, blue flames underneath. Her face shows pure focus and confidence, eyes narrowed, slight smirk.

SHOT 11 (10.00–11.00s) – Tight close-up, high angle: Her hands elegantly plate the perfectly cooked dish (seared protein, Carrot, cherry tomatoes half, glossy sauce) from the copper pan onto a white plate using a spoon.

SHOT 12 (11.00–12.00s) – Medium two-shot in restaurant, eye-level: Elderly lady proudly holds up the finished plate toward the chef. The male chef, seated at the table, takes a big bite with a fork, eyes bulging in extreme delight and surprise.

SHOT 13 (12.00–13.00s) – Close-up two-shot: Elderly lady leans forward with a huge satisfied smile, gesturing with her hand as if saying "See?" while looking at the chef.

SHOT 14 (13.00–14.00s) – Close-up on male chef's face: He suddenly starts crying, big cartoon tears rolling down his cheeks.

SHOT 15 (14.00s – end) – Final wide shot, eye-level, slow pull-back: Elderly lady stands triumphantly beside the table, hands clasped, smug satisfied smile. Male chef is on his knees on the floor, head bowed, both hands dramatically holding his tall chef hat above his head.
