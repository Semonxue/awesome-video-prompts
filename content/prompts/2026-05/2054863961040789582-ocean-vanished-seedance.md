A cinematic apocalyptic mystery where the oceans vanish without warning.
0–3s: Nighttime coastline, calm and dark. Waves suddenly slow unnaturally, then freeze completely. A deep, low rumble vibrates through the air as the water begins to recede at impossible speed.
3–6s: Morning light reveals an empty ocean basin. Fishing boats sit stranded on dry seabed. People arrive at the shore in disbelief as fog rolls across exposed terrain stretching beyond the horizon.
6–9s: Cinematic reveal — colossal ancient creatures, fossilized leviathans, and massive skeletal remains lie half-buried in the drained ocean floor. Strange glowing ruins emerge between cracked seabed plates.
9–12s: Explorers cautiously descend into the abyss. Towering lost cities rise from the depths, covered in coral-like structures and alien architecture. Wind howls through the empty ocean trench.
12–15s: Final wide shot. The drained ocean stretches endlessly, revealing a world that was never meant to be seen. In the distance, something massive shifts beneath the cracked earth, suggesting the ocean is not truly gone.
Style: ultra cinematic realism, mysterious apocalyptic tone, massive environmental scale, eerie silence, detailed seabed landscapes, ancient ruins, soft golden sunrise lighting, film-grade color grading, suspense-driven sci-fi fantasy atmosphere.
