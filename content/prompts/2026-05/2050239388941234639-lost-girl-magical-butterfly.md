A little girl is lost in a dark, dense forest at night, surrounded by tall shadowy trees and a cold, quiet atmosphere. She looks scared and confused, slowly walking forward while searching for a way out. The wind gently rustles the leaves, adding a mysterious feeling. Suddenly, a glowing magical butterfly appears in front of her and softly lands on her hand. A warm golden light begins to shine from the butterfly, illuminating her face. Gradually, the darkness fades and the entire forest starts to glow with a soft magical light. A bright, sparkling path appears ahead between the trees. The girl's fear turns into hope, she smiles with relief and begins to follow the glowing path. Cinematic, emotional, soft glow lighting, magical atmosphere, ultra-realistic, 4K.
