A cinematic psychological horror sequence where the entire world experiences the exact same terrifying dream.

0–3s: Silent nighttime shots across different countries — people sleeping in apartments, trains, hospitals, hotels. A deep distorted hum slowly grows louder as all of them begin twitching in their sleep simultaneously.

3–6s: Rapid nightmare flashes — a dark endless hallway, flickering red lights, shadowy figures standing motionless, and a massive door slowly opening beneath a blood-red sky. The same terrifying symbol appears repeatedly.

6–9s: Everyone suddenly wakes up at the exact same moment in panic. People gasp for air, scream, cry, and stare at phones as emergency alerts flood social media worldwide. News broadcasts begin reporting the impossible phenomenon.

9–12s: Close-up shots reveal people from different countries drawing the exact same symbol from the dream. Television screens glitch briefly, showing the nightmare hallway for a split second before cutting out.

12–15s: Final cinematic shot. Millions of people stand frozen in cities around the world looking toward the sky as the same massive door from the dream slowly appears among the clouds above Earth.

Style: ultra cinematic psychological horror, unsettling atmosphere, realistic human panic, dreamlike transitions, analog glitch effects, eerie ambient sound design feel, dark volumetric lighting, film-grade color grading, global-scale suspense.
