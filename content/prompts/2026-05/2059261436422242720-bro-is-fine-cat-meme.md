0.0–1.5s — Medium-wide hook. Cat faces camera. Huge explosion blooms behind.

3.0–4.5s — Close-up on cat's deadpan face.
4.5–6.0s — Wide reveal of full fireball and smoke.
6.0–8.0s — 3/4 profile. Cat gives a tiny side glance.
