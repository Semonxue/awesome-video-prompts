A beautiful girl is sitting in a small wooden boat on a calm, peaceful lake, wearing stylish yet modest clothes. She is happily fishing, casting her line into the clear water. A small fishing box beside her holds some caught items. The environment is serene with soft sunlight reflecting on the water and gentle waves moving the boat.

Suddenly, her fishing hook catches a small frog instead of a fish. The girl looks surprised and confused as she carefully pulls it up. The frog stares at her for a moment and playfully winks. Then, suddenly, the frog jumps out of her hand and dives back into the water with a splash.

The girl becomes slightly annoyed, crossing her arms. In the background, another frog sitting on a lily pad seems to be laughing at the situation. The calm lake scene turns humorous and light-hearted. Cinematic, bright natural lighting, soft water reflections, 4K, funny tone.
