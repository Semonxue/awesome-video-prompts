High action, chaotic sequence on a collapsing bridge over a wide river in India, shot on 35mm with smoke, sparks, debris, and fire. Sound: explosions, metal tearing, engine roar, helicopter blades.
Shot 1 (0–3s):
Mid-chase. The biker in riding gear guns a sport bike across a partially destroyed bridge. Sections are missing; cars burn. A helicopter tracks him overhead. A blast behind sends debris skidding past him.
Shot 2 (3–6s):
Ahead, a broken slab has tilted into a ramp over a gap. He lines up fast, weaving around falling chunks. The helicopter dips lower, trying to keep pace over the gap.
Shot 3 (6–9s):
He hits the ramp at speed—launches into the air. The bike arcs over the river. At peak, he kicks off the bike, leaving it to fall, and reaches forward.
Shot 4 (9–12s):
He grabs the helicopter’s landing skid with both hands, swinging hard. Stabilizes for a split second, then with one hand plants a sticky explosive onto the underside near the engine housing. A small LED on the device shows “3”.
Shot 5 (12–15s):
He lets go, dropping cleanly toward the river. The LED ticks down off-screen—
Explosion—the helicopter ruptures in a fireball above. Debris rains as he hits the water below.
Splash. Smoke overhead.
