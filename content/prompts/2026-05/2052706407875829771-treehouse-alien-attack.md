A hyper-real action-horror sequence set inside a massive wooden treehouse structure high in the Amazon rainforest during humid daylight. Rope bridges, hanging lanterns, wooden platforms, dense jungle canopy all around. Leaves sway constantly in the wind. Sound: insects, distant birds, creaking wood, jungle ambience.

Shot 1 (0–2s):
On an open wooden platform of the treehouse, a rugged man (early 30s, holding a rifle) stands beside a woman wearing shorts and a bikini top.

Nearby jungle leaves suddenly begin shaking violently.

The man raises his gun instantly, tense:

"Stay back… something's there."

Shot 2 (2–5s):
Without warning, a huge slimy alien creature explodes out from the foliage and crashes onto the platform. Long limbs, oily skin, black slime dripping from its body.

The man fires instinctively but gets slammed backward into the wooden railing.

The woman screams and runs deeper inside the treehouse.

Shot 3 (5–9s):
The alien creature crawls after her at terrifying speed, smashing through hanging doors and wooden partitions. Thick black slime smears across walls and floors as it moves.

Interior corridors shake from the impact.

The woman sprints barefoot across creaking wooden floors, slipping slightly while knocking over furniture and ropes.

Shot 4 (9–12s):
She turns sharply into a room—

Dead end.

A wooden wall behind her.

She backs away in panic as the alien creature slowly crawls closer, unfolding its slick limbs unnaturally. Black slime drips from its open mouth onto the wooden floor.

Shot 5 (12–15s):
The creature opens its massive jaw inches from her face—

Gunshots erupt from behind.

The alien jerks violently and collapses sideways into the wall.

The man stands in the shattered doorway, rifle raised, breathing heavily.

The woman drops to the floor, trembling.

Black slime slowly spreads across the wood beneath the creature.
