FPV shot: The camera gets violently pulled forward at impossible speed. Skyscrapers whip past frame so fast they become streaks of light. The camera threads microscopic gaps between buildings milliseconds before impact. Violent downward drop into a subway tunnel. Sparks explode across concrete walls. The tunnel compresses tighter and tighter until the camera bursts directly into a collapsing reactor chamber filled with spinning debris and fire. Shockwaves slam the frame sideways. The camera gets dragged through explosions, through smoke, through collapsing machinery, then launched out over a storming ocean at hypersonic speed. Waves blur beneath frame. Lightning detonates overhead. No slowdown. Constant acceleration. Extreme inertia, brutal motion compression, survival-speed chaos.
