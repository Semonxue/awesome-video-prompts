It is late at night. Dark clouds fill the sky as lightning flashes again and again, and strong winds blow outside the window. The room feels cold and quiet.
A girl sits alone on her bed, slowly looking through a box of old photographs, her face calm but slightly emotional.

Suddenly, she notices something strange…
One person inside a photo starts moving slightly, as if the picture is alive.

Confused and scared, she brings the photo closer to her eyes, trying to understand what is happening.

The person inside the photo slowly turns and looks directly at her… then begins to reach a hand out of the picture.

The lights flicker… thunder strikes loudly…
In a split second, the girl disappears from the room.

Silence…

And then…
That same person from the photo is now standing inside the room, looking around slowly.
