Treat the attached storyboard @[image] as your guide for shot order, framing and motion. Animate the 8 panels in order as one continuous self-filmed phone livestream that flows naturally. Do NOT render any of the storyboard's panel labels, "Panel" text, captions, dots or borders in the video — those are reference markup only.

LOOK: fully PHOTOREALISTIC live-action. A real vibrant young woman with long bright pink hair and bold makeup at a real neon night market. Real crispy-insect street food, real fire, real vendor, real crowd. Keep her face, hair and outfit IDENTICAL to the reference in every shot.

CAMERA — strict phone-POV, matching each panel:
- Panels 1, 4, 6, 7: FRONT-camera selfie (her face at arm's length).
- Panels 2, 5: REAR-camera POV (her hand/the food in front of her).
- Panel 3: REAR POV on the vendor's flame show.
- Panel 8: REAR POV on the smiling vendor with a thumbs-up.
Natural handheld selfie motion, never a third-person or side angle.

KEY SHOT: Panel 6 — the insect skewer clearly at her mouth, an actual visible bite and chew, eyes wide. This bite must read clearly.

SPEECH — realistic English lip-sync, lively influencer voice, matching each panel:
1: "Guys, no idea what this is — getting it."
2: "Wait… those are bugs?!"
3 (off-screen): "Oh my god, the fire—"
4: "Hype me up, chat!"
5: "Okay… here we go."
6: (no words — just a crunch, frozen wide eyes)
7: "No way — this is amazing?!"
8: "Best thing ever. Coming back!"

ON-SCREEN TEXT — CRITICAL:
- All on-screen text and numbers must be in clean, correct ENGLISH only.
- Absolutely NO gibberish, NO random symbols, NO Chinese/Thai/Japanese characters anywhere — not on signs, not in the UI, not in the background.
- Keep a simple livestream UI: a red "LIVE" badge, an eye icon with a rising viewer count (start ~2.3K and tick up toward ~3.8K across the video), short readable English chat lines, a "Comment…" bar, and heart/share icons.
- Text and digits must stay sharp, legible and stable — do not warp, flicker or morph the letters or numbers.
- If background signage would otherwise show foreign text, keep it blurred, abstract or out of focus rather than rendering fake characters.

MOTION & PACING: make it feel alive, each beat flowing into the next with cause and effect. Tempo: hyped open → fire spectacle → nervous build → the bite (brief held beat) → burst of joy → warm close. Keep constant motion (fire, steam, crowd, neon flicker, her hair and gestures). Authentic phone-stream feel — slight handheld bounce, natural lighting, subtle grain. Never stiff, never a slideshow of stills.
