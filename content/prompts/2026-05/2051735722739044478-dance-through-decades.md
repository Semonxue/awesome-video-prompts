DANCE THROUGH DECADES

Cinematic continuous side-scrolling sequence, 16:9, 15 seconds. One unbroken shot — the camera tracks left to right following a single dancer whose style, costume, and environment morph through every decade of dance.

[0:00–0:02] A 1920s speakeasy. A woman in a fringed flapper dress does the Charleston — knees swinging, pearls flying, jazz band blazing behind her. The floor vibrates with stomping feet. Smoke and amber light. She kicks right and the room stretches.

[0:02–0:04] The speakeasy becomes a 1940s ballroom. She's now in a flowing gown, swing dancing with a soldier — he tosses her between his legs, she flies through the air, lands in a split. Big band horns blare. The crowd claps in rhythm.

[0:04–0:06] The ballroom becomes a 1960s TV studio. She's in go-go boots doing the Twist on a checkerboard floor. Background dancers in matching outfits. A psychedelic light show swirls. The camera pushes through a bead curtain.

[0:06–0:08] 1977. A disco floor. She's in sequins and platforms, pointing to the sky and striking the classic pose. Mirror ball sends a galaxy of light across every surface. The bass line thumps through the floor. Roller skaters orbit past.

[0:08–0:10] The disco melts into a 1984 street corner. Boombox on the ground. She breakdances in a tracksuit — windmill, headspin, freeze. A crowd circles. Cardboard on concrete. Graffiti-covered walls. Raw and electric.

[0:10–0:12] The street becomes a 2000s music video set. She's in low-rise jeans and a crop top, doing choreography from the era — sharp, synchronized, MTV-ready. Rain machines activate. Backup dancers appear and vanish.

[0:12–0:14] The set dissolves into a present-day bedroom. She's in sweats, dancing alone to her phone propped on a stack of books. TikTok ring light. No audience. But she dances with more joy and freedom than every era before — no rules, no stage, just her.

[0:14–0:15] She spins one final time. Every era's dance floor appears as transparent layers behind her — speakeasy, ballroom, disco, street, studio — all dancing simultaneously. She stops. Bows. Every era applauds. Fade to black.

Sound: jazz piano/Charleston stomps → big band swing → 60s pop/go-go → disco bass/mirror ball shimmer → boombox hip-hop → 2000s pop choreography → phone speaker/bedroom quiet → all eras merging into one final beat → applause → silence.
