"Parallel Cat Universe Podcast"
Format: horizontal 16:9
Duration: 15s
FPS: 24
Style: cinematic podcast show, American dark comedy, subtle absurdism, claymation + semi-realistic hybrid
Audio: light lo-fi podcast intro → awkward silence → sudden TV SFX → audience laugh track + applause
Lighting: warm studio key light, soft shadows, slight flicker from old TV

0:00–0:02 — Establishing wide shot
A cozy recording studio. LEFT: a white cat host wearing silver-rim glasses, pink dress, lavender eyeshadow, bright red lipstick, resting chin on one hand, bored elegance. Beside her, an old CRT TV (screen off, dusty). RIGHT: Einstein as a clay puppet, oversized head, messy white hair, wrinkled suit, holding a book titled "AI Physics."
Soft lo-fi intro music fades.

HOST (dry tone):
"So, Professor… what do you think about Cat Master making dumb comedy videos?"

0:02–0:04 — Medium close-up on Einstein
He adjusts the book slowly, eyes slightly unfocused but confident.

EINSTEIN:
"In my professional opinion… stupidity is a fundamental force of the universe."

Audience: light chuckle

0:04–0:06 — Shoulder view from behind host toward Einstein
Einstein leans forward slightly, more engaged.

EINSTEIN:
"And Cat Master… is simply harnessing it."

Audience: louder laughter + claps

0:06–0:07 — Quick close-up on host (NEW BEAT)
She raises one eyebrow slightly, curiosity breaking her bored expression.

HOST (short, curious):
"…Harnessing it?"

0:07–0:09 — Sudden TV activation (wide shot)
The CRT TV beside the host flickers ON with a loud bzzt. Static → cuts to chaotic footage:
An orange cat in a gray Taoist robe fighting suited hyenas wearing sunglasses in an ancient courtyard (sanheyuan), dust flying, exaggerated kung-fu chaos.

0:09–0:11 — Close-up on Einstein, TV light flickering on his face
He watches intensely, eyes widening like revelation.

EINSTEIN (excited whisper):
"This… confirms my theory."

0:11–0:12.5 — Insert TV footage (dynamic zoom)
Orange cat launches into absurd slow-motion spin kick while hyenas dramatically overreact.

Audience: explosive laughter

0:12.5–0:14 — Close-up on Einstein
He raises one finger like a genius breakthrough moment.

EINSTEIN:
"Quantum physics suggests… a parallel cat universe."

0:14–0:15 — Wide shot (final punchline)
Host side-eyes the camera. Einstein stares directly forward.

EINSTEIN (dead serious):
"Also… statistically speaking… the audience might be AI."

Beat.

Audience: HUGE laughter + applause

TV flickers. Freeze frame energy. End.
