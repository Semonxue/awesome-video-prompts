DURATION: 10 seconds

STYLE:
Ultra realistic cinematic FPV roller coaster skateboarding sequence, gritty urban realism, sunset golden hour lighting, grounded street skateboarding physics, hybrid one-continuous-shot illusion mixed with cinematic multi-shot editing, aggressive but readable camera movement, realistic inertia, intense momentum, cinematic motion blur, realistic rooftop textures, dusty urban atmosphere, IMAX-scale energy.

MAIN CHARACTER:
Adult male skateboarder. Realistic face, focused expression, grounded street skater appearance.

CAMERA LANGUAGE:
Hybrid FPV roller coaster and cinematic multi-shot filmmaking.
Combination of:
extreme FPV chase shots,
side tracking shots,
front tracking shots,
cinematic full-body hero shots,
low angle wheel shots,
close-up facial shots,
wide rooftop reveal shots.
The camera must constantly change perspective dynamically while maintaining flow continuity. Camera movement feels physical and heavy with realistic inertia, not weightless CGI.

ENVIRONMENT:
Dense realistic urban rooftops during sunset. Rooftop houses, taller buildings, antennas, water tanks, metal rooftops, narrow rooftop gaps, moving train passing between buildings. Dry weather, warm sunset glow, long cinematic shadows.

SEQUENCE:

0:00 - 0:01
Cold open.
Wide cinematic full-body shot from the front as the skateboarder bursts across rooftops at high speed during sunset. Hoodie flaps in the wind. The camera rapidly pulls backward while tracking the skater head-on. Intense rooftop parallax.

0:01 - 0:02
Hard whip pan transition into extreme FPV side tracking shot close to the skateboard wheels. Small ollie across a rooftop gap. Sparks briefly appear from a quick metal grind. Fast near miss with rooftop antennas.

0:02 - 0:03
Quick cinematic close-up of the skater's face while moving at high speed. Focused expression, sunlight hitting the face, realistic wind movement on the hoodie. The background streaks with motion blur.

0:03 - 0:04
FPV roller coaster movement returns. The camera aggressively dives beside the skater while he rides across slanted rooftops and performs a subtle wall ride. Realistic balance corrections and wheel vibration.

0:04 - 0:05
Wide rooftop reveal shot.
The moving train suddenly appears between the buildings below. Full-body side tracking shot of the skater accelerating toward the rooftop edge.

0:05 - 0:07
EPIC SLOW MOTION CENTERPIECE.
Ultra cinematic multi-shot sequence.
The skateboarder launches from the rooftop toward the moving train.

Shot 1:
Full-body side profile in slow motion.

Shot 2:
Front close-up of the skater's face mid-air, focused expression, hoodie and pants violently flapping in the wind.

Shot 3:
Wide cinematic orbit shot showing the skater floating above the moving train during sunset.

Shot 4:
Extreme close-up of spinning skateboard wheels.

Strong cinematic atmosphere, drifting dust particles, subtle camera shake, realistic motion blur, IMAX-scale feeling.

0:07 - 0:09
Hard impact back into realtime.
The skateboard lands heavily onto the moving train rooftop. Extreme FPV roller coaster energy resumes. Fast traversal across train cars, ducking obstacles, intense near misses with poles and rooftop structures, aggressive banking camera movement, rapid dives and pull-up recoveries.

0:09 - 0:10
Final cinematic ending.
The skateboarder jumps off the train and lands cleanly onto the street below.

The camera swings around into a frontal full-body tracking shot revealing the character's face one final time while he rolls forward calmly into the sunset. The camera slowly stabilizes while maintaining subtle momentum and wheel vibration.
