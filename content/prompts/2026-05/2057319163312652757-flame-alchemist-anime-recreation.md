0–1s
The camera feels like a crew member secretly filming from behind the movie set using an iPhone 17.
Slight handheld shaking, slightly tilted framing.
Live-action Roy Mustang stands in the middle of the ruins, his black military uniform illuminated by firelight.
Lust kneels on the burning ground, parts of her body already charred while regenerating.
Sparks, dust, and smoke drift through the air.
Blurred movie cameras, monitors, and the shoulder of a crew member are visible in the foreground.
Only the sound of burning flames and distant falling debris can be heard.
1–3s (Main Hook)
Roy suddenly snaps his fingers without warning.
In the very next instant, massive flames violently erupt around Lust's body.
Huge orange-red pillars of fire blast upward.
The iPhone camera instantly loses exposure control because of the overwhelming brightness.
The frame briefly becomes overexposed and washed out white.
The cameraman instinctively stumbles backward as if hit by the heatwave.
The camera shakes violently.
The flames illuminate the entire ruined street.
This sequence must feel like a real dangerous accident suddenly happening on an actual movie set.
3–4s
The flames continue burning intensely.
Lust struggles in pain while the edges of her body constantly burn away and rapidly regenerate.
Her long hair is blown wildly by the heatwave.
Visible heat distortion appears in the air.
Roy remains standing still, calmly watching her.
The camera experiences slight blur and focus drifting due to the extreme heat.
4–5s (Second Impact Moment)
Roy snaps his fingers again rapidly.
This time the movement is faster and more ruthless.
The remaining flames in the air and on the ground instantly reignite in a second explosion.
A much more violent burst of fire detonates from the ground.
Large amounts of sparks shoot directly toward the camera.
The cameraman instinctively ducks to avoid them.
The camera suffers from intense motion blur and temporary loss of focus.
