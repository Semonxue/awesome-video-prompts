Create a 15-second ultra-cinematic, dark psychological video titled: "What your fear looks like in real life"
STYLE: dark cinematic realism, psychological horror, high contrast lighting, shallow depth of field, slow atmospheric camera movement, subtle film grain, emotional tension.
SCENE 1 (0-3s): A lonely person walking at night in an empty urban street. Streetlights flicker softly. The environment feels quiet but heavy. Camera follows from behind in a slow tracking shot.
SCENE 2 (3-6s): Subtle distortion begins in the shadows. The darkness behind street corners and walls starts moving unnaturally. Barely visible humanoid shadow shapes begin forming-unstable, shifting, watching.
SCENE 3 (6-10s): The person starts feeling uneasy and looks around. The shadow creatures become clearer now-tall, distorted figures made of smoke and void-like darkness, silently following but never touching. Reflections in windows briefly show them, but not directly.
SCENE 4 (10-13s): The fear intensifies. Shadows multiply and move closer. They mirror the person's emotions-hesitation, anxiety, panic. The environment slightly warps as if reality is bending under psychological pressure.
FINAL SCENE (13-15s): Wide cinematic shot: the person stops walking, surrounded but not physically touched. The shadow creatures stand all around, forming a circle. One steps closer-screen cuts to black instantly.
MOOD: fear of the unseen, psychological dread, isolation, inner anxiety visualized as external entities.
CAMERA: slow tracking, subtle handheld tension, occasional abrupt focus shifts for unease.
LIGHTING: dim streetlights, heavy shadows, fog diffusion, high contrast blacks.
QUALITY: ultra-detailed, cinematic 8K look, realistic textures, immersive horror atmosphere.
