Create a 15-second ultra-realistic cinematic B-roll video of a luxury perfume bottle with a delicate pink satin ribbon tied around its neck.

Scene 1 (0-4s): Begin with an extreme macro deep-focus shot of the intricate crystal cap. The facets sparkle as soft, diffused light glides across the surface. Tiny highlights shimmer, emphasizing premium craftsmanship. Background is creamy blur with soft pink tones.

Scene 2 (4-9s): Camera slowly tilts and pans downward in smooth slow motion, revealing the neck of the bottle wrapped in a silky pink satin ribbon. The ribbon gently moves as if touched by a subtle breeze. Focus gradually shifts to the transparent glass body filled with glowing pink liquid.

Scene 3 (9-13s): Full bottle reveal on a glossy reflective marble surface. The reflection is clean and slightly diffused. Soft, ethereal lighting creates shimmering refractions through the glass and liquid. Background shows out-of-focus pink roses and warm golden bokeh, creating a dreamy luxury atmosphere.

Scene 4 (13-15s): Final hero shot. Camera makes a subtle push-in. Light flares softly kiss the edges of the bottle. The pink liquid glows elegantly. End with a premium still frame feel.
