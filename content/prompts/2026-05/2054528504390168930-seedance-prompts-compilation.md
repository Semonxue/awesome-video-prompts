  Prompt 1: The 70s Disco Heist

  An ultra-wide shot reveals a neon-lit Los Angeles boulevard, 1974. A gold 1971 Chevrolet Caprice slowly rolls through the night, headlights cutting through the fog. Camera follows from the sidewalk as it passes record stores, palm trees, and neon signs. The sedan stops at a closed record shop. The trunk pops open. A figure in a wide-lapel suit and skinny tie hops out — mid-forties, cool as hell. He grabs a cardboard box marked FRAGILE from the trunk. He looks at the camera, raises one eyebrow, and smiles. He walks toward the shop entrance, footsteps echoing. He pulls out a brick-sized mobile phone and makes a call. Cut to black. Title card: "DISCO INFERNO."

  Style: 35mm anamorphic, warm orange and blue color grade, cinematic dolly, 1970s nostalgic.

  Prompt 2: The Medieval Tavern Cleanup

  Extreme wide shot of a crumbling medieval tavern exterior at dusk. Rain pours down on a wooden sign reading "The Red Stag." Lightning flashes. Cut to: inside — a enormous, messy bar fight in slow motion. Chairs flying, mugs shattering, a bar wench spinning and slamming a hoodlum over the bar. A dwarf with a beard like a broom grabs a full keg and rolls it across the floor, crushing three invaders. A monk is literally thrown through a second-floor railing and lands on a table that explodes on impact. Candles fly through the air lit, trailing fire. The tavern keep, a massive woman with arms like ham hocks, appears from behind the bar with a battleaxe. Everyone freezes. Wide smile. Slow clap. Cut to black.

  Style: 24mm, desaturated medieval tones, rain and fire effects, slow-motion action, chaotic energy.

  Prompt 3: The Cyberpunk Noodle Brawl

  Ground-level shot of a rain-soaked neon alley in Neo-Chengdu, 2087. Steam rises from a street noodle cart. A woman in a chrome-trimmed qipao and glowing LED braids eats noodles with chopsticks, completely unbothered. Three corporate security guards approach, armored and menacing. The first guard grabs the cart. The woman's chopsticks don't even tremble. She slowly sets them down. She grabs a noodle strand, flicks it — it slices through the first guard's helmet like a vibro-wire. She grabs a ladle, spin-kicks the second guard into a neon sign. Third guard draws a laser pistol — she catches the beam with a steam basket, deflects it into a water pipe, which bursts and sprays the guard into a noodle rack. She goes back to her noodles. Sips the broth. The surviving guards scramble. She smiles at the camera. Slurp.

  Style: Cyberpunk, neon-lit rain, steam and reflections, wire-fu action, 2087 futuristic Asia, vibrant color.

  Prompt 4: MOMA chaos

  Extreme wide shot of the Museum of Modern Art lobby in New York. Families in casual clothes browse quietly. A woman in an all-black designer outfit stands contemplatively before a massive Rothko painting — deep crimson and maroon, floating in dark space. She stares. The painting seems to grow... darker. She takes a step back. The red in the painting begins to bleed at the edges. A family notices too, pointing. Security guards appear in the background. The painting begins to leak actual thick red paint down the museum wall. Visitors begin to scream and run. The woman doesn't move. She smiles. The paint drips form shapes — figures, faces, a whole scene of chaos within the abstract. Camera pushes in on the woman's face — tears of genuine awe. Title card: "ABSTRACT."

  Style: Contemporary art museum aesthetic, cinematic tension, red and maroon tones, slow-burn horror, wide shot to close-up push.

  Prompt 5: Supermarket cleanup

  Wide establishing shot of a supermarket at night — massive, half-empty aisles lit by buzzing fluorescents. A janitor in a yellow vest mops the floor, talking to himself. He's been here for years, knows every aisle. Camera follows behind him as he enters the cereal aisle. Something is wrong. The cereal boxes are... too perfect. The colors are too vivid. He taps a box — it feels like cardboard. He looks closer — the cartoon mascot is staring at him. The mascot's eyes move. He drops the mop. He backs away. The cereal boxes begin to wobble. One box falls — out spills not cereal but a stream of white powder. A customer in sweatpants emerges from between the shelves, walking like an old man but... wrong. The janitor runs. The customer-walking-wrong starts running too — fast, broken-glass gait. He's coming. Wide shot. Cut to black.

  Style: Horror-comedy, fluorescent lighting, grocery store mundanity twisted, slow tension build, wide-to-close-up push.

  Prompt 6: Fast food revenge

  Wide shot of a suburban McDonald's parking lot at sunset. Kids on bikes, cars pulling in. Inside: a woman in her mid-30s in a fast food uniform, exhausted, mopping. Corporate efficiency posters on walls. A manager in a polo shirt micromanages her every move. She's done. She puts down the mop. She picks up a salt shaker. She shakes it — but it's not salt. Pink powder fills the air. Everyone who breathes it starts... dancing. Not fun dancing — weird, broken robot dancing, like their bodies aren't theirs. The manager tries to run but his legs do the worm. The woman walks out the front door, past the dancing people, over to a vintage convertible. She gets in. She drives off, smiling in the rearview mirror. No one can stop her.

  Style: Surreal comedy, suburban fast food setting, fluorescent lighting, synchronized chaotic dancing, slow zoom out.

  Prompt 7: The Victorian Tea Party Disaster

  Wide shot of a pristine English garden, 1887. Long white table, fine china, roses, top hats, parasols. The aristocrats sit for tea — dainty, proper, so polite. A waiter brings a silver tray with a three-tiered cake stand. He places it on the table. He lifts the lid. Out crawls a tiny animatronic spider. One aristocrat screams. The spider hops to another plate — triggers a chain reaction: teacups rattle, a sugar bowl falls, cream splashes everywhere. The orderly tea party descends into chaos. A dowager duchess bonks a vicar with her umbrella. A general spills his tea and goes absolutely feral. A butler slips on a teacup and crashes through the dessert table. Everyone runs. The garden is left in ruins — broken china, scattered scones, spilled Earl Grey on the manicured lawn. A single teacup rolls across the ground. A small dog runs past, wearing a tiny top hat. End card: "XOXO."

  Style: Victorian period aesthetic, saturated color, slow-motion comedy chaos, wide establishing to ruined garden, playful and absurd.
