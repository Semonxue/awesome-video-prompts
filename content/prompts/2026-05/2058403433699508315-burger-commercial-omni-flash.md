Use @[storyboard ref] as the storyboard reference. Follow the panels as sequential keyframes in order. Do not show panel borders, labels, annotations or text in the final video.
Create a 10-second cinematic food delivery ad for The Burger Co.
Character: Young woman, mid-twenties, voluminous curly hair, oversized mustard yellow hoodie. Keep her appearance consistent across all shots.
Follow this shot sequence:
01 — ECU burger on wooden board, steam rising, slow low-angle push-in. Product hook.
02 — Wide shot. Woman slumped on sofa, bored, empty coffee table.
03 — MCU. She picks up phone, opens delivery app. Screen glow on her face.
04 — Over-shoulder. Thumb scrolls to burger, taps order.
05 — Wide static. She watches the door impatiently. Comic pause.
06 — Front door. She opens it. Delivery hand enters frame with branded paper bag. She reacts with delight.
07 — MCU. She lifts the bag to her face, inhales, eyes closed, deep satisfaction.
08 — Floating beauty shot. Burger components suspended mid-air, slow rotation, dark warm background.
09 — MCU push-in. She takes a huge first bite. Steam escapes. Thumbs up.
10 — Wide hero. Burger foreground, woman smiling softly behind. The Burger Co. logo upper left.
Style: Photorealistic, warm amber color grade, shallow depth of field, cinematic food commercial quality. Steam and melted cheese must look appetizing and real. No AI artifacts. No watermarks.
