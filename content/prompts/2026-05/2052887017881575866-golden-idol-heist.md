ANCIENT MAYAN RUIN ESCAPE. Style: High-octane survival adventure, "Uncharted meets Apocalypto," photorealistic jungle lighting, handheld "GOPRO-on-a-stuntman" energy. Tactile textures: crumbling limestone, slick moss, ancient gold, and frayed rope. Same rugged explorer consistently throughout.

SHOT 1 - WIDE ESTABLISHING SHOT (THE DESCENT): Colosseal overgrown stone temple in steaming tropical canyon, massive waterfall, structure begins to tilt, thousands of birds erupt, dust billows.

SHOT 2 - HANDHELD TRACKING (INTERNAL CHAOS): Inside collapsing corridor, sprinting behind explorer leaping over floor-trap pit, moody flickering torchlight mixed with sun shafts, stones crashing.

SHOT 3 - TIGHT REACTION SHOT (THE PENDULUM): Massive stone pendulum blade, rusted and moss-covered, swinging erratically due to temple tilt.

SHOT 4 - THE LEAP (LOW ANGLE PHYSICS): Desperate running start, boots slipping on wet moss, catches fraying hemp rope over bottomless dark chasm.

SHOT 5 - CLOSE UP (THE GRIP): Hands on rope, fibers snapping one by one, dust falls into open gasping mouth, lunges for stone gargoyle as rope gives way.

SHOT 6 - POV (THE SLIDE): Sliding down 45-degree algae-covered stone ramp, jungle floor rushing up, daggers to slow descent, sparks flying.

SHOT 7 - MEDIUM IMPACT SHOT: Bursts through vines, crashes onto muddy jungle floor, rolls, slams into massive tree root, lying there clutching gold idol.

SHOT 8 - THE REVEAL (WIDE SHOT): Massive temple collapses behind him in spectacular dust cloud, sound roars then fades into waterfall.

SHOT 9 - FINAL CLOSE UP: Mud-covered face, blood from lip, shaky laugh, glimmering eyes, raspy whisper: "Found you."

SHOT 10 - LONG SHOT (FADE OUT): Stands with limp, trekking into dense foliage, camera pulls back high above canopy until he is just a speck, sun catches gold one last time.
