Continuous tracking shot :

Continuous first-person camera of the dragon with colossal transparent giant wings, at ultra-high speed, faithfully following the red arrow.

Flight tracking of the dragon with colossal transparent giant wings from a low angle over the river, splashing in the water as it flies, with exotic villages and jungle vegetation surrounding it, following around a white tree with blue light.

Flight tracking of the dragon at speed along the river with a port and an exotic village with orange glass domes.

Flight tracking of the dragon around a colossal giant tree with an ancient white trunk, red leaves, and light inside.

Photorealistic cinematic scene.

This addictive technique is possible in Seedance with the right prompt.

But the most important thing is to provide reference images of the beginning of the path without the arrow.

I've added three reference images of a close-up view of the river.

I'm doing more tests to optimize the process and will have conclusions soon.
