A cinematic clash between ancient valor and modern warfare.

0–3s: A devastated modern battlefield at dusk. Tanks roll through smoke-filled streets while drones scan overhead. Explosions light the horizon. Suddenly, a glowing portal tears open in the middle of the chaos.

3–6s: A medieval knight on horseback emerges from the portal wearing battered silver armor and carrying a massive sword. Soldiers freeze in confusion as ash and sparks swirl around him.

6–9s: The knight rides directly through the warzone while missiles streak overhead in slow motion. Drones lock onto him, but he slices one apart midair with impossible precision.

9–12s: Cinematic battle montage — tanks firing, bullets ricocheting off armor, flames reflecting across the knight's helmet while he advances fearlessly through explosions and collapsing buildings.

12–15s: Final epic wide shot. The knight stands alone atop a destroyed tank surrounded by smoke and burning wreckage, sword pointed toward the sky as drones circle through thunderclouds above.

Style: ultra cinematic realism, gritty war atmosphere, medieval fantasy meets modern military sci-fi, dramatic fire and smoke effects, high-speed action choreography, realistic destruction physics, IMAX-style cinematography, emotional heroic tone.
