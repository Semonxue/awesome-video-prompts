14-second cute 3D style animated comedy short, ultra-detailed, vibrant colors, warm cinematic lighting, smooth 24fps animation, exaggerated expressions and physics, emotional and funny tone.    

**SHOT 1 (0.00–1.00s)** – Wide establishing shot, eye-level, slow push-in: Cozy modern apartment dining area. A 30-year-old Chinese daughter (messy bun, casual t-shirt and apron, nervous but proud) lifts a plate cover to serve her mother. The 60-year-old Chinese mother (short permed graying hair, cardigan, glasses) sits at the table with a warm, knowing smile.    

**SHOT 2 (1.00–2.00s)** – Tight close-up, high angle, static: Beautifully plated but slightly imperfect homemade dish (seared fish, stir-fried vegetables, rice). Daughter’s hands visible in background.    

**SHOT 3 (2.00–3.00s)** – Extreme close-up on mother’s face: She takes a bite, eyes widen in delight then narrow playfully. Chews while giving her daughter a gentle, knowing side-eye with a soft smirk.    

**SHOT 4 (3.00–4.00s)** – Close-up on daughter’s face, slight push-in: Her proud smile instantly turns to wide-eyed shock, mouth open, eyebrows raised high.    

**SHOT 5 (4.00–5.00s)** – Extreme close-up on mother’s face: She lowers her chopsticks with a cheeky, loving grin, eyes sparkling with mischief.    

**SHOT 6 (5.00–6.00s)** – Wide shot, low angle in kitchen: Mother (now in colorful oversized apron) stands confidently by the stove, hands on hips. Daughter stands behind her in the doorway, looking surprised.    

**SHOT 7 (6.00–7.00s)** – Same kitchen wide shot, dynamic tilt down: Bright orange flames flare from the wok. Mother leans in with fierce, focused energy.    

**SHOT 8 (7.00–8.00s)** – Dynamic low-angle close-up: Mother dramatically raises a large knife, fierce yet caring expression, surrounded by flying slices of beef and vegetables.    

**SHOT 9 (8.00–9.00s)** – Extreme close-up, low angle, fast-paced: Ginger, garlic, green onions, bok choy, tomatoes, and chilies being sliced in slow-motion with juice and pieces flying.    

**SHOT 10 (9.00–10.00s)** – Medium close-up, eye-level: Mother intensely stirs a sizzling wok, steam rising, strong flames below, face full of confident focus and a warm smile.    

**SHOT 11 (10.00–11.00s)** – Tight close-up, high angle: Her hands elegantly plate the perfect glossy stir-fried beef and vibrant vegetables.    

**SHOT 12 (11.00–12.00s)** – Medium two-shot at dining table: Mother proudly presents the finished plate. Daughter takes a big bite, eyes bulging in delighted surprise.    

**SHOT 13 (12.00–13.00s)** – Close-up two-shot: Mother leans forward with a huge satisfied smile, gesturing “See?” lovingly.   

**SHOT 14 (13.00–14.00s)** – Close-up on daughter: She bursts into happy tears, big cartoon tears rolling down her cheeks.    

**SHOT 15 (14.00s – end)** – Final wide shot, slow pull-back: Mother stands triumphantly. Daughter sitting on the table crying  happy tears and saying "mom, you are the best"
