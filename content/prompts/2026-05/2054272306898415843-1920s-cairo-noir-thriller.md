A retro-styled cinematic short set in 1920s Cairo. A woman in a glamorous flapper dress is being chased through a bustling souk marketplace at night, winding between narrow alleyways draped with colorful textiles and lanterns. She carries a mysterious leather briefcase. She darts past spice stalls with cascading pyramids of saffron, paprika, and cumin, past a tailor's shop with intricate brass lamps hanging in the window, past a hookah cafe with men in jalabiyas playing backgammon under dim amber lights, and past a cobbler's stall with colorful slippers. She moves quickly and urgently, glancing nervously behind her. A menacing detective in a felt fedora and trench coat pursues her through the shadowy labyrinth, his silhouette sharp against the warm golden light of the market. Low-key lighting with dramatic shadows. Film grain. High contrast. Cinematic 4K. Smooth camera tracking shots. Rain begins to fall as the woman slips away. Atmospheric and moody. High production value. High fps. Sharp focus. Realistic lighting and physics.
