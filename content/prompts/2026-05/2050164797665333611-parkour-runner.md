A dynamic 15-second cinematic animation set in a graffiti-covered urban street environment.
[Environment]
A narrow street filled with colorful graffiti walls, concrete ground, urban textures, posters, and street art. Slight dust and ambient particles in the air.
[Action Timeline]
0-3s: Starts with a natural run forward, then jumps into a fast spinning aerial flip.
3-6s: Smooth mid-air twists and agile parkour-style wall kicks using the surrounding walls.
6-10s: Brief grounded landing, immediately transitioning into a high backflip combined with a corkscrew spin.
10-13s: Dramatic slow-motion mid-air extension pose, emphasizing flow and control.
13-15s: Clean landing with dust impact and subtle ground vibration.
[Camera Work]
Professional cinematic camera direction. Start with a low-angle tracking shot (steadycam feel), slightly behind and to the side.
Transition into a fast orbital camera circling around during aerial movement.
Introduce dynamic parallax using foreground elements (walls, poles).
Use speed ramping (normal → slow-motion at 10-13s → back to real-time).
Final landing includes subtle handheld shake for realism.
Lens behavior: 24mm wide-angle for dynamic motion, slight perspective distortion.
Use depth of field shifts (focus pull) during movement. Include motion blur consistent with camera speed.
[Motion & Physics]
Realistic gravity, weight, and inertia. Precise and fluid transitions between moves. Cloth and hair react naturally to motion and wind.
[Style]
Cinematic, high contrast urban lighting, slightly gritty atmosphere, 4K, film-like color grading, motion blur, depth of field.
