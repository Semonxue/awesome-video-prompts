A cinematic sci-fi catastrophe unfolds over a modern mega city.

0–3s: Normal day in New York. Busy streets, traffic, distant sirens, birds flying between skyscrapers. A subtle distortion appears in the clouds above the skyline.

3–6s: A massive black hole suddenly forms in the sky. The atmosphere warps instantly. Light bends unnaturally, street reflections ripple, and the horizon begins to curve inward.

6–9s: Panic erupts. Cars slide without control, glass vibrates, and people struggle against an invisible force. Skyscrapers begin leaning upward as debris and water are slowly pulled into the sky.

9–12s: Cinematic close-ups of gravitational chaos — windows shattering in reverse motion, ocean water from nearby shores lifting into the air, billboards twisting into streaks of light toward the singularity.

12–15s: Final ultra-wide shot. The black hole dominates the sky above Manhattan, swallowing sunlight as entire buildings begin rising toward it. The city is silhouetted against a collapsing horizon, fading into darkness.
