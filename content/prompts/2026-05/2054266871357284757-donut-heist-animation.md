[CINEMATIC SETUP] Full-color 3D animated comedy short, cinematic cartoon style, bright sunny park, polished lighting, expressive faces, vibrant colors. Keep left-right continuity: the squirrel always runs from screen left to right straight toward the raccoon and donut box. The raccoon stays by the bench.

[CHARACTERS] Chubby donut-loving raccoon with striped tail. Small hyperactive squirrel with fluffy tail.

[TIMELINE]

0–1.5s: [Wide / Static] Raccoon finds a donut box on a park bench.
1.5–3s: [Medium / Push-In] Raccoon opens the box in awe.
3–4.5s: [MCU / Pan] Raccoon hears rustling from screen left and looks that way.
4.5–6s: [Wide / Tracking] Squirrel sprints from screen left toward the bench and donut box.
6–7.5s: [Medium / Push-In] Raccoon protects the donut box.
7.5–9s: [Close / Forward] Squirrel crashes into raccoon, donuts fly.
9–11s: [Close / Tight] Both scramble for falling donuts.
11–13s: [Medium / Static] Both freeze, each holding half a donut.
13–15s: [Wide / Final Hold] They glare, then bite at the same time.

[STYLE & QUALITY] Sunny park, green grass, grey-brown raccoon, orange-brown squirrel, pink icing, smooth comedic timing.
