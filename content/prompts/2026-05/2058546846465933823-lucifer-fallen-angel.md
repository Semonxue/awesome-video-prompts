[0-3s] Lucifer kneels in the crater of his landing scorched earth in perfect circle around him, smoke rising. He is still, head down, both wings spread one white one black as if catching breath after the fall. Camera orbits the crater edge slowly.

[3-7s] He slowly raises his head. Looks up at the sky above where he fell from. His two different eyes reflect heaven above and earth below. Expression: not anger. Grief. Slow push-in on his face.

[7-11s] He stands. Black flame sword rises from the crater ground into his hand as if recognizing its master. Cold black flames erupt from blade they move wrong, burn downward. His scorched wings spread fully. Magnificent and terrible.

[11-15s] He looks at camera directly, knowingly. The first time any character breaks fourth wall. He smiles slowly not evil, something far more complex. He turns and walks into darkness, wings folding. Camera holds on empty crater, then tilts up to sky a single fallen star visible. Silence.
