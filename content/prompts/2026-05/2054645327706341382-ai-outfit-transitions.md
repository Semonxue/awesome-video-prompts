Create a 15 second high energy continuous shot featuring a confident male subject moving through rapidly evolving eras. The camera follows him in one fluid motion as he runs, spins, slides, and jumps forward while both his outfit and the environment transform around him. Start in a prehistoric cave with firelight and stone walls, where he begins as a caveman. As he slams his club into the ground, the cave morphs into an Egyptian temple, then a Roman arena, a medieval castle hall, a pirate ship deck, a Victorian street, a 1920s jazz club, a neon 1980s dance floor, a modern city rooftop, a cyberpunk alley, and finally a futuristic spaceship launch bay. Each transformation is triggered by action and feels seamless, with no cuts, only match motion and morphing scenery. Keep the pace fast, stylish, fun, and cinematic. End with the male subject in a sleek astronaut suit sprinting into a glowing airlock as the ship launches behind him in an epic climax.
