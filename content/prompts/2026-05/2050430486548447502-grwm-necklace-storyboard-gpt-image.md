<<<image_1>>> as the storyboard sequence reference. Follow the nine panels in order as a single 15-second animated short. 

CUT 1 — 0:00–0:02s (Hook)
Close-up, looking down at camera — sunglasses on, tongue out.
🗣️ "Okay wait, let me show you something…"
CUT 2 — 0:02–0:04s (Reveal)
Slow push of sunglasses down, eyes lock into camera.
🗣️ "You ready?"
CUT 3 — 0:04–0:06s (Detail shot)
Close-up on gold heart necklace, finger taps it.
🗣️ "This necklace though — obsessed."
CUT 4 — 0:06–0:08s (Fit check)
Full body tilt — hand runs down outfit.
🗣️ "Playboy top, leather skirt — I cannot stop wearing this combo."
CUT 5 — 0:08–0:10s (Hair flip)
Side angle, slow hair flip.
🗣️ "And my hair is actually cooperating today, so…"
CUT 6 — 0:10–0:12s (Attitude moment)
Sunglasses back on, arms crossed, dead stare into lens.
🗣️ "Yeah. That's the look. You're welcome."
CUT 7 — 0:12–0:15s (Outro/CTA)
Finger-gun point → wink → walk off frame.
🗣️ "Link in bio, go get it before it sells out."
Voiceover tone: Confident, casual, unbothered
Music: Low background bass, let the voice carry
