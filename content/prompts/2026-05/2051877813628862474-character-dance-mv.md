Create a 15-second vertical dance music video featuring the character from @dance.png.

Format: Vertical 9:16, 24fps, 15 seconds duration.

Style: Real-person MV aesthetic, cinematic feel, soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field, fluid body movement, natural skin texture, realistic fabric movement. No filters, color grading shifts, or visual effects throughout — maintain a consistent visual style from start to finish.

Character Rules: One person only. Prioritize faithfully recreating the facial features, hairstyle, outfit colors, and overall silhouette from the reference image. Maintain natural facial structure. If the reference is illustrated or stylized, reinterpret as a realistic live-action person while preserving the face, hairstyle, outfit, and overall vibe.
Camera Rules: Camera switches only between full-body shots and above-the-knee shots. No close-ups of hands, feet, or body parts. First and last shots must be full-body. Avoid sudden zooms and rotating camera moves. Add subtle handheld camera shake and natural focus shifts to enhance the authentic MV feel.

Movement Rules: Dance movement stays fluid and continuous throughout the 15 seconds with no repeated moves. Every 0.5 seconds includes a clear weight shift or pose change. Movement sequence includes: footwork, body turns, shoulder groove, hip sway, natural arm swings, and hair flowing with movement. No big jumps or spins. Arm movements stay smooth and controlled. Opening follows the music rhythm, mid-section hits the most energetic peak, ending settles into a strong held pose.
Emotional Expression: Emotion is conveyed through eye movement, flowing hair, fabric shifting with motion, and natural light reflection — no exaggerated facial expressions. A balance of cute and cool, natural and grounded, suitable for TikTok short-form content.

Environment & Lighting: Background complements the dance style without overpowering it. Lighting is soft and natural, evenly illuminating the face and body, avoiding overexposure and harsh shadows. No CG effects, artificial skin texture, or plastic appearance.
Overall Constraints: Face remains stable and undistorted, features stay sharp, body proportions normal, movement fluid and never stiff, no filters or color shifts at any point, absolutely no subtitles, text, watermarks, or logos, no frame stuttering or visual glitches.
