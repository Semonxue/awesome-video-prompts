Shot 1 (0s–2s) —
Ultra-wide cinematic storm shot.

A gigantic offshore oil rig burns violently in the middle of the ocean at night.

Suddenly —

BOOM.

One of the massive support pillars EXPLODES upward from below the waterline.

The entire rig tilts hard as workers are thrown sideways.

Something enormous moves beneath the flaming water.

Shot 2 (2s–5s)
Handheld chaos on the rig.

Workers sprint through sparks, collapsing metal, and leaking fire.

Another underwater IMPACT shakes the structure.

A worker grabs a rocket launcher from an emergency weapons case.

Behind him:

the ocean surface BULGES upward fast.

Shot 3 (5s–8s)
The worker climbs a collapsing crane high above the burning rig while carrying the launcher.

Wind screaming.

Metal snapping.

Below him:

the Megalodon circles through flaming oil-covered water, its dorsal fin slicing through fire.

The crane begins collapsing sideways.

Shot 4 (8s–11s)
The Meg EXPLODES vertically out of the ocean through burning water directly toward the crane.

Its jaws open wide beneath the falling worker.

Mid-air chaos.

The worker hangs one-handed from the crane and aims the rocket launcher downward while falling.

Shot 5 (11s–14s)
ROCKET FIRES.

The rocket streaks directly into the Megalodon's throat.

Half-second silence.

Then A MASSIVE INTERNAL EXPLOSION erupts inside the shark's body mid-air.

Fire bursts outward through its mouth and gills.
The shockwave blasts water and debris across the rig.

Shot 6 (14s–15s) — PAYOFF
Ultra-wide blockbuster shot.

The burning Megalodon crashes sideways into the ocean beside the rig, sending up a gigantic flaming tidal wave.

The oil rig survives but continues collapsing in sections from the shockwave.

Workers cling to railings as burning debris rains down while the dead Meg slowly sinks beneath the fire-covered ocean surface.

Key Visual Hook
The oil rig support pillar exploding upward from beneath the ocean while something massive moves below the burning water.

Every shot escalates in scale — collapsing industrial structures, flaming ocean water, airborne Meg attacks, and a massive fireball explosion create pure $ 300M disaster-movie energy.
