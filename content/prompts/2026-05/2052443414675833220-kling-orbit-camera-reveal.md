Prompt:
The shot opens at ground level, camera positioned frontally on @ character's feet. When the orbit completes its full 360° and returns exactly to the frontal position, the camera has reached face level and performs a slow gentle zoom out, framing @ character in their full environment. Camera holds. [Epic golden hour light / cold stormy sky], shallow depth of field opening into deep focus, cinematic grandeur.
