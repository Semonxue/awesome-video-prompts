First frame uses the reference image.
Realistic live Japanese baseball broadcast crowd cutaway during a fictional playoff game in a packed stadium. A couple sits together among the audience in soft-blue Y2K summer outfits.

Single continuous take. No cuts. No angle changes.

0–3s:
The camera lands on the couple naturally. The boyfriend is watching the game and cheering. The girl looks slightly nervous, glancing at him then reaching into her lap.

3–7s:
The girl pulls out a small ring box and opens it toward the boyfriend. He turns to look at her, sees the ring, and his jaw drops in total disbelief. He freezes for a second, looking stunned and overwhelmed with emotion.

7–11s:
The girl speaks (visual only) with a wide, teary-eyed smile. The boyfriend breaks into a massive, joyful grin and nods his head "Yes" vigorously. Nearby spectators begin to point, cheer, and clap as they realize what is happening on the stadium big screen.

11–13s:
The boyfriend pulls her into a warm, tight hug, laughing with joy. The girl leans her head on his shoulder, looking incredibly happy. The movement remains realistic and grounded.

13–15s:
The couple stays in a happy embrace, ignored by the camera but clearly the center of the broadcast moment. The boyfriend looks back at the ring in her hand, still grinning. Both remain seated the entire time.
