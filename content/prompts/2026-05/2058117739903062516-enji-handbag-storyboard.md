Create a clean, premium storyboard poster for a 10-second fashion advertisement featuring the "En-Ji Collection" handbag. The layout should look like a professional commercial production storyboard with organized cinematic sections and a luxurious feminine aesthetic. Use a soft beige, cream, white, and gold color palette with elegant typography and minimal modern design. The storyboard is divided into six horizontal scenes with columns for Time, Visual/Shot, Visual Description, Camera Movement & Action, Voice Over, and Audio/Text. Include realistic cinematic visuals of a stylish young woman in a bright luxury interior placing and carrying a premium cream-colored handbag with gold hardware and heart-shaped charms. Add close-up product shots showing elegant details like zippers, logo plate, leather texture, and accessories. The lighting should feel warm, soft, airy, and high-end with shallow depth of field and luxury fashion commercial vibes. Include smooth camera directions like slow push-in, slight zoom-in, pull-back movement, and cinematic detail montage transitions. Add elegant English voice-over lines such as "Elegance that speaks in every moment" and "Every detail is crafted to perfection." Finish with a final luxury hero shot of the handbag and a clean brand logo screen for 'En-Ji' Collection" with the tagline "Elegance in Every Detail." The entire storyboard should resemble a polished advertising agency presentation board for a premium feminine fashion brand.
