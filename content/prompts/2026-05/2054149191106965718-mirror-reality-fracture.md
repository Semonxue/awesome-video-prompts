A cinematic sci-fi thriller where reality fractures through mirrors across the world.

0–3s: Quiet modern city morning. A person stands in a bathroom staring into a mirror — but the reflection blinks a fraction too late. Subtle digital distortion ripples across the glass.

3–6s: Cut to multiple locations worldwide — homes, malls, streets — mirrors flicker simultaneously. Reflections begin showing different versions of reality: burning cities, ocean-filled streets, floating architecture.

6–9s: Close-up on a cracked mirror surface. A hand reaches toward it — the reflection shows an alternate version of the same person wearing futuristic armor in a war-torn neon battlefield. The mirror glitches violently as the hand passes through the glass — but instead of touching the reflection's palm, the person is grabbed by it. The screen freezes on the moment their fingers touch.

9–12s: High-speed sequence. In rapid succession, mirrors in different locations shatter outward in slow motion, each revealing a portal to another dimension. People on both sides of the mirrors reach toward each other, screaming silently.

12–15s: Pull back to reveal thousands of mirrors across a vast futuristic cityscape, all flickering and glitching, projecting infinite versions of the world simultaneously. The sky cracks like broken glass and reality itself folds inward.
