Key words: Character consistency: same girl, same school uniform, same face. A Marvel-style telekinetic action scene takes place in a wide, brightly lit outdoor cobblestone plaza, with wide stone steps and green belts in the background. The girl stands in the center of the empty plaza in a medium shot. The camera begins with an extreme close-up of the girl's cold, aloof face, then rapidly pulls back and climbs steadily into the air, eventually evolving into a grand aerial shot (bird's-eye view/drone shot), showcasing an astonishing scale. Without any physical movement, the girl unleashes devastating power purely through telekinesis. Initially, tiny cracks and tremors appear in the ground beneath her feet; then, a violent circular shockwave erupts instantly from her center. The thick stone ground collapses and shatters violently like a spiderweb, with huge chunks of concrete being hurled into the air by an invisible force. A massive circular blast of air, composed of sand, dust, and debris, rises into the sky and spreads violently outward, blasting a huge crater in the plaza. Amidst this cataclysmic destruction, the girl, like the eye of the storm, stood unharmed and utterly calm on the only intact piece of ground in the center of the crater, her hands hanging naturally at her sides.
