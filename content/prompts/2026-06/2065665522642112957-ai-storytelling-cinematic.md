Use the reference storyboard image as the main visual and motion guide. Create a short cinematic animated cooking video with two fully original chefs in a warm professional kitchen.

IMPORTANT DIRECTION: Follow the storyboard sequence closely. Match the action order, camera progression, and movement flow from the reference. Keep the characters original and consistent across the full video.

DO NOT USE: Copyrighted characters, Movie references, Franchise styles, Brand logos, Recognizable IP.

VIDEO STYLE: Original high-end 3D animated film look, Warm kitchen lighting, Smooth cinematic motion, Fun energetic performance, Clean readable action, Natural food fire steam herbs and kitchen details, Light magical accents and rhythmic motion.

MAIN ACTION: The main chef is playful, athletic, and expressive. He cooks with fun gymnastics and music-synced movement. The supporting chef is warm, grounded, and joins the rhythm.

SHOT FLOW:
1. CLOSE-UP: Start with a close-up of hands chopping vegetables on the beat.
2. MEDIUM SHOT: Show the main chef lightly bouncing and prepping ingredients in rhythm.
3. WIDE SHOT: Show a wide shot of the chef doing a playful gymnastic vault across the kitchen workspace.
4. CLOSE-UP: Show a close-up of ingredients tossed upward with magical spark-like trails.
5. LOW-ANGLE SHOT: Show a low-angle shot of the chef spinning into place at the stove with the pan.
6. CLOSE-UP: Show a close-up of the pan igniting with flame on a strong beat.
7. DYNAMIC WIDE SHOT: Show a dynamic wide shot of the chef flipping food during an acrobatic move.
8. MEDIUM TWO-SHOT: Show a medium two-shot where the supporting chef joins and both chefs cook in sync.
9. SIDE SHOT: Show a side shot of the main chef sliding into position and presenting the plate with style.
10. CLOSE-UP: Show a close-up of final garnish dropping onto the dish with magical sparkle accents.
11. HERO MEDIUM SHOT: Show a hero medium shot of the chef landing in a confident final plating pose.
12. FINAL WIDE SHOT: End with a wide shot of both chefs hitting a fun final pose as steam, herbs, and magical energy settle.

CHARACTER RULES: Two original chefs only.
MAIN CHEF: Slim young male chef, Athletic, Expressive, Playful, Agile.
SUPPORTING CHEF: Slightly older male chef, Broader build, Kind, Grounded, Supportive.
