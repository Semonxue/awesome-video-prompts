Create a cinematic realistic single-take beach volleyball video using the reference image as the exact scene layout and a strict hidden camera-path guide. The camera must follow the hidden red line exactly in order from 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5, but the red line must never appear in the final video. The video starts with the blonde woman in red already diving slowly through the air toward the ball, still moving forward as if trying desperately to save it. 
Use this motion pattern for each speaking beat: 
fly in → close-up → line → pull back → next character. Player 1 says with intense effort and determination, 
“I can reach it!” 
Player 2, her red-team teammate, looks excited and hopeful and says, “Come on!” 
Player 3, the blue-team opponent, looks pleased and confident and says, “Too late.” 
Player 4, the other blue-team opponent, looks happy and sure she will miss and says, “We got it!” 
Player 5, the female referee, looks firm, extends her arm, and says, “Point!” 
Then return to a wide shot: the ball is on the sand, the blonde player has fallen and missed the save, the red team is disappointed, and the blue team is celebrating. No subtitles. The red drawn line must not appear in the video; it is only a hidden flight-path reference.
