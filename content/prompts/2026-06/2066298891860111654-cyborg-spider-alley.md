SUBJECTS: Protagonist: A female cyborg wearing a tattered leather trench coat, glowing optical visor, and heavy hydraulic boots, possessing plasma-whip powers. Scrap-Spider: A massive arachnid constructed entirely from discarded city scrap, flickering neon tubes, and sparking cables.
ENVIRONMENT: A dystopian megacity under-level. The main location remains a single, narrow, vertical alleyway: background towering brutalist skyscrapers, massive holographic advertisements, and streams of flying traffic; midground rusted fire escapes, flickering neon shop signs, overflowing dumpsters, and industrial steam vents; foreground oil-slicked asphalt, shattered concrete, glowing puddles, and tangled cables. Toxic rain pours steadily throughout.
MUSIC: Heavy synthwave basslines, electronic glitches, driving industrial beats, continuous rain sounds.
STYLE: 90s Cyberpunk Anime aesthetic. High-contrast neon lighting (cyan and magenta), cel-shaded edges, gritty textures, heavy film grain, retaining hand-drawn 2D animation traces. No clean 3D rendering, no modern hyper-realism.
