MASTER PROMPT

Global Intent: High-fashion western aesthetic merging haute couture with wild desert nature. 35mm film grain, harsh sun. Color palette: burnt orange, deep black, blood-red silk, shiny gold. Gritty, energetic vibe with realistic physics and authentic textures.

SEQUENCE LIST:

SHOT 1 (0-1s) Extreme Macro - Heavy blood-red silk drags slowly across dry, cracked desert earth. 35mm film grain highlights individual sand particles. Complex cloth simulation, realistic material friction.

SHOT 2 (1-2s) Wide Shot - A female model in an intricate, avant-garde black couture outfit stands atop a massive sand dune. The wind aggressively billows her heavy fabric clothing.

SHOT 3 (2-3.5s) Extreme Close-Up - Hyper-realistic human skin texture with visible pores, glistening with sweat under harsh sunlight. A single bead of sweat rolls slowly down her temple.

SHOT 4 (3.5-4.5s) Close-Up - Hands, fingers completely covered in massive gold rings, sharply snap open a black vintage wooden hand fan with energetic movement.

SHOT 5 (4.5-5.5s) Medium Shot - A wild black mustang horse with no saddle rears up sharply on its hind legs directly in front of the camera.

SHOT 6 (5.5-7s) Extreme Macro - Blood-red silk fabric flaps aggressively in the strong wind, completely covering the lens in pure red color.

SHOT 7 (7-9s) Medium Shot - Split-screen visual effect showing three horizontal frames simultaneously. Top frame: the horse's eye. Center frame: the woman's dark lips. Bottom frame: the shiny gold rings.

SHOT 8 (9-11s) Medium Shot - The female model executes a sharp, rapid spin. Motion blur creates the visual effect of a dark vortex.

SHOT 9 (11-13s) Extreme Close-Up - Lips painted with perfect dark lipstick press firmly against a shiny gold coin pendant.

SHOT 10 (13-15s) Close-Up - The woman looks directly into the lens, giving a subtle, defiant upward tilt of her chin. Hard directional sunlight casts dramatic, harsh shadows across her face. Abrupt cut to pure black frame.
