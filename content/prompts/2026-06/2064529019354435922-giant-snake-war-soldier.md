A giant python, thick as a fuel truck, has snatched a soldier off a jungle riverbank and is dragging him through the mud toward deep water in bright humid daylight. Churned riverbank, scattered weapons, hovering military helicopter, towering green jungle. Sound: rotor thunder, screaming, coiling muscle, minigun spin-up, radio chatter.

Shot 1 (0–3s) — THE HOOK: Ground-level tracking shot. The python's coils are wrapped around the soldier's legs, hauling him backward through the mud as he claws at roots and fires his pistol into the scales — no effect. His squad sprints along the bank, unable to get a clean shot. He screams into his radio: "IT'S TAKING ME UNDER! DO SOMETHING!"

Shot 2 (3–6s): Aerial shot. The squad's helicopter swings low over the river, rotor wash flattening the reeds, and drops the rescue winch — the steel hook swinging down fast. Crew chief leaning out the door: "HOOK'S DOWN! CLIP THE COIL! CLIP THE COIL!"

Shot 3 (6–9s): Tight handheld on the soldier. Half in the water, he grabs the swinging hook and slams it shut around the python's coil at his leg, locking steel through muscle-thick scale. He looks up at the chopper and roars: "PULL! PULL NOW!"

Shot 4 (9–13s) — BULLET TIME PAYOFF: The helicopter hauls skyward. Bullet time — the python is stretched off the ground full length, an impossible hanging cable of muscle between the straining chopper and the river, the soldier dangling from its lower coils, water streaming off in suspended droplets — and the door gunner opens up, the minigun's tracer stream sawing across the stretched body in slow motion, scales and red mist erupting in a line until the python rips in half.

Shot 5 (13–15s): Time snaps back. Both halves of the monster plummet and detonate the river surface in two huge splashes. The soldier drops free, plunges in feet-first between them, and surfaces in the settling foam, fist up, screaming in victory. The chopper circles overhead, gunner saluting.
