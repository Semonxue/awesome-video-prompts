Use the attached THE MURALIST storyboard image as the exact reference.
Create a 12-second 16:9 animated mural-painting sequence that follows the 8-shot storyboard exactly. Preserve the same Pixar-style energetic young male muralist, paint-splattered overalls, bandana, large city wall, and bright urban outdoor aesthetic throughout.
Rules:
→ Follow the sequence exactly from 1 to 8
→ One shot per panel, approximately 1.5 seconds each
→ No skipped steps, no extra props beyond the storyboard
→ Maintain character and city wall continuity throughout
→ Emphasize the bold base color sweep, figure painting energy, detail linework, and final crowd awe reaction
Shot sequence:
→ Muralist faces blank wall — eyes wide, creative vision sparking dramatically
→ Marker flies across paper thumbnail — bold confident composition forming fast
→ Chalk grid drawn on wall — design scaled up with precision and urban energy
→ Large roller applies bold background — sweeping dramatic color strokes across wall
→ Large brushstrokes bring central figures alive — bold, expressive, powerful
→ Fine brush adds crisp outlines and expressions — intricate detail layers building
→ Brightest accent colors added — mural suddenly vibrating and alive with energy
→ Muralist steps back — crowd has gathered, everyone staring up in pure awe
Camera:
→ Wide shot for blank wall discovery and final crowd awe reveal
→ Close-up for thumbnail sketch, chalk grid, and fine detail brush work
→ Dramatic low angle for large roller base color sweep
→ Slow pull-back wide shot for final mural complete crowd reaction
Style:
→ Bright outdoor daylight throughout
→ Electric cobalt blues, vibrant sunflower yellows, deep magenta pinks, bold jungle greens, crisp whites
→ Pixar CGI vivid expressive explosive animation
→ Shallow depth of field on close-up brush and detail shots
→ Smooth energetic cuts, bold and electrically alive creative energy
Goal: A beautiful 12-second mural journey from blank city wall to a crowd-stopping living masterpiece — bold, expressive, and deeply alive to watch.
