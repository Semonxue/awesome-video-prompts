Zero to hero[Character Design] Zara Kovac: 33 years old, highly muscular female, blood-red mohawk with shaved sides, strong jawline, cross-shaped scar on right cheek, dark circles under eyes. Wearing a black leather jacket with white shearling collar, white "THE EXPLOITED" punk shirt, ripped black jeans, black military boots with red laces. [Scenario, Lighting & Effects] Rattling cargo train car, rusted steel walls, greasy wood floors, wooden crates with Cyrillic text, oil drums, swinging ceiling chains. Single bare lightbulbs swaying and flickering. High-contrast stroboscopic lighting. Dynamic fluid simulations for blood splatters, soft-body swinging cables, and 2-frame intense white muzzle flashes. [Temporal Cuts & Action Sequence] - 0-3s [Cut 1 - Wide Shot]: Tense wide shot inside the swaying cargo train. Two armed guards search between crates. Zara is crouching on the right behind an oil drum, tightening her leather gloves. Handheld camera shake mimicking train rumble. - 3-5s [Cut 2 - Close-up on Boots]: Sharp close-up of Zara's combat boot. She pulls out a tactical knife. The blade glints sharply under a flickering, swinging overhead lightbulb. - 5-7s [Cut 3 - Medium Action Shot]: Zara leaps out from cover, spins, and throws the knife. Simulated 30% slow-motion on the knife spinning through the air. - 7-9s [Cut 4 - Impact Close-up]: The knife strikes the first enemy's shoulder. He screams and falls backward into a stack of boxes. Instantly cuts back to Zara sprinting toward the second guard, pulling her second knife. - 9-11s [Cut 5 - Tight Action Combat]: The second guard raises his pistol. Zara grabs his wrist, forcing the barrel to point to the ceiling. The gun fires, creating an explosive muzzle flash that lights up the frame. She stabs his forearm, disarming him. - 11-13s [Cut 6 - Whip Pan & Slam]: She delivers a heavy knee to his midsection, grabs his collar, and throws him against the steel wall. Camera whip-pans rapidly following the throw. - 13-15s [Cut 7 - Final Close-up & Title Card]: Zara stands over him, panting. She looks at the camera with raw fury, wiping blood from her cheek with the back of her hand. She grabs the fallen gun, tucks it in her belt. The train brakes screech violently, lights go out, and the screen cuts to black. Red graffiti style text "ZARA" spray-painted on steel fades in. [Camera & Physics Control] High-energy shaky-cam and whip pans to sell the physical impact. Maintain perfect character model continuity across all cuts. Flawless simulation of cloth, hair, swinging chains, and fluid blood splatters. in one cinematic sequence
