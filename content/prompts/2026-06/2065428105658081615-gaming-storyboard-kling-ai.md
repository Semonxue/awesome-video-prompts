Treat each frame as a separate sequential scene. Animate every frame one by one from left to right, top to bottom. Start from frame 1 top-left and end with frame 8 bottom-right. Do not skip any frame. Do not reorder any frame. Do not add any new frame. Play each frame in strict order as one continuous uninterrupted cinematic video of exactly 15 seconds total duration, giving each frame equal time of 1.875 seconds. Use bored face lit by cold phone screen in dark room in real time, game launch screen exploding with neon light in slow motion, first level portal opening with electric cyan flash, intense gameplay with fingers blurring over screen in macro, near death screen glowing red with heartbeat visible, final boss fight with purple neon light strobing, victory screen explosion with confetti in slow motion, real world fist pump with phone raised in electric room light. Gen Z gaming aesthetic, total immersion and pure adrenaline mood, phone as portal to infinite worlds. A single phone screen glowing in dark room remains the emotional focus across all 8 frames. Ultra-realistic, 4K photographic quality, no cartoon style.
