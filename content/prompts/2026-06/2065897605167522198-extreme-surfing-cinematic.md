Create a hyper-cinematic 15-second continuous single-take extreme surfing video.

REFERENCE USAGE:
Image 1 = strict identity, outfit, and surfboard reference. Preserve her exact face, pale skin, blue eyes, long straight black hair, body proportions, feminine surf outfit, and the same surfboard design. Do NOT change her identity, outfit, or surfboard. She must feel like a real person performing dangerous but believable professional surfing action.

SETTING:
Open ocean in bright daylight, deep blue water, strong sunlight reflections, rising waves, sea spray, and a powerful cinematic atmosphere. The environment should feel fast, raw, thrilling, and realistic.

VIDEO STYLE:
One continuous 15-second shot, absolutely no cuts, no hidden cuts, no transitions, and no montage. Hyper-realistic action, natural motion blur, water droplets on lens, sea spray, rushing wave sound, board skimming noise, wind noise, and believable body-weight corrections. The shot must feel immersive and physically real.

OPENING SHOT:
She is already surfing when the video begins. The camera starts very close in front of her, focused mainly on her upper body and face. Her wet black hair moves in the wind, her expression is calm, fearless, and focused, and the water moves dynamically beneath her board.

CAMERA MOVEMENT:
The camera begins briefly in front of her upper body, then in one smooth uninterrupted move swings around her body to the back. As soon as it reaches behind her, it drops low to surfboard level and stays there for the rest of the entire video. From that moment on, the camera must always remain very close to the surfboard, low near the water surface, following from behind and slightly below or slightly offset near the tail and rail. It must never rise to a high angle. It should always feel like it is chasing the surfboard from behind at board height.

ACTION FLOW:
Once the camera settles behind her at surfboard level, she lowers into a deep crouch and accelerates hard across the water. She leans forward aggressively, keeps her balance with athletic precision, and reaches one hand down so her fingertips touch and skim the water, throwing spray backward toward the low camera. The board slices fast through the surface while the camera stays locked close behind it.

She then builds more speed toward a stronger wave section and performs an acrobatic aerial move. She launches off the wave with the surfboard beneath her feet, and the entire jump remains in the same continuous shot with no cut. During the airborne moment, time shifts into dramatic slow motion while the camera still stays low, close, and near surfboard level, following from behind and slightly underneath the action. While in the air, she rotates her upper body toward the camera, faces us, and grabs the surfboard with one hand, completing the aerial in a stylish and impressive pose. Her hair lifts in the wind, droplets hang in the air, and the board remains clearly visible.

She then completes the motion, rotates back naturally, and lands cleanly on the wave with a hard burst of spray. The camera remains low behind the surfboard, still at board height, and follows the impact closely. After landing, she stays crouched, keeps one smooth line of motion, and accelerates again toward the rising waves ahead.

FINAL MOMENT:
She continues powering forward at high speed, still viewed from the same uninterrupted low trailing angle behind the surfboard, with spray exploding around the lens and the wave face growing larger ahead. The video ends while she is still charging forward in the same continuous single take. No text, no logos, no subtitles.
