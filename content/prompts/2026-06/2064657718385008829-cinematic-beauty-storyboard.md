Create a 15-second cinematic beauty product commercial storyboard video for a luxury eyeshadow / blush compact.

Visual style: high-end Korean beauty campaign, minimalist editorial aesthetic, cool gray studio background, soft diffused lighting, glossy skin texture, delicate pink makeup tones, clean shadows, premium cosmetic advertising look.

Main subject: young Asian female model with smooth natural skin, black hair, soft pink eyeshadow, subtle blush, glossy lips, elegant calm expression. Keep the same face, hairstyle, makeup style, and identity consistent across all shots.

Product: small transparent acrylic square compact with two soft pink / nude powder pans. The product should appear premium, clean, glossy, and sculptural.

Video structure: 12-shot commercial storyboard layout, with a large main preview frame on top and smaller storyboard thumbnails at the bottom. Each thumbnail has a cinematic label and technical notes like camera lens, SFX, and music mood. The whole video should feel like a professional creative director’s beauty ad storyboard.

Shot sequence:

Extreme macro close-up of closed eyelid with shimmering pink eyeshadow, visible eyelashes, natural skin texture.
Top-down flat lay of the transparent cosmetic compact on a textured gray surface, soft shadow.
Slow-motion powder destruction shot, pink makeup powder exploding and floating in the air, dust burst, black background.
High-angle beauty portrait, model’s black hair sweeping across her face, minimal expression.
Macro texture scrape of pink powder inside the compact, detailed product texture.
Abstract motion blur shot, cosmetic color sweeping across the frame.
Model’s eye seen through the transparent compact lid, refracted glass effect.
Side profile silhouette with strong graphic shadow on the wall, elegant beauty pose.
Surreal product contact shot, compact gently touching the model’s eyelid or cheek, macro skin detail.
Three-quarter close-up portrait, model holding the compact beside her face, soft click gesture.
Low-angle confident beauty portrait, model looking down at camera, cinematic attitude.
Final sculptural hero shot of the transparent compact standing on a clean pedestal, premium product finish.

Camera language: macro 100mm lens, 85mm beauty portrait lens, top-shot flat lay, low-angle 35mm, shallow depth of field, slow motion, polished commercial camera movement.

Motion style: slow elegant cuts, subtle zoom-ins, soft hair movement, powder burst in slow motion, product rotation, glass reflection, cinematic motion blur.

Lighting: soft studio light, cool gray tones, gentle highlights on skin, glossy reflections on acrylic packaging, controlled shadows.

Mood: minimal, sensual, premium, artistic, experimental, editorial beauty film.

Negative prompt: no messy background, no distorted face, no extra fingers, no broken product shape, no inconsistent model identity, no harsh over-smoothing, no plastic skin, no cheap packaging, no unreadable text, no logo distortion.
