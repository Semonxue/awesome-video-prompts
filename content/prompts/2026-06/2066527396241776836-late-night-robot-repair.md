3D animated cinematic short, high-quality stylized realism.
No BGM, no dialogue, no subtitles, no text, with ambient sounds.

# Constraints
Vary the framing and angles in every cut to avoid monotony.
Focus on tiny mechanical details, warm desk lighting, careful hands, sparks, gears, and emotional repair.
No violent robot action.

# Character
Teen inventor.
Slim build, tired eyes, messy hair, oversized hoodie, fingerless gloves.
Gentle, focused, emotionally attached to the small robot.

# Secondary subject
Tiny broken robot.
Palm-sized, round body, small arms, cracked lens eye, weak flickering chest light.

# Setting
Messy bedroom workshop at night.
Desk covered with tools, wires, screws, batteries, notebooks, and a warm desk lamp.
Rain and city lights visible through the window.

# Scene
The teen repairs their tiny robot companion late at night.
Alternate between tools, hands, robot reactions, sparks, and emotional payoff.

cut1:
Wide shot from bedroom doorway.
The teen sits at a cluttered desk under a warm lamp, hunched over the broken tiny robot.
Ambient sounds: Rain on window, soft electrical hum, chair creak.

cut2:
Close-up, overhead.
The tiny robot lies open on a soft cloth, chest light flickering weakly.
Ambient sounds: Weak electronic beep, tiny servo twitch.

cut3:
Macro close-up.
Tweezers lift a tiny gear and place it back into the robot’s chest.
Ambient sounds: Metal click, soft tool movement.

cut4:
Low side angle.
The teen tightens a microscopic screw, face blurred in the background.
Ambient sounds: Screwdriver turn, quiet breathing.

cut5:
Close-up of the teen’s eyes.
They blink slowly, tired but hopeful, reflected robot light glowing in their pupils.
Ambient sounds: Rain, faint electric pulse.

cut6:
Extreme close-up.
Two wires touch, creating a tiny blue spark.
The robot’s chest light grows stronger.
Ambient sounds: Electric snap, rising beep.

cut7:
Medium shot.
The teen gently closes the robot’s chest panel and waits.
The robot remains still for one tense moment.
Ambient sounds: Silence, rain, tiny mechanical tick.

cut8:

cut9:
Overhead shot.

Ambient sounds: Cloth rustle, robot chirp.

cut10:
Final close-up.
The robot sits upright on the desk beside scattered tools, chest light glowing warmly while rain reflects in the window behind it.
Ambient sounds: Gentle robot hum, rain, peaceful room tone.
