Single Continuous Shot

Realistic body-cam footage from an armed soldier strapped into the open side door of a military helicopter. Raw body-cam style, slight grain, violent camera shake, bright daylight over rough open ocean. One continuous unbroken shot. No cuts.

The shot begins in pure chaos.

The helicopter is already circling a gigantic container ship listing heavily to one side in rough seas. Shipping containers are scattered across the deck. Crew members are already running in every direction.

The reason becomes immediately clear.

A colossal ocean hybrid is already attached to the ship.

The creature has the armored head and massive jaws of a prehistoric mega-crocodile combined with enormous squid-like tentacles covered in suction cups. Its jaws are clamped into the side of the ship, twisting steel inward like soft metal.

The soldier is already firing a heavy machine gun.

Bright muzzle flashes illuminate the helicopter doorway.

Tracer rounds strike the creature’s thick scales.

No visible effect.

The helicopter banks lower.

The camera shakes violently.

Below, the creature jerks its head sideways.

The entire ship lurches.

Rows of containers slide across the deck.

Several break loose and crash into the ocean.

Crew members scramble away as containers skid past.

The hybrid releases one bite and immediately bites down again farther along the hull.

Metal buckles.

The ship tilts even harder.

The helicopter circles rapidly around the vessel, keeping both the creature and collapsing deck in view.

The soldier continues firing.

The creature responds by raising several massive tentacles above the deck.

One wraps around a shipping container.

Another tentacle smashes into the side of the ship, sending debris flying.

The helicopter swings around for another pass.

The camera tracks the creature.

Then—

A tentacle suddenly hurls the container upward.

The container spins through the air directly toward the helicopter.

The soldier barely reacts.

The container fills the entire frame.

Impact.

Half a second of distorted static.

Hard cut to black.

Key Visual Hook

A giant crocodile-squid hybrid already biting into a container ship while containers slide and fall into the ocean.
