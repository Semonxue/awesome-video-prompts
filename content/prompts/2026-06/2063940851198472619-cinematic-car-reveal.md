Scene: clean modern parking lot / driveway, smooth concrete, soft daylight, minimal background, premium tech-commercial feel. Realistic hands visible. A guy holds a sleek matte-black collector box with the reference car as a tiny die-cast toy inside.

0–2s:

2–4s:

4–6s:
