Style & Character:
Cinematic realistic Wuxia demon-hunting style, reference the female protagonist from Image 1.
Scene & Action Description:
Wide shot, slow dolly out. Deep inside a dim, damp, and mist-shrouded cave. The female protagonist holds a wooden sword, stepping out slowly from the shadows of the cave, her expression solemn and calm. Low echoes resonate within the cave, the sound of dripping water intertwining with the wind, creating an oppressive and mysterious atmosphere.
Cut to close-up (Static). She suddenly senses something amiss; her pupils shrink, and her eyes snap open. She rapidly raises her hands into a combat stance. Her aura instantly condenses, and the air seems to freeze.
Wide shot, low angle, slight tilt up. From a crevice in the cave's ceiling, a mass of demonic energy—interweaving dark red and pitch black—plummets like a meteor, diving down with an intense, oppressive force. Red and black smoke churns, while the monster's low roar echoes against the rock walls.
Medium shot, fast tracking. The demonic energy smashes into the ground and instantly explodes, the shockwave shattering loose rocks. The female protagonist retreats rapidly, leaping backward into the air to evade the fatal strike.
Wide shot tracking the subject, entering slow motion. She performs a graceful one-and-a-half backflip in mid-air, her garments fluttering, her posture elegant like a crane. The sound of the wind is drawn out. Her movements are clear and fierce, showcasing ultimate control of Qinggong (lightness skill).
Camera tilts down to follow the landing. She lands steadily in a single-leg bow stance. Her left hand flips out a talisman and instantly slaps it onto the ground. The talisman paper resonates, and magical ripples spread across the ground, causing the cave dust to tremble slightly.
Wide shot, fast pan. The red and black demonic energy circles in the air and dives down again at high speed—faster than before—the wind whistling like a knife slicing through the air.
Medium shot, static. As the demonic energy closes in, it gradually condenses. The smoke gathers into a humanoid silhouette, revealing a ferocious dark shadow monster with blood-red eyes, brandishing its claws.
Profile wide shot. The female protagonist stands up straight. Beneath her feet, a glowing, golden Tai Chi Bagua magic array instantly deploys, its runic patterns flowing. She points her wooden sword directly at the monster. Human and monster face off, their immense auras clashing.
Sudden whip pan. The monster roars, and its red demonic energy transforms into a whip-like beam that shoots out at extreme speed, tearing through the air.
Medium close-up, static. The red demonic energy explodes around her into circular halos, wrapping her layer by layer. The female protagonist stands with one hand held in front of her chest, two fingers forming a Taoist hand seal (mudra). Her aura is drawn inward, with a faint golden light shimmering around her body.
Close-up. Her eyes are as sharp as a sword, and her pupils ignite with a golden light. She shouts in a low voice:
"Break!"
Wide shot. The red demonic halos instantly shatter. The energy backlashes and disperses. The dark shadow monster lets out a piercing shriek as the smoke collapses and scatters into the cave. The glowing light of the golden array gradually retracts. The cave returns to dead silence, leaving only echoes and lingering smoke.
Medium shot. The female protagonist slowly lowers her wooden sword, her gaze icy. She turns and walks deeper into the cave.
