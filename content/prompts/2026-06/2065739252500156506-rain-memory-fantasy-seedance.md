A 15-second cinematic fantasy short film. A reference-image-based prompt designed for sharing. Use only one reference image. No text, no logos, no watermark.

The camera begins close to the curved glass and slides quickly around the bowl, showing the reflections, distortions, depth, and sparkling highlights of the transparent glass in an impressive way.

Water rises upward from the bottom in spiraling motion, tiny waves appear on the surface, white foam spreads, and underwater light shimmers through the glass.

The color palette must never look dull. It should be bright, crystal clear, transparent, and luxurious in appearance. Emphasize vivid aqua blue, turquoise, clear deep blue, transparent glass, sparkling highlights, clean bright light, and beautiful underwater reflections.
