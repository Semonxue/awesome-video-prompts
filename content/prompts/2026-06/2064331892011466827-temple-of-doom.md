Deep inside a lost rainforest temple, an athletic explorer is already fleeing for her life through collapsing ruins while a massive T-Rex smashes its way through the ancient structure behind her. Every trap originally built to stop intruders now becomes part of the chase. Stone bridges collapse, giant statues fall, ancient mechanisms activate, and a rescue helicopter races to reach her before she runs out of temple.
The audience should feel like they entered during the final minute of a huge adventure movie.
Shot 1 (0s–2s) — THE HOOK
Low handheld tracking shot running backward in front of the woman.
She is already sprinting across a narrow ancient stone bridge suspended high above a jungle canyon.
Behind her—
A MASSIVE T-Rex smashes through a giant temple wall.
Stone blocks explode outward.
Dust fills the air.
The bridge shakes violently beneath her feet.
Shot 2 (2s–5s)
Side tracking shot.
The woman leaps across missing sections of the bridge.
Behind her the T-Rex ignores the gaps and simply crashes through support pillars.
Ancient statues topple into the canyon.
Chunks of stone rain downward.
A rescue helicopter appears above the jungle canopy beyond the ruins.
Shot 3 (5s–7s)
Rear chase shot.
A giant swinging stone blade trap suddenly activates.
The woman ducks under it.
The T-Rex smashes straight through the mechanism.
Stone fragments explode everywhere.
The helicopter dives lower.
Rotor wash sends dust and jungle leaves flying.
The woman yells:
"I can't keep this up!"
Shot 4 (7s–9s)
Wide cinematic shot.
The bridge ends.
Ahead:
A gigantic temple courtyard suspended above a waterfall gorge.
The floor begins collapsing section by section.
The helicopter swings into position.
A crew member throws down a rescue rope.
Shot 5 (9s–12s) — BULLET TIME
Time slows dramatically.
The woman launches herself from the final collapsing stone platform.
The rope swings through sunlight and dust.
Broken temple debris hangs frozen in the air.
Behind her—
The T-Rex leaps through a collapsing stone archway.
Jaws wide open.
Shot 6 (12s–15s) — PAYOFF
She catches the rope.
The helicopter pulls upward.
She swings over the canyon.
The T-Rex misses by inches.
Its momentum carries it onto the collapsing temple edge.
Ancient stone gives way beneath it.
The dinosaur disappears into the mist-filled waterfall gorge below.
The woman swings beneath the helicopter, laughing in disbelief.
The helicopter climbs away above the jungle ruins.
Hard cut.
Key Visual Hook
A T-Rex exploding through an ancient temple wall while a survivor sprints across a collapsing stone bridge.
