Solar Sail Duel Above A Desert City
Two rival pilots race on glowing solar-sail skiffs above a sandstone megacity at sunset. The sails flare and fold as they slip between minarets, market balloons, and mirrored heat waves.
Path beats: bazaar rooftops → canyon street → palace domes → sandstorm wall → final skim over a glassy oasis.
Whale-Fall Chase In The Cloud Ocean
A first-person aerial pursuit through an ocean of clouds where enormous sky-whales drift between floating reefs. The chase target is a small bioluminescent courier creature.
Path beats: cloud reef → under a sky-whale belly → through glowing jellyfish swarms → dive into cloud tunnel → burst into sunrise.
Storm-Bell Sky Race
A high-speed chase through a floating city of bronze bell towers during a thunderstorm. Riders use winged gliders or magnetic boards, weaving through swinging cathedral bells as lightning reveals the route.
Path beats: rooftop launch → bell tower slalom → lightning bridge → dive through a giant swinging bell → finish above a rain-soaked plaza.
