“DRAGON INTERCEPT — OCEAN FIRESTORM”

Single Continuous Shot (15 Seconds)

Realistic body-cam footage from an armed soldier strapped into the open side door of a military helicopter. Raw body-cam style, slight digital grain, violent camera shake, bright daylight over a rough open ocean. One continuous unbroken shot. No cuts.

The shot begins in pure chaos.

The helicopter cabin is already FILLED with fire.

Orange flames roar through the open doorway, washing across the frame. Crew members duck instinctively as heat distortion warps the image. Warning alarms scream. The soldier stumbles backward while gripping a mounted machine gun.

The source is immediately visible.

A gigantic dragon is flying alongside the helicopter only a short distance away over the ocean. Massive wings beating against the air. Scales glinting in the sunlight. The dragon pulls away, then abruptly slides back toward the helicopter, weaving unpredictably through the sky.

The soldier opens fire.

Bright muzzle flashes erupt beside the camera.

Tracer rounds streak across the gap.

The dragon twists through the air with impossible agility, rolling sideways, dropping toward the ocean surface, then climbing again without losing speed.

The helicopter banks aggressively to stay alongside it.

Ocean waves blur beneath.

The dragon suddenly turns its head.

A massive stream of fire blasts across the helicopter’s path.

The entire frame becomes orange and white flame.

For a moment the camera sees almost nothing except fire and smoke.

Then the helicopter punches through.

The dragon emerges again ahead, skimming only a few feet above the waves.

The soldier continues firing.

The dragon sways left, right, then rolls almost completely upside down while maintaining forward speed.

The helicopter struggles to match the movement.

Then—

The ocean detonates upward.

A colossal Megalodon erupts from the water beneath the dragon.

The giant shark’s jaws snap shut around the dragon’s torso mid-flight.

The helicopter immediately banks hard and circles the spectacle.

The camera whips around the chaos as dragon and shark crash into the ocean together.

Water explodes hundreds of feet into the air.

The dragon disappears beneath the surface.

Massive turbulence and bubbles boil across the water.

The helicopter circles the disturbance.

Hard cut to black on rotor noise, gunfire, fire crackling, and churning ocean water.
