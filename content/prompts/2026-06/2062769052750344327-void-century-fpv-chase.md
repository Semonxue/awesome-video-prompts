Approach the massive Defense Sorus ring. Circle its perimeter at extreme velocity, execute barrel rolls, corkscrews, rapid altitude changes, and fly directly through its center before diving back into the city.

Cross the skyline as hundreds of flying vehicles move through layered aerial lanes. Waterfalls, floating gardens, energy conduits, anti-gravity platforms, suspended plazas, and luminous blue architecture rush past with intense motion blur.

Final sequence: descend through the ocean-facing district. Perform aggressive banking turns between towers and docking structures. Lock onto the cybernetic bird above the central palace balcony overlooking the ocean. Trigger a final boost and catch the target.
