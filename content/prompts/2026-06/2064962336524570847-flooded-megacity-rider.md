A lone motorbike rider races across a flooded megacity of tilted skyscrapers rising from a stormy ocean. Wet, broken rooftops and collapsing skybridges form a deadly path above crashing waves. Bright daylight cuts through dark storm clouds, reflecting off water, glass, and steel. The camera flows in one seamless shot starting with a wide aerial, then diving fast toward the rider. It snaps into tight tracking, skims the wet surface, whips sideways through sharp turns, rises overhead to reveal broken paths, then drops under structures for dramatic low angles. Midway, a massive wave smashes into buildings, triggering a chain collapse. The rider launches off a breaking edge, dropping onto a lower structure. The camera follows fluidly pulling wide, diving through debris and spray, twisting mid-air, then locking back into a close chase as the rider lands and accelerates. End with a wide pullback showing destruction behind and a narrow collapsing path ahead. Fast, intense, continuous, and cinematic.
