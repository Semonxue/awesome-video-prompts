Animate the uploaded storyboard image into one single continuous video. The storyboard has 12 frames arranged in a 3x4 grid. Read every frame strictly from left to right, top to bottom, starting from frame 1 top-left and ending at frame 12 bottom-right. Do not skip any frame. Do not reorder any frame. Do not add any new frame. Every frame must appear in the final video exactly as shown in the uploaded storyboard. Animate all 12 frames sequentially in one uninterrupted video of exactly 15 seconds total duration, giving each frame equal time of 1.25 seconds. Each frame must smoothly transition into the next with seamless continuity. Use slow silk fabric drift in wind, heel strike on cobblestone with subtle camera shake, breath mist visible in cold morning air, and shadow of couture dress travelling across marble floors. Lighting transitions from soft diffused cool morning light to warm Parisian golden hour with lens flare kissing fabric edges. Valentino and Bottega Veneta editorial aesthetic, controlled grace, elevated femininity mood. A single leather glove held delicately remains the emotional focus across all 12 frames. Ultra-realistic, 4K photographic quality, no cartoon style.
