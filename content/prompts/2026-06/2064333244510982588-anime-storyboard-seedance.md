TITLE: The Lost Wind Chime: Memories in the Wind

Use the uploaded storyboard and character sheet as the main visual reference. Follow the storyboard naturally. Maintain character, kitten, wind chime, porch, lighting, and environment consistency from the storyboard reference. Do not add characters.

STABILITY: prioritize consistency over motion. Avoid face/body morphing, proportion changes, clothing changes, kitten changes, object duplication, flicker, warped camera, reconstructed backgrounds, or style shifts.

SUBJECTS: Girl gentle, peaceful, reflective. Kitten tiny, fluffy, curious, playful, gradually sleepy. Wind chime delicate glass, nostalgic emotional centerpiece.

SETTING: Japanese countryside porch at blue-hour twilight, distant hills, lantern glow, moving grass, subtle fireflies, gentle breeze.

STYLE: premium anime film, painterly backgrounds, soft atmospheric lighting, natural acting, minimal motion, nostalgic peaceful mood. No text, subtitles, logos, or UI.

AUDIO: natural ambience only, soft wind, fading cicadas, delicate chime, distant night insects. No music.

CAMERA: 16:9 cinematic framing, slow push-ins, gentle pans, soft depth of field, smooth motion. No rapid cuts, sudden zooms, or aggressive perspective shifts.

ANIMATION: subtle motion only, blinking, breathing, light hair/dress movement, grass motion, slow fireflies, gentle chime sway. One main motion per shot.

TIMELINE:

0:00–0:03 Wide shot. Girl and kitten sit peacefully on the porch as twilight deepens. Sun nearly gone behind hills. Wind chime sways gently. SFX: wind, cicadas, chime.

0:03–0:05 Medium wide. Small fireflies appear near the grass and porch. Kitten notices them with subtle curiosity. Girl watches quietly. SFX: wind, distant insects.

0:05–0:07 Low gentle shot. Kitten watches and takes a few small steps toward one drifting firefly. Keep movement small and natural. Girl smiles softly. SFX: soft paws, ambience.

0:07–0:09 Close-up. Wind chime rings softly. Paper strip moves in the breeze. Only chime, strip, and a few fireflies move. SFX: glass chime, breeze.

0:09–0:11 Close emotional shot. Girl closes her eyes and listens. Hair and dress move slightly. Keep facial features stable. SFX: chime, wind.

0:11–0:13 Medium wide. A few fireflies drift slowly through the scene. Kitten settles beside girl, becoming sleepy. Lantern glow remains soft. SFX: insects, wind.

0:13–0:15 Final wide shot. Camera slowly pulls back. Girl and kitten sit peacefully on wooden porch under blue-hour twilight. Wind chime sways as a few fireflies drift. Mood nostalgic, magical, comforting. SFX: wind, night insects, chime.
