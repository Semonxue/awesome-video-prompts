Use the uploaded image as a route-planning terrain map.

The red line indicates the intended camera flight path and direction only. The red line, arrow, and all annotations must be completely removed from the final video.

Create a 15-second single continuous 16:9 cinematic FPV flight through the exact fantasy island city shown in the image.

No cuts.
No transitions.
No teleporting.
No map view.
Invisible first-person drone camera only.

Follow the route continuously from the bottom harbor entrance to the palace at the top of the island.

Timeline:

0s–3s

Fly forward through the illuminated city gate, passing close to docked ships, harbor towers, merchant buildings, lantern-lit bridges, waterfront streets, ropes, banners, and crowded port structures.

Stay close to foreground objects to create strong parallax.

3s–6s

Enter the lower city district.

Fly between dense fantasy buildings, market streets, balconies, rooftops, narrow alleys, glowing windows, taverns, shops, and interconnected stone walkways.

Bank naturally around corners while maintaining uninterrupted forward momentum.

6s–9s

Approach the massive circular city center.

Glide above the outer ring roads and enter the grand central plaza.

Pass over the illuminated fountain and circular gathering area.

Reveal the scale of the surrounding concentric city districts.

9s–12s

Continue climbing upward through the upper city.

Fly beside cliffside buildings, elevated terraces, bridges, waterfalls, towers, observatories, and the giant illuminated ferris wheel visible on the right side of the city.

Maintain smooth forward acceleration.

12s–15s

Ascend toward the palace district at the top of the island.

Pass through the final ceremonial approach and climb above the highest structures.

Finish with a dramatic cinematic reveal overlooking the entire fantasy island metropolis, surrounding ocean, waterfalls, harbor districts, bridges, towers, ferris wheel, and glowing city lights.
