Script 1: Glass Tide Atrium — Phase II (The Overload)
**Premise**: Directly following the guardian's collapse, its core goes critical, threatening to detonate the entire station. Astra and Nyx must combine their weapons to channel the energy before they are blown apart.

### Characters
- **Astra Vey**: Cyber duelist in a sleek white-and-silver advanced exosuit, violet accents, visor crown, wielding a plasma ribbon.
- **Nyx Calder**: Techno-huntress in a glossy black biomechanical bodysuit with cyan highlights, six blade-tipped spider-arms.
- **Threat**: The core of the fallen Colossus, pulsing in unstable orange and purple arcs, cracked, spewing superheated steam.

### Timeline & Shot Sequence

#### Act I (0:00 - 0:15) | The Crisis
- **Shot 1 (Wide)**: The fallen Colossus lies in the flooded atrium. Its violet core suddenly flashes violent orange-white. The water around it begins to boil, steam rising rapidly.
- **Shot 2 (Medium)**: Astra steps back, her visor flashing red warning symbols. She raises her arm to shield her face from the heat.
- **Astra**: *"The core's cascade-charging. It's going to level the block."*
- **Shot 3 (Medium Close-Up)**: Nyx leaps backward onto a hanging train rail. Her spider-arms anchor into the metal, cyan lights pulsing aggressively.
- **Nyx**: *"I'm not dying in a sewer, Vey. Ground it!"*
- **Shot 4 (Medium)**: Astra ignites her plasma ribbon, extending it into a glowing violet whip. She leaps toward the core.
#### Act II (0:15 - 0:30) | The Channeling

- **Shot 5 (Low Angle)**: Astra lashes the plasma ribbon around the boiling core. Violet arcs of energy shoot up the whip, lighting up her exosuit.
- **Shot 6 (Handheld)**: Astra struggles against the pull, her boots sliding through the boiling water. The steam forms a thick mist around her.
- **Shot 7 (Close-Up)**: Nyx looks down from the rail. Her eyes narrow. She launches three of her spider-arms down, piercing the concrete next to Astra.
- **Nyx**: *"Brace yourself."*
- **Shot 8 (Medium Tracking)**: Cyan energy flows from Nyx's suit down her spider-arms into the water, forming a localized magnetic field that stabilizes Astra's footing.
#### Act III (0:30 - 0:45) | The Release
- **Shot 9 (Dynamic Orbit)**: With her footing secured, Astra pulls the ribbon tight. The orange core flare shifts to violet-cyan as the energy is redirected.

- **Astra**: *"Now, Nyx! Discharge it!"*
- **Shot 10 (Close-Up)**: Nyx fires her forearm plasma bolt at the ceiling skylight, drawing the excess energy up from the core.
- **Shot 11 (Extreme Wide)**: A massive beam of violet-cyan energy shoots through the shattered glass roof into the night sky, parting the rain clouds. The core falls silent and dark. Astra and Nyx stand panting, looking at the sky.
---
### Visual Style
High-contrast cyberpunk, volumetric steam, boiling water ripples, neon orange-to-violet gradients, anamorphic lens flares, rain-slick textures.

### Camera Notes

Fast pans, slow-motion water drops, high-angle downward tracking, deep shadow contrasts.
