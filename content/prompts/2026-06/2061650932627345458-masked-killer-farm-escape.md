Ultra-realistic horror-thriller. Large abandoned wooden barn at night. Moonlight streaming through gaps in old wooden walls. Hay bales, farm equipment, hanging chains, dust floating through the air. Fast-paced chaotic cinematography with handheld camera, Dutch angles, whip pans, crash zooms, speed ramps, and dramatic bullet-time moments. Sound: heavy breathing, creaking wood, distant thunder, footsteps on hay, fox snarls.

0–2s — HOOK

Dutch-angle handheld shot.

A terrified young woman in a bikini top and shorts SMASHES through a thin wooden barn wall.

Wood splinters explode everywhere.

She crashes onto the hay-covered floor.

Small cuts cover her arms and face.

Blood streaks across her skin.

Breathing hard, she desperately crawls backward.

At the opposite end of the barn—

A masked killer calmly steps through the hole she just made.

Moonlight silhouettes him.

2–5s — ESCALATION

Low-angle handheld shot.

The woman struggles to her feet.

Limping.

Shaking.

She stumbles between hay bales and old farming equipment.

The killer keeps coming.

Not running.

Walking.

Confident.

Relentless.

She turns and runs deeper into the barn.

5–8s — THE ATTACK

Tracking shot through the barn aisle.

The killer suddenly pulls a hunting knife and throws it.

Bullet-time.

The knife spins through dusty moonlit air.

The blade slices across her shoulder as she turns.

Blood droplets scatter through the light beams.

She screams.

The knife buries itself into a wooden support post behind her.

The woman crashes hard into a hay bale.

8–11s — THE KILLER CLOSES IN

Handheld close-up.

The woman crawls backward across the floor.

The killer slowly approaches.

Step.

Step.

Step.

He stands over her.

Raises a second knife.

The woman freezes.

Certain she's about to die.

11–13s — PAYOFF

Suddenly—

A vicious snarl echoes through the barn.

A wild fox bursts from beneath a hay pile.

It launches directly at the killer's face.

The killer stumbles backward.

The fox claws and bites furiously.

Dust and hay explode into the air.

13–15s — ENDING

The woman scrambles to her feet.

She sprints through the large barn doors and out into an open moonlit field.

Camera follows her outside.

She looks back while running.

Inside the dark barn, the killer and fox struggle in the shadows.

Then the barn doors slowly swing shut on their own.

Hard cut to black.
