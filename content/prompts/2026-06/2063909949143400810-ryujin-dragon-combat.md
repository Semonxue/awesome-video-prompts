00:00–00:03 (Abyssal Resonance · Ley Line Awakening)

Camera: Extreme close-up of a single droplet of water hitting the dry ground in slow motion → Rapid pull-back to an overhead shot as a massive ripple violently expands across the earth.

Action: The girl stands tall, then suddenly drops into a low crouch, punching one fist firmly into the ground while her other hand forms a half-frame mudra gesture over her eye. The solid earth beneath her begins to ripple and warp like liquid.

Effects: A swirling, deep-ocean blue magic circle with ancient Ryujin (Dragon God) crests explodes outward. Runes shaped like stylized waves and lightning bolts twist in a high-speed whirlpool formation. Torrential rain begins falling upwards from the circle, completely defying gravity.

Atmosphere: Heavy and suffocating, the sudden, immense pressure of the deep sea responding to her call.

00:03–00:06 (Dragon Manifestation · Abyssal Leviathan Descent)

Camera: Low angle pointing up from the ground → High-speed orbiting tracking shot spiraling upward alongside the water torrent.

Action: The girl throws her head back and extends her arms outward, her fingers curving like talons. Her body begins to levitate slightly, suspended within the center of the upward-flowing rain.

Effects: The rising water instantly coalesces into the gargantuan, spectral form of a classic Japanese Dragon (Ryujin). Its scales glitter with bioluminescent neon-blue and silver light as it coils tightly around her body. Crackling arcs of white lightning snap between its horns, perfectly synchronizing with her breathing.

Japanese Incantation (Echoing, majestic, dual-toned voice): "Lord of the endless deep, crash through the heavens! Awaken, Ryujin!"
Atmosphere: Mythical and terrifying, a ancient storm deity sealing its contract.

00:06–00:09 (Spirit Overdrive · Dragon Core Fusion)

Camera: Side-profile close-up → Instant, jarring zoom into her chest as the dragon silhouette makes impact.

Action: The girl clamps her hands together in a fierce dragon-jaw mudra. The massive water dragon roars and dives headfirst into her sternum. Her spine arches violently backward as she fully absorbs the massive elemental force.

Effects: A colossal shockwave of pressurized water and lightning blasts outward in a 360-degree ring, instantly clearing the battlefield of dust. Brilliant blue scales temporarily manifest across her skin before sinking beneath the surface. Her standard uniform tears away, replaced by fluid, flowing samurai-monk robes with wave crests and heavy, dark metallic gauntlets.

Details: Her hair unravels into long, floating strands that drift around her head as if submerged underwater, glowing with electrical static at the tips. Sharp lightning patterns permanently etch themselves into her irises.

Atmosphere: Primal fusion, the awakening of an unstoppable tempest.

00:09–00:12 (Final Ascension · Katana Manifestation · Storm Form Complete)

Camera: High-angle wide shot tracking her descent from the air → Cinematic slow-motion still the exact millisecond her boots touch the ground.

Action: The girl draws her hands apart as if pulling a thread from the air. A sleek, traditional katana forged from condensed black lightning and liquid silver materializes in her grip. She executes a lightning-fast mid-air spin, landing flawlessly in a classic iaido stance, hand resting on the hilt.

Effects: The air around her blade hums with immense voltage, leaving a lingering trail of dark blue electrical discharge. The edges of her heavy robes billow dramatically in a phantom wind, and the faint, spectral silhouette of the dragon's tail sways gently behind her, mimicking her balance.

Styling: Hydrodynamic, combat-ready traditional robes. A crystalline katana crackling with raw storm energy. Movements are heavy yet perfectly fluid.

Atmosphere: Absolute mastery, the ultimate fusion of blade and storm.

00:12–00:15 (Initialization Complete · Execution Stance)

Camera: High-speed 360° orbit focusing tightly on the sheathed blade → Sharp snap to a front-facing, wide-angle "ready" shot with a heavy depth-of-field blur on the background.

Action: She slowly lowers her center of gravity, shifting her weight onto her back leg. She slightly unseats the katana blade with her thumb, letting a single, blinding flash of blue lightning escape the scabbard. Her expression is completely deadpan, hyper-focused on the enemy ahead.

Effects: All the floating water droplets in the air instantly freeze in place, then shatter like glass into a misty vapor. The crackling lightning silences entirely, replaced by a ominous, low-frequency sub-bass hum.

Atmosphere: The breathless, terrifying moment of absolute stillness right before the explosion of the first strike.
