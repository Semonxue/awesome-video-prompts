The camera then deliberately tilts up, following his gaze, and the focus racks to reveal an impossible spectacle: the sky has been replaced by a deep cyan ocean. Two more massive, photo-realistic sharks, complete with visible scars, circle lazily between the towering skyscrapers as if they were canyon walls. The water is filled with tiny, illuminated particles. In a final surreal touch, a flock of pigeons flies through the water, their wing beats unnaturally slow, leaving faint trails of tiny air bubbles.
