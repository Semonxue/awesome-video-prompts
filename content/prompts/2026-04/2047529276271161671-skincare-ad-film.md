### Shot 1 — The Morning Wake-Up
```
Early morning bedroom, golden hour. Mia — late 20s, 
warm medium-brown skin, loose natural wavy hair, wearing 
an oversized soft cream linen shirt — lies on a white 
linen bed with rumpled sheets and a single pillow. Warm 
sunlight filters through sheer white curtains, casting 
long soft rays across the bed and her face. She slowly 
opens her eyes, blinks softly, and stretches her arms 
above her head with a quiet exhale. The room is calm — 
a bedside table with a glass of water, soft shadows on 
the wall. Camera starts at mid-distance and does a slow 
deliberate push-in toward her face. Shallow depth of 
field. Warm golden tones, slightly desaturated. 
Handheld phone-camera grain texture. Authentic 
lifestyle feel, no studio lighting.
```

---

### Shot 2 — The Bathroom Routine
```
Bright minimal bathroom, morning light. Mia — late 20s, 
warm medium-brown skin, wavy hair loosely tied back, 
wearing a soft white robe — stands close to a round 
mirror above a white ceramic sink. She holds a small 
glass dropper bottle, dispenses two drops of clear serum 
onto her fingertips, then gently presses and taps it 
into her cheekbones and jawline in slow circular 
motions. Her expression is focused, unhurried, 
completely at ease — this is a private ritual, not a 
performance. Natural diffused window light enters from 
the left, casting soft shadows. Skin texture is visible 
and real. Camera is static with subtle organic handheld 
micro-shake. Vertical 9: 16 frame. Warm neutral tones. 
Realistic skin, no beauty filter.
```

---

### Shot 3 — The Product Hero

```
Close-up on a bathroom counter. A cylindrical amber 
glass dropper bottle with a matte black cap sits on 
clean white marble tile. Tiny water droplets cling to 
the side of the glass. In the background, a softly 
blurred window lets in diffused morning light, creating 
a gentle warm bokeh. A white hand towel and a few green 
leaves from a small plant sit out of focus in the far 
background. Camera starts at mid-close distance and 
executes a slow, deliberate extreme macro push-in, 
filling the frame with the bottle's glass texture and 
condensation droplets. No hands. No branding visible. 
Natural light only. Clean, grounded, real bathroom 
context — not a studio product shot.
```

---

### Shot 4 — The Glow Payoff
```
Late morning, near a large apartment window. Mia — 
late 20s, warm medium-brown skin, wavy hair down and 
relaxed, wearing a soft cream linen shirt — stands 
angled 45 degrees toward the window. Warm directional 
side light falls across the left side of her face, 
illuminating her cheekbones and skin with a natural 
luminous glow. No shimmer or filter — just healthy, 
hydrated skin catching real light. Her expression is 
calm, self-assured, a barely-there half-smile at the 
corner of her mouth. She is not posing. Camera begins 
at her shoulder level and executes a slow, smooth tilt 
upward, landing on a close-up of her face in the final 
two seconds. Warm golden tones, soft shadow detail 
preserved. Shallow depth of field. Handheld 
documentary texture. Feels observed, not staged.
```

---

### Shot 5 — Out the Door
```
Bright apartment entryway, morning. Mia — late 20s, 
warm medium-brown skin, wavy hair down, now dressed in 
casual daytime clothes: white fitted top and light 
trousers — reaches for her canvas tote bag and keys 
hanging on a hook near the front door. She swings the 
bag over her shoulder, takes two steps toward the door, 
then pauses and glances back over her left shoulder at 
a full-length mirror on the wall. She looks at herself 
for one quiet beat — expression: satisfied, confident, 
at ease — then turns back and opens the door, stepping 
into soft hallway light. Camera follows loosely behind 
her, tracking at half a step back, slightly low angle. 
Natural warm indoor light. Shallow depth of field, 
background softly blurred.
