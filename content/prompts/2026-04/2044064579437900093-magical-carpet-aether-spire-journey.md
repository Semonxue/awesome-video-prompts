It features soaring ivory towers wrapped in "living" gold vines, massive rotating rings of stone circling the highest spires, and bridges made of pure, solid light connecting different districts. A wide, glowing river of liquid starlight bisects the city, with ethereal ships soaring above its surface.Characters & Magic PropsThe Rider: A woman in shimmering saffron silk robes that don't just flutter; they glow faintly with protective runes.
