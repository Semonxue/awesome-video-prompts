15-second ultra-realistic comedy short, photorealistic live-action style, sunny backyard bar setup, detailed feathers and fur, realistic liquid physics.

A colorful parrot is bartending at a tiny outdoor bar. Various animals (dog, rabbit, squirrel) line up for drinks.

Dialogue:

Parrot (cool bartender voice): "What'll it be today?"

Dog (excited): "Bone juice please!"

Rabbit (polite): "Carrot martini, extra hops."

Squirrel (hyper): "NUT-ella shot! Double!!"

Parrot (after spilling): "You animals are gonna bankrupt me…"

Shot-by-shot:

[0-3s] Wide shot: Parrot polishing glasses behind the bar. "What'll it be today?"

[3-7s] Quick montage: Dog orders "Bone juice!", rabbit "Carrot martini!", squirrel "NUT-ella shot!!"

[7-11s] Parrot mixes drinks but spills everywhere from the chaos.

[11-15s] Final shot: All animals happily drinking while parrot looks exhausted. Parrot sighs: "You animals are gonna bankrupt me…"
