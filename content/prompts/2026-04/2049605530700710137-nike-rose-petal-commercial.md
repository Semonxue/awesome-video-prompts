SHOT 1 (0:00–0:02) — PETAL DROP OPENING
Speed ramp deceleration + macro push-in. Orange and blush rose petals fall from above in real time — at peak density, frame drops to 15% speed, petals suspended mid-air. Camera pushes into the flower bed from above. Warm amber grade floods the frame. Hard cut on a single petal hitting ground.
SHOT 2 (0:02–0:04) — SWOOSH CLOSE REVEAL ⭐ SIGNATURE
Digital zoom scale-out + lens flare sweep. Starts extreme macro on the gold Swoosh embroidery — stitching texture fills every pixel. Frame scales back slowly revealing the full floral upper, terracotta body, orange laces. A warm gold lens flare sweeps left to right across the Swoosh at peak scale. Cut on flare exit.
SHOT 3 (0:04–0:06) — SHOE HERO ORBIT
Slow arc orbit + depth-of-field rack focus. Camera orbits the sneaker at 20% speed — roses surround the base, soft orange bokeh pulses behind. Focus snaps from foreground rose to the embroidered floral panel mid-orbit. The full silhouette holds center frame. Whip-pan right, motion blur smear.
SHOT 4 (0:06–0:07) — LACE TEXTURE MACRO
Rack focus snap + chromatic aberration pulse. Extreme close on the orange woven laces — light catches individual fibers. Frame snaps focus from lace to the glitter dot detailing on the upper. Single chromatic aberration ripple runs across frame once. Hard cut.
SHOT 5 (0:07–0:09) — PETAL EXPLOSION ⭐ SIGNATURE
Speed ramp acceleration + particle burst. Roses erupt outward from beneath the shoe — petals scatter in all directions. Frame accelerates from 20% to full speed in 0.8 seconds. Orange and gold particles blur outward from the sole. Camera locked wide, shoe centered. Peak energy moment. Smash cut to clean orange.
SHOT 6 (0:09–0:11) — SOLE STAMP CLOSE
Slow push-down + warm bloom. Camera descends slowly toward the yellow midsole stripe and black outsole. Warm amber bloom pulses once from the midsole — clean, graphic, deliberate. Low density breath moment after the explosion. 6-frame dissolve to near-white.
SHOT 7 (0:11–0:13) — FULL PRODUCT FLOAT
Parallax drift + bokeh bloom. Shoe appears to float above the rose bed — camera drifts imperceptibly upward, creating subtle parallax between sneaker and flowers beneath. Background orange deepens in saturation. Clean, still, premium. Slow dissolve begins.
SHOT 8 (0:13–0:15) — LOGO ON ORANGE
Fade-up on warm orange field. White Nike wordmark rises cleanly. Single soft bloom pulse on the type — warm, not harsh. Settles to static. Hard fade to black.
