Create a funny dramatic scene in the style of an ancient Chinese costume drama, featuring two female cats arguing and fighting over a handsome prince cat in a royal palace courtyard at night. The tone is theatrical, emotional, playful, and culturally rich, like an exaggerated palace romance scene from classic Chinese television, but with cute anthropomorphic cats. Both female cats wear luxurious traditional Chinese hanfu with long flowing sleeves, silk embroidery, ornate hair accessories, and elegant layered fabrics. One wears deep red, the other wears jade green. The prince cat stands between them in embroidered golden royal robes, nervous and overwhelmed.

Open with a wide cinematic shot of the moonlit palace courtyard, red lanterns glowing, silk banners moving in the wind, carved stone floor, soft mist, and petals drifting through the air. Cut to dramatic close-ups of each cat's eyes, whiskers, and sleeve movements. Cat A steps forward with jealous pride and says in Mandarin Chinese: "太子殿下是我的！" Cat B snaps her fan shut and replies in Mandarin Chinese: "做梦！昨夜他先看的是我！" The prince cat looks panicked and quietly says in Mandarin Chinese: "别吵了……"

Then the argument turns into a funny, elegant catfight with dramatic Chinese-drama energy: sleeve flicks, fan swats, spinning turns, paw slaps, proud poses, exaggerated reactions, and graceful chasing around the courtyard. No brutality, only theatrical comedic conflict. The prince keeps trying to step back as the two cats circle him with intense eye contact and melodramatic gestures. Use hard cuts, fast reaction shots, close-ups, over-the-shoulder shots, slow push-ins, sleeve motion, flying petals, and comedic timing. End with both female cats frozen in proud warrior-like poses while the prince cat stands in the middle in total confusion.
