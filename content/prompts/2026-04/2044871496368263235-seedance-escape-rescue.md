  00:00–00:01: Close-up of slender hands wrapping faded black cloth tape around knuckles. Subtle tremble, chipped dark nail polish. She's wearing a worn-out dark hoodie and fitted cargo pants. Soft flickering fluorescent light, cramped interior, muted teal tones.

  00:01–00:02: Extreme close-up of a young woman's face—sharp eyes, tired but determined. A faint scar runs across her eyebrow. She exhales slowly, fogging slightly in the cold air. Dim, bluish lighting from a nearby window.

  00:02–00:03: Medium shot: she pulls a small folded photograph from her pocket—an old picture of her and a child. Her thumb brushes over it briefly before she tucks it away. Background: peeling wallpaper, abandoned apartment.

  00:03–00:04: Wide shot: she quietly opens a creaky door and steps into a dim hallway. Long corridor, flickering lights, damp walls. Her footsteps are careful, controlled.

  00:04–00:05: Over-the-shoulder shot as she peeks around a corner. Two shadowy figures stand at the far end, talking. She pulls back quickly, pressing against the wall. Tension builds.

  00:05–00:06: Low-angle shot of her slipping through a half-open service door into a stairwell. Concrete steps, rusted railings, echoes of distant footsteps.

  00:06–00:07: Handheld shot: she ascends rapidly but silently, gripping the railing. Her breathing quickens. The camera shakes slightly, adding urgency.

  00:07–00:08: Close-up: she reaches the rooftop door, hesitates for half a second, then pushes it open. Harsh daylight floods in, contrasting the dark interior.

  00:08–00:09: Wide shot: rooftop garden—unexpectedly lush but overgrown, with scattered planters and wind-blown fabric. A small greenhouse structure sits at the center. Wind rustles everything.

  00:09–00:10: Medium shot: she approaches the greenhouse cautiously. Her eyes scan every detail. A faint metallic sound comes from inside.

  00:10–00:11: Inside the greenhouse: soft light filters through dirty glass. A young girl sits quietly, clutching a stuffed toy. She looks up, startled but hopeful.

  00:11–00:12: Close-up on the woman's face—relief washes over her. Her tough exterior softens instantly. She kneels down slowly, careful not to scare the child.

  00:12–00:13: Medium shot: the girl runs into her arms. They embrace tightly. The woman closes her eyes for a moment, holding her close as if grounding himself.

  00:13–00:14: Wide shot: the woman takes the girl's hand and leads her toward the rooftop edge where a hidden zipline cable stretches across to another building. Wind howls softly.

  00:14–00:15: Final shot: silhouette of the woman and child gliding across the skyline on the zipline into warm golden light breaking through clouds. The city below fades into calm. Ending tone: escape, hope, and quiet victory.
