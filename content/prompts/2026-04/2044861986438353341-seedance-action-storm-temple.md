Generate an intense cinematic action fight scene

REFERENCE: main_character] — Use this exact reference photo as the main hero. Keep face, body, clothing and all details 100% faithful.

SETTING: Ancient stone temple courtyard on a stormy mountain cliff at night. Heavy rain pouring down, water reflecting dramatic lightning flashes. Broken pillars and cracked marble floor. Strong wind whipping the rain sideways.

HERO: [main_character] — Exact person from the reference. Wet, focused, determined expression.

OPPONENT: Tall, muscular rival warrior in dark tactical armor with glowing red energy lines. Carries a long curved blade. Aggressive and mocking.

OPENING 2-SECOND HOOK (0-2s):
Extreme close-up on hero’s wet face as lightning flashes. He opens his eyes with intense focus. Instant cut to wide shot — opponent is already mid-air, descending with a powerful downward slash. Hero barely raises his sword. Massive orange-white sparks explode on impact. Rain freezes for a split second around the clash.

FIGHT SEQUENCE:

2-6s: 
Hero blocks the heavy strike and immediately counters with a fast spinning kick to the ribs. Opponent staggers but recovers instantly. Rapid sword exchange — sparks flying with every clash. Rain splashes violently from their footwork. Camera orbits dynamically around them.

6-10s: 
Opponent grabs hero’s sword arm and headbutts him hard. Hero stumbles. Opponent follows with a brutal spinning slash. Hero ducks at the last moment — blade cuts several bamboo stalks behind him that fall dramatically. Hero rolls forward and delivers a sharp upward strike.

10-13s (CLIMAX): 
Both blades lock in the center. They push against each other with full strength, rain pouring between them, lightning flashing overhead. Hero suddenly twists, breaks the lock and delivers a powerful spinning heel kick to the opponent’s chest.

13-15s (RESOLUTION): 
Opponent is sent flying backward, crashing into a stone pillar that cracks on impact. Hero stands breathing heavily in the pouring rain, sword lowered but ready, looking at his opponent with focused intensity. Lightning illuminates both of them.

TECHNICAL SPECS:
- Duration: Exactly 15 seconds
- Resolution: 4K
- Frame rate: 24fps (cinematic)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Camera: Dynamic orbiting shots, fast whip pans, dramatic slow-motion on the final kick
- Lighting: Strong cinematic lighting with bright lightning flashes and volumetric rain
- Rain: Heavy and interactive — must splash and react with every movement and impact
- Color grade: Dark teal-blue tones with bright orange lightning and sparks for high contrast
- Mood: Raw, intense, epic, high-stakes action. Rain must feel like a major character.

Make every movement sharp, powerful and weighty. Maximum visual impact in 15 seconds.
