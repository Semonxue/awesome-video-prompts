Abandoned Hospital Dream Infection 
(8 shots, ~15s) – 
Organic-Tech ParasiteDim abandoned hospital corridor at night, flickering emergency lights, overgrown vines and rusted medical equipment. 

[0-2s] Shot 1: Forward tracking shot following woman in nurse scrubs exploring cautiously, flashlight beam revealing pulsing organic growths on walls. 

[2-3.5s] Shot 2: Medium handheld, she touches a vine; black tendrils with metallic veins crawl up her arm with slick organic stretching. 

[3.5-5s] Shot 3: Close-up on arm, skin bulges as parasitic cables and fleshy tubes burrow underneath with realistic vein pulsing. 

[5-6.5s] Shot 4: Over-the-shoulder, her reflection in a broken mirror shows extra eyes forming, purple bioluminescent orbs glowing. 

[6.5-8s] Shot 5: Dynamic circling medium shot, back arches as mechanical-organic wings of bone and circuit boards sprout with wet cracking sounds implied via motion. 

[8-10s] Shot 6: Full shot push-in, tentacles of fused flesh and wire lash out knocking over IV stands, glass and fluid splattering realistically. 

[10-12s] Shot 7: Extreme close-up on face, mouth stretches as additional jaw-like mandibles emerge with dripping viscous saliva. 

[12-15s] Shot 8: Slow 360° orbit pull-back as the complete parasite-human hybrid stands, multiple limbs twitching, bioluminescent veins illuminating the dark corridor. 

Global style: Photorealistic medical body horror, ultra-detailed VFX with organic-tech merging, fleshy cable physics, bioluminescent glow, debris and fluid interactions, eerie green-purple lighting, shallow depth of field.
