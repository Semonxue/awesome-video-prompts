A clean white background fills the frame. Thin streams of gray smoke rise slowly from the bottom in extreme macro view. The camera is completely static, capturing the delicate motion. The smoke curls and spreads like ink in water, forming soft organic shapes. Gradually, the shapes sharpen into tree trunks, branches, and leaves. Hints of green and brown fade in as the smoke transforms into a tiny realistic forest landscape. Light fog drifts gently between the trees. The final second holds on the finished miniature forest, perfectly still.
