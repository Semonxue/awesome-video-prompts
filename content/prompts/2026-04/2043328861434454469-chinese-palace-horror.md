Terrifying cinematic horror sequence inside an abandoned imperial palace at night, ancient Chinese palace architecture, endless red corridors, dying lantern light, torn silk curtains, drifting ash, deep shadows, oppressive silence, elegant supernatural dread, hard cuts only, no fade, no dissolve, no morph transition.

Shot 1: Wide establishing shot of a vast deserted imperial corridor lit by dim red lanterns, ash drifting through frame, torn curtains swaying slightly, a pale woman in ancient robes standing far away with her back to camera.

Cut to Shot 2: Slow dolly-in down the corridor. Her sleeves drag across the floor. Her hair hangs completely still, unnatural against the moving curtains. She does not move.

Cut to Shot 3: Hard cut. She is suddenly much closer without walking. Medium-long shot. Her head tilts slightly, almost imperceptibly. One lantern flickers out.

Cut to Shot 4: Over-the-shoulder shot from behind the camera position as palace doors slam one by one in the distance behind, each impact echoing through the corridor. Return to her: closer again, still facing away.

Cut to Shot 5: Close-up of her trailing sleeves scraping across the stone floor, ash circling around the fabric, then hard cut to her shoulders and neck as a faint smile slowly begins to form though her face is still unseen.

Cut to Shot 6: Fast reaction cut to the corridor walls, shadows stretching too long, lantern glow weakening, silk curtains lifting as if something unseen passes through them. Hard cut back: she is now only a few steps away.

Cut to Shot 7: Extreme close shot. She slowly turns around with broken ceremonial elegance. Her face is almost human but subtly wrong, skin too pale, empty eyes, whispering mouth moving with no visible emotion.

Cut to Shot 8: Final terrifying reveal. Locked frame on her face as she leans slightly toward camera, smile widening unnaturally, whispering mouth opening too far while the last lantern dies and the corridor falls into darkness.

Style: ancient Chinese palace horror, elegant ghost story, oppressive cinematic tension, painterly red-black contrast, slow dread, premium costume detail, subtle supernatural movement, terrifying final reveal, normal proportions without stretch.
