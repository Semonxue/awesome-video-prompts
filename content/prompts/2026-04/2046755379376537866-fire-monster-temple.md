Ruined ancient temple at golden sunset, cracked pillars, heavy wind, glowing dust, embers, flying debris, strong volumetric god rays, cinematic destruction atmosphere.

A pink-haired girl with small devil horns, black leather jacket, white crop top, black mini skirt, walks forward confidently through smoke and rubble.

A massive dark monster emerges from the front, ground shaking.

She stops, raises her hand—fire ignites, forming a glowing fireball. Her eyes blaze.
The monster charges. She thrusts her hand—fireball launches and rapidly grows larger, absorbing embers, pushing debris outward.
Massive impact—fireball explodes into the monster, huge blast, shockwave, debris scattering everywhere.
Final shot: dust settles, she stands calm and powerful in the destroyed temple, embers floating, fire fading from her hand.
