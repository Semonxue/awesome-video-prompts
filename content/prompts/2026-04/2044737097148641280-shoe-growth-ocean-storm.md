Shoe Growth: Cinematic 3D Animation (Minimalist Core Version)
Rendered with UE5 ray tracing, physically based materials, macro telephoto, shallow depth of field, pure dark background, high-contrast cool tone, smooth camera movement, focusing on shoe growth and material development.
0-2s: Macro zoom in, white and light blue particles converge, outlining the blue-gray rubber contour of the toe, showing a matte, grainy texture.
2-5s: Lens zooms in slightly, the contour grows into the white mesh upper (showing fiber texture), particles fill the logo, generating dark blue TPU stripes (showing paint reflection).
5-9s: Zooming in further, the tongue, upper, and laces grow, midsole foam particles stack (showing pores), TPU support extends, and the outsole rubber pattern takes shape.
9-12s: Macro focus switches, close-up of the mesh, TPU, logo, midsole, shoe opening, and outsole material differences, side backlighting highlights the texture.
12-15s: The camera zooms out, freezing the complete side profile of the shoe against a floating pure black background, with particles finishing the shot, color matching restored, and details maximizing.
