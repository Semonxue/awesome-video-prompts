Each frame can change clothing and visual style independently. Do not maintain outfit or style continuity.

Ultra-realistic cinematic video, 15s, 24fps.

A female dancer performs hip-hop choreography, including a smooth moonwalk. Her body movement must remain smooth, natural, and continuous throughout.

CAMERA: Dynamic handheld energy camera swings, slight shake, crash zoom in/out, fast push-ins, and orbits around the dancer, following her movement rhythm.

CORE ACTION: She dances confidently, centered in frame, full-body visible, strong rhythm and expressive movement.

CHAOS TRANSFORMATION (VERY HIGH SPEED): Clothing AND visual style change extremely fast (near every frame flicker).

CLOTHING: Every frame = new trendy outfit (no repetition, no blending): modern streetwear, layered urban outfits, edgy fashion looks, contemporary performance wear.

STYLE TRANSFORMATION (identity preserved): Rapidly shift between unique visual styles: realistic human rendering, anime-style rendering, minimal continuous line art, watercolor wash illustration, inked comic style (bold outlines, graphic shading), soft pastel illustration, textured digital painting, rough sketch drawing style, marker illustration style, paper cut-out collage style, clay-like sculpted look, low-poly geometric 3D, neon outline silhouette style, glitch-outline graphic style (clean, non-distorting), storybook illustration style, hand-painted brush style, chalkboard drawing style, airbrush gradient illustration, silhouette shadow art style.

STYLE BLENDING BURSTS: Occasionally combine 2–3 styles in a single frame (e.g., line art + watercolor, comic + low-poly, sketch + digital paint) for brief visual bursts.

CRITICAL: Her facial features, proportions, and identity must remain consistent across all styles. Only rendering style and clothing change NOT the person.

SYNC: Style and outfit changes align tightly with dance rhythm and camera motion.

VISUAL FEEL: Hyper-fast flicker, energetic but clean and readable. No distortion or warping of body.

LIGHTING: Dynamic studio lighting adapting to style changes.

CONSTRAINTS: body motion must remain perfectly continuous, no pose resets, no face distortion or identity change, only clothing and visual style change rapidly, no text, no logos.
