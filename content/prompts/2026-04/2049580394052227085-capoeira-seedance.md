Scene: One continuous drone shot of 2 men fighting Capoeira on a mountain top platform overlooking the Rio Favelas. Create a cinematic drone-shot sequence of two men performing an intense capoeira exchange on a circular concrete fighting platform perched on the edge of a cliff high above the Brazilian favelas. The platform is weathered concrete with chipped yellow paint markings. Massive mountain cliffs, dense colorful shanty buildings, and layered Rio-style mountain scenery surround the location. The mood is tense, athletic, and beautiful. Keep the motion fluid and continuous like a skilled drone operator is capturing the action in real time. Use realistic movement, natural body mechanics, and strong environmental scale.

Style: Use image_3 as the style and aesthetic of the scene for the cinematorgraphy.

Camera Movement: Use image_2 and follow the camera motion exactly as directed in the storyboard.

Men Fight Sequence: Use Image_1 as the diagram to follow the sequence to create choreography of the fight scene.

Start from exact view from image_1.

We start high above the brazilian favelas looking down at the men in the ready position about to fight.

Start with a very high diagonal aerial establishing shot looking down on the circular platform and both men squared up in ginga, tiny within the massive landscape. The drone descends in a smooth arc toward them, maintaining the overhead angle as the environment establishes danger, height, and scale.
As the drone gets closer, reveal the men clearly in ginga, circling lightly and rhythmically. Continue pushing in. The lighter fighter begins a feint and entry while the darker fighter stays reactive and ready. The drone sweeps lower and closer to the platform edge, then glides into a low sweeping pass near the fighters as the exchange intensifies.
Begin an orbit around them as the darker fighter drops into esquiva, slipping under the incoming motion. The orbit should continue leftward around the pair as the lighter fighter flows into meia-lua de compasso, carving a spinning heel kick through space. Keep the drone moving with parallax so the background mountains and favelas shift behind them.
Tighten the orbit for a more intimate view as the darker fighter answers with a cocorinha counter, dropping low under the kick. Then continue the close orbit into the armada exchange: one fighter spins high while the other defends and recoils with awareness. The drone should stay dynamic, circling them with changing depth, sometimes one fighter dominating the foreground while the other stays in midground.
Then transition into the au / flow transition. One fighter inverts through a fluid cartwheel while the other ducks and tracks the movement. Let the drone change depth and angle mid-orbit, drifting past one fighter’s shoulder and then widening slightly so the full acrobatic motion reads clearly.
Finish by easing back into a wider orbit as both fighters reset and return to ginga, facing each other again in a controlled, rhythmic stance. End with the drone pulling slightly wider to reconnect the men to the circular platform, the cliff edge, the favela below, and the surrounding mountains.

Overall style: cinematic realism, controlled drone cinematography, fluid capoeira motion, dynamic parallax, strong sense of height and danger, elegant fight choreography, natural lighting, subtle filmic texture, high-end action documentary feel.
