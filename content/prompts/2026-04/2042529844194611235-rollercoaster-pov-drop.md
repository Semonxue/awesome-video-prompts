First-person POV at the top of a massive rollercoaster in a modern amusement park under a bright blue sky with scattered clouds. Stylized anime hands grip the safety bar, slightly trembling as the park stretches below with dense twisting tracks. The coaster pauses briefly; hands tighten and fingers twitch as a nervous voice says, 'Wait... are we about to drop?' Suddenly, a violent vertical drop begins, the world rushes downward at extreme speed with heavy motion blur and speed lines. A sharp right turn throws the body sideways; hands lift slightly as a scream bursts out. The ride speeds through a dense track section with near-miss moments and rapid up-down motion, creating chaos with lens flares and intense track rumble. It then shoots into a straight track at high speed, the voice shifts to excitement, 'This is AWESOME!' The sequence ends with a smooth forward glide, hands relaxing on the bar.
