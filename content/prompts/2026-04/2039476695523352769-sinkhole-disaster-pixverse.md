  Satellite view. A massive sinkhole opens in the centre of a dense city block, not slowly, instantly, half a city block dropping 40 metres in under two seconds, buildings tilting and sliding in. Camera plunges toward it at the same speed the ground falls. Shot 2: Snap into a low ground-level shot alongside a water main rupturing at the sinkhole edge, the pipe tears open and a wall of pressurized water detonates sideways across the street. Shot 3: Cut to a fast tracking shot behind a fire truck that has locked its brakes on the tilting street, the driver fighting the wheel as the road beneath the rear wheels begins to crumble. Shot 4: The rear axle drops over the edge. The truck pivots. The driver kicks the door open and jumps onto the tilting street surface, rolls, grabs a fire hydrant base as the truck slides past him and drops into the void. Shot 5: Wide shot, the sinkhole has stopped growing. The street around it is devastated, water geysering from multiple broken mains. Shot 6: Extreme wide aerial pulling upward. The city grid intact everywhere except this one block, a dark circular wound in the middle of it.
