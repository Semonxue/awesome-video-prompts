Cinematic time-freeze short film, 15s, ultra-realistic, Arri Alexa Mini, 35mm lens. Lighting: Industrial kitchen, harsh overhead fluorescents mixed with warm copper stove glows, heavy volumetric steam, deep shadows, shallow DOF.

[0:00–0:03] A high-pressure Michelin-star kitchen. Chefs are screaming, pans are flaming. Steadicam frontal medium shot tracking a calm Head Chef walking through the frantic line. A massive bag of flour has just burst in the background. She raises her hand and snaps.

[0:03–0:06] Snap triggers a spherical shockwave with light refraction, and the entire world freezes instantly. A cloud of white flour is suspended in mid-air, every grain distinct. Arcs of boiling red demi-glace sauce hang in the air like glass sculptures. A tray of silver spoons is caught mid-clatter, hovering at odd angles. Absolute silence.

[0:06–0:09] Only she moves, her boots clicking on tile. The camera retreats as she weaves through the frozen chefs. She ducks under a suspended splash of wine. She reaches into a cloud of floating black peppercorns and plucks one.

[0:09–0:11] She stops before a commis chef whose face is contorted in panic, holding a lopsided plate. She tilts her head, uses a finger to perfectly center a black peppercorn on the plate. She smirks.

[0:11–0:15] She snaps, and reverses the entire shockwave. The flour cloud settles, the demi-glace splashes, and the kitchen roar returns instantly. She walks off-frame as the chaos resumes.
