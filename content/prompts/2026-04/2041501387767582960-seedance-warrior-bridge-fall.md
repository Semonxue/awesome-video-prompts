(Cinematic 35mm lens， handheld shake， 8k realistic texture， low-saturation cool tones)
A female warrior with blowing hair in intense high-speed motion， wearing worn tactical armor.

TIMELINE & ACTION LOGIC:
0-2s: [Intro] MS tracking， she is sliding at high speed on a wet cracked bridge， kicking up water spray. A massive industrial ruin shadow descends from clouds， intensifying pressure.

2-4s: [Drop] WS follow-shot， the bridge vanishes under her feet. As she falls into the fog-filled abyss， she fires a high-tension grappling cable that anchors to a steel beam.

4-7s: [Swing] MS wide arc swing. The cable snaps taut， converting falling momentum into a powerful lateral swing. Her body curves with inertia， reaching a zero-G peak at 6.5s.

7-9s: [Vault] MS push-in， she releases the cable and lands on a falling concrete fragment. She performs a violent kick-off (reaction force)， vaulting herself upward while the debris drops.

9-11s: [Impact] Low-angle WS， the massive ruin slams into the remaining bridge， erupting a radial shockwave and volumetric dust clouds. She shrinks her body to pass through a structural gap mid-air.

11-13s: [Braking] CU slow-motion， her boots friction against the ruin surface creating bright sparks. She fires a short-range hook for a high-G tangential turn， redirecting toward the center.

13-14s: [Leap] MS → WS， taking two heavy accelerating steps on the descending structure， she leaps into the void toward the palm-like turbine core with arms outstretched.

14-15s: [Loop] A secondary inertia tremor blasts her off the surface， throwing her back toward a new bridge section. She enters the initial sliding state again， creating a perfect seamless loop.

(SFX: high-frequency wind， metal screeching， low-frequency shockwave， heavy impact)
