100% real-life filmed texture, iPhone documentary look, handheld single take feeling, natural daylight, soft Scandinavian lighting, slight breathing shake, subtle focus shifts, minimal clean aesthetic.
Character Reference Use Image1 as the exact person reference. Face, body, outfit must remain identical. No exaggeration.
Environment A bright minimalist Scandinavian living room. White walls, wooden floor, large windows, soft daylight. Several unopened flatpack boxes scattered around.
Hook (0–3s) One IKEA box starts shaking.
Then another.
Then all of them.
13-Second One-Take Sequence
0–2s Boxes burst open.
Flatpack pieces (wood panels, screws, frames) float into the air.
SFX: wood sliding, light mechanical clicks
2–3s A folded instruction manual rises slowly in front of the character.
Pages flip rapidly on their own.
3–5s The IKEA logo on the manual glows soft blue-yellow.
A subtle energy spreads into the floating pieces.
Music: light playful orchestral build
5–7s All furniture parts begin self-assembling mid-air:
• chair forms piece by piece • table legs snap perfectly into place • shelves lock together with precision
Everything is fast, clean, satisfying.
7–9s The assembled furniture doesn’t stop,
It keeps combining into a giant humanoid wooden figure made of IKEA parts.
SFX: layered wooden clicks, deep assembling 
9–10s The wooden figure gently steps forward…
Then carefully adjusts a slightly crooked picture frame on the wall.
10–11s It pauses.
Looks at the room.
11–12s Instantly collapses back into individual furniture pieces
Perfectly placed around the room in a clean layout.
12–13s The character looks around.
Everything is now a perfectly styled IKEA showroom.
On screen:
“Some assembly required.”
Audio Direction
 SFX: wood clicks, sliding panels, soft mechanical assembly sounds
 Music: light playful build → slightly epic → soft comedic finish
