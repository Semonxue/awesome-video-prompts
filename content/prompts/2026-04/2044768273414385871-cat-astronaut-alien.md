STORY FORMAT: 15s / 150 BPM / ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT / 3D sci-fi action comedy / exaggerated cartoon space fantasy
TONE: curious exploration → absurd confrontation → sudden escalation → comedic domination payoff
SUBJECTS: Orange cat astronaut in a bulky white space suit holding a flashlight; Alien queen with green skin and large black eyes wearing a luxurious red diamond-studded gown and crown; Small hovering UFO craft
PROPS: Flashlight, smartphone, giant rocket launcher, UFO, lunar dust trails
ENVIRONMENT: Moon surface with craters and drifting dust, deep space skybox, distant Artemis II spacecraft tilted and embedded in the ground
COLOR: Cold blue-grey lunar tones → sharp contrast highlights → explosive fire glow → crisp heroic finish
CAMERA: Single continuous 24mm dynamic tracking shot, low-gravity float movement, smooth orbital pans, fast whip transitions, no cuts
1 (0-2s) LUNAR WALK
Camera glides low across the moon surface. Orange cat astronaut bounces lightly in low gravity, scanning with a flashlight. In the distance, Artemis II spacecraft sits tilted in the lunar dust.
2 (2-3.5s) UFO ARRIVAL
A sharp hum builds. Camera tilts up as a small UFO descends aggressively, kicking up a circular dust wave. It lands directly in front of the cat.
3 (3.5-5s) QUEEN DESCENDS
Hatch snaps open. Alien queen steps out dramatically, gown flowing despite no atmosphere. She points angrily at the cat.
DIALOGUE (Queen): Excuse me?! Who said you could just stroll onto MY moon?!
4 (5-6.5s) CAT CLAPBACK
Cat barely reacts, casually lowering flashlight.
DIALOGUE (Cat): Oh please… like you guys don't sneak down to Earth all the time.
5 (6.5-8s) EVIDENCE DROP
Cat pulls out smartphone and flips it toward her. Camera pushes in briefly: screen shows ancient pyramid construction—green alien in pharaoh outfit commanding white rabbit slaves moving giant stones.
DIALOGUE (Cat): Yeah… wanna explain this little construction project?
6 (8-9s) QUEEN DENIAL
Queen scoffs, visibly irritated, crossing arms.
DIALOGUE (Queen): Irrelevant! You're trespassing. That's still illegal.
7 (9-10s) FINAL WARNING
Cat tilts head slightly, smirks.
DIALOGUE (Cat): Alright… then I won't hold back.
8 (10-11s) MOCKING RESPONSE
Queen laughs arrogantly, stepping closer.
DIALOGUE (Queen): Oh really? And what exactly are gonna—
9 (11-12.5s) INSTANT ESCALATION
Before she finishes, cat whips out an oversized rocket launcher from off-frame. No hesitation—fires instantly. Massive recoil blast kicks up lunar dust cloud.
10 (12.5-13.5s) EXPLOSIVE EXIT
Rocket hits. Queen screams as she is launched backward, spinning into the void—small explosion bloom flashes in distance.
11 (13.5-15s) HERO SHOT END
Dust settles. Camera slowly pushes in. Cat adjusts suit calmly, turns to camera with confident grin.
DIALOGUE (Cat): Never mess with Cat Master.
SFX: soft ambient space hum, low-gravity foot thumps, rising UFO whine, sharp landing burst, comedic dialogue timing beats, phone beep reveal, heavy rocket launch blast, distant explosion pop, ending with subtle heroic sting and silence.
