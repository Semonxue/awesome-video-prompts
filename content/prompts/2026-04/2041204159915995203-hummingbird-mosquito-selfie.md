A graceful hummingbird weaves an impossibly perfect nest in the fork of a branch in a sun-drenched garden, every silk thread and lichen flake placed with architectural intention. The camera orbits slowly like a nature documentary — golden backlit strands glowing like cathedral glass, dramatic close-ups of each delicate connection point. Soft orchestral music swells. The hummingbird finishes, hovers at the center, and spreads its wings proudly to admire its creation. A single tiny mosquito drifts in and lands delicately on the nest. Tense pause. The mosquito looks around, looks at the camera, pulls out an impossibly small phone, takes a selfie — then gently lifts off and flies away completely unharmed. The hummingbird stares. Long silence. Style: Pixar-inspired 3D animation, warm garden lighting, hyper-detailed textures, nature documentary pacing with comedic payoff.
