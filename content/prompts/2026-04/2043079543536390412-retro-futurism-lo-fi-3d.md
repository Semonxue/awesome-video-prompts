product ad style 4: retro-futurism / Lo-Fi 3D

prompt:

A frantic 8-bit retro-futuristic 3D animation

0-3s (Voxel Blast): Extreme low-poly snap-zoom. Chunky 8-bit voxel threads blast outward from a glowing grid into 3D space.

3-6s (Tessellation Fast-Forward): Camera executes a stiff, mechanical 180-degree sweep as neon plastic cubes rapidly tessellate and click together to build the shoe.

6-9s (Voxel Text Integration): Dynamic speed ramp. The tessellating cubes form a dedicated, blocky voxel text panel on the side, clearly displaying "el.cine". Poly-chain laces zigzag into place.

9-12s (Stutter Spin): A stuttering, intentionally low-framerate orbit around the blocky sneaker floating above an infinitely scrolling synthwave grid.

12-15s (16-bit Flare): A massive 16-bit lens flare erupts from the side stripe, sending a cascade of glowing square pixels crashing directly into the camera.
