8–10s: Front tracking shot — bike moving toward camera, rider steady and focused, headlights flaring into lens.

10–12s: Dynamic tracking — the bike weaves smoothly between cars, tight spacing, near-contact moments with side mirrors.

12–13.5s: Close-up — wheel and suspension compressing slightly over uneven road, water splashing in slow detail.

13.5–15s: Final hero shot — long straight road, bike accelerating away from camera, taillight shrinking into the distance.
