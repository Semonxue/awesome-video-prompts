Movie-level Bang & Olufsen brand ad, hyper-realistic, first-person underwater acoustic journey, extreme sonic precision, continuous flowing camera. Vibe: deep, intelligent, Danish minimalism, acoustic architecture. A humpback whale's song — nature's most complex sound — seamlessly becomes the Beolab speaker's output. No background music — THE WHALE SONG IS THE MUSIC. 15 seconds, visual upgrade every 3 seconds.

【0-3s】THE DEEP. Camera descending into open ocean — deep blue to midnight. Silence. Then — a SOUND. Low. Felt more than heard. A humpback's opening note — bass frequency that travels 10,000 miles. Camera finds the source: a massive humpback, vertical in the blue, mouth opening. Sound waves VISIBLE as concentric rings from its throat.

【3-5s】Extreme close-up whale's throat — accordion pleats expanding, pushing sound through water. Sound waves' concentric rings — spacing, amplitude, decay — TRANSITION into the Beolab speaker's acoustic dispersion. Throat ridges become aluminum lamellar grille. Curved body becomes speaker's tapered form.

【5-8s】Speaker world. Camera orbiting the Beolab 90 in dark minimalist room — aluminum fins, teardrop profile, Danish precision. Camera along lamellar surface — each fin catching light like whale baleen. Through gaps: 18-driver array visible, each covering a different band.

【8-11s】Camera pulls back — speaker in its element. Dark Scandinavian room — concrete, oak, glass. Speaker standing like sculpture. Sound VISIBLE — air pressure differentials as faintest shimmer. Room responding: water in glass rippling, curtain moving, dust suspended in output pattern.

【11-13s】Camera finds reflection in glass wall — and in the reflection: the whale. Swimming. STILL SINGING. Speaker and whale separated by architecture, connected by frequency. Two acoustic architectures — biological and engineered — producing identical sound.

【13-15s】Hero lock. Beolab 90 centered. Dark room. Single sound wave ripple from grille. Text: BANG & OLUFSEN BEOLAB "Sound. Evolved."
