Subway Tunnel Shadow Puppet Awakening 

– Silhouette Entity Dark underground subway tunnel, single moving train light casting long shadows, distant rumbling. 

[0-2s] Shot 1: Tracking shot from behind woman in commuter clothes walking through tunnel, her shadow on wall behaving independently. 

[2-3.5s] Shot 2: Medium side shot, shadow detaches slightly and reaches toward her with inky stretching physics. 

[3.5-5s] Shot 3: Close-up on her legs, shadow tendrils wrap around ankles pulling her shadow self outward. 

[5-6.5s] Shot 4: Over-the-shoulder, her real body begins flattening and stretching like rubber as shadow merges. 

[6.5-8s] Shot 5: Dynamic low-angle orbit, multi-limbed shadow entity forms with elongated silhouette tentacles whipping realistically. 

[8-9.5s] Shot 6: Full shot, hybrid form smashes against tunnel wall, concrete cracking with dust and spark particles. 

[9.5-11s] Shot 7: Extreme close-up on face, features distort into abstract shadow puppet lines with glowing white eye slits. 

[11-12.5s] Shot 8: Medium action, shadow tentacles interact with train light creating dramatic multiplying silhouettes. 

[12.5-14s] Shot 9: 360° orbit as full towering shadow-puppet hybrid towers, body composed of layered 2D/3D silhouette layers. 

[14-15s] Shot 10: Final wide pull-back as the entity merges back into the wall, leaving only faint glowing outlines. 

Global style: Cinematic shadow horror, innovative VFX with 2D silhouette to 3D merging, ink stretching physics, dramatic single-light source casting, dust and concrete debris, high-contrast black-white with subtle glows.
