SCENE: A magical spring garden filled with blooming pink flowers, soft sunlight, and floating glowing particles. Atmosphere is dreamy, pastel-toned (white, pink, soft green), with a fantasy aura. SUBJECT / CHARACTER: A beautiful fairy-like girl with long blonde hair, soft elf ears, glowing skin. She wears a flowing silky ribbon dress in pink and green tones, semi-translucent, moving elegantly with the wind.

SHOT 1 — The Melody (INTRO)
Action: The girl stands among flowers. Gentle wind flows through her hair and dress. She slowly opens her lips and begins singing a magical melody (visible lip movement, emotional expression).
Camera: Extreme close-up → lips and lower face. Slow cinematic push-in. Soft depth of field, dreamy bokeh.
Audio: Clear, enchanting vocal melody.

SHOT 2 — Magic Awakens
Action: As she sings, glowing particles intensify in the air. Light begins to gather behind her.
Camera: Side profile shot → slow orbit around her.

SHOT 3 — Dragon Emerges
Action: A massive crystal dragon forms from light particles. Its body is made of glowing white and pink crystal, semi-transparent, refracting light.
Camera: Low angle wide shot → dragon rising.

SHOT 4 — Approach
Action: The dragon moves gracefully toward the girl. Its movement is slow, elegant. The girl keeps singing softly.
Camera: Tracking shot → follows dragon from side.

SHOT 5 — First Contact
Action: The dragon lowers its massive head gently. The girl lifts her hand and softly strokes the dragon's face.
Camera: Medium close-up → hand touching crystal surface.

SHOT 6 — Connection (EMOTIONAL CLIMAX)
Action: The girl turns her head slowly. She leans forward. The dragon moves closer. Their foreheads touch gently. Both close their eyes peacefully.
Camera: Close-up → symmetrical framing. Very slow push-in. Soft glow increases around them.
Audio: Melody fades into soft ambient harmony.
