A continuous shot of a vast, vibrant green hedge maze stretches across an open landscape beneath a soft, dusky sky. Towering hedges, well over twice a person's height, loom on either side of the narrow paths, creating walls of dense greenery that fully obscure any view beyond the immediate corridor. A man walks slowly through the winding maze, clearly lost, glancing around with quiet uncertainty.

The camera begins to rise, lifting steadily into the sky. As it ascends, the full layout of the maze starts to emerge, its intricate, hidden design gradually revealing itself from above.

Then, scattered figures begin to appear, first one, then several, then dozens, each previously concealed by the towering walls. As the camera continues to rise, the entire phrase "YOU HAVE COMPANY" is unveiled.
