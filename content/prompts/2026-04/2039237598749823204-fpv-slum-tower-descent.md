A massive vertical slum tower rises from a polluted futuristic city, its exterior covered in cables, balconies, and flickering lights. FPV camera spirals downward around the structure in a tightening corkscrew, skimming walls within inches as laundry, drones, and debris whip past. Without slowing, the camera punches through a shattered window into a claustrophobic interior maze of corridors, stairwells, and hanging neon signs, then bursts back outside through another opening, continuing its downward spiral into the glowing streets below. Aggressive speed ramps, tight spins, and rapid exposure shifts amplify chaos and density
