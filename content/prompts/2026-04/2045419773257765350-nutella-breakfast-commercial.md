Nutella Breakfast Ad Concept made with Seedance 2.0

Prompt: 15-second cinematic product advertisement for Nutella in a bright, sunlit modern kitchen.

[0-5s] Start with a smooth orbital shot circling a sealed glass jar of Nutella centered on a rustic wooden table, warm morning sunlight streaming through a window creating golden highlights and soft shadows on the glossy jar label. The jar dramatically bursts open in hyper-realistic slow motion, rich chocolate-hazelnut spread exploding outward in thick, glossy swirls with flying hazelnut pieces sparkling in the light.

[5-10s] Cut to a dynamic overhead tracking shot as fresh ingredients assemble magically onto the table: golden-brown toast slices fly in and land perfectly, thick layers of Nutella spread smoothly across them, sliced bananas and ripe strawberries tumble into place, a glass of milk and steaming coffee cup settle beside them forming an appetizing breakfast board. Slow-motion splashes and gentle particle movement emphasize the creamy texture and freshness.

[10-15s] Final smooth push-in to a close-up medium shot of the completed breakfast spread, with subtle steam rising from the coffee, glistening spread on toast, and natural light reflections. Camera slowly orbits the perfect arrangement, ending on an inviting hero view that highlights the irresistible chocolate swirls.

Photorealistic food commercial style, high detail on textures like glossy chocolate, crunchy hazelnuts, soft bread crust, and juicy fruit. Warm golden hour lighting with god rays, vibrant yet natural colors, shallow depth of field for appetizing focus. Cinematic, smooth motion, no text or logos, ultra-detailed 4K quality, stable composition, rich details, sharp clarity, no distortion. Diegetic sounds only: satisfying pops, gentle clinks, and ambient kitchen ambience.
