A young dark-skinned woman with green eyes and long wet dreadlocks sprints through a dense tropical jungle at golden hour. Rain pours down, leaves slash across her face.
SHOT 1 (0–2s): Low angle tracking shot — bare feet pounding wet jungle floor in slow motion, water splashing in ultra close-up.
SHOT 2 (2–4s): Wide dynamic shot — camera flies alongside her at speed through thick green foliage, motion blur on leaves.
SHOT 3 (4–6s): Front-facing handheld close-up — her green eyes fierce and focused, dreadlocks flying back, rain on skin glistening.
SHOT 4 (6–8s): She launches into a powerful leap mid-sprint — camera rotates 360° around her body as she reaches peak height, frozen for one frame in slow motion, body arched against dark stormy sky, rain droplets suspended in air. Cinematic 4K, photorealistic, epic action film tone. At the peak of the leap, mid-air — her body begins morphing into a black panther (melanistic jaguar).
SHOT 5 (8–10s): Extreme close-up — her green eyes stay identical as black fur ripples across her face from the edges inward, rain still falling.
SHOT 6 (10–12s): Wide shot — the silhouette of the woman twisting in air seamlessly becomes the sleek silhouette of a black panther, same momentum, same arc.
SHOT 7 (12–14s): The panther lands powerfully on all fours on wet jungle ground — massive slow-motion splash of mud and water, low angle looking up.
SHOT 8 (14–15s): Drone pull-back shot — the black panther continues sprinting at full speed through the jungle, same direction, same energy as the woman. Its golden-green eyes glow. The jungle swallows it whole. Cinematic 4K, photorealistic, seamless morphing transition, dark epic atmosphere.
