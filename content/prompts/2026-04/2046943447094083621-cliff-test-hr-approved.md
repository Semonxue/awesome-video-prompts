TITLE: “The Cliff Test (HR Approved)”
Seedance 2 | 15-second cinematic wuxia-comedy | snowy mountain trial | absurd dark humor | 1960s Shaw Brothers aesthetic | dramatic wind + exaggerated wire-action | punchy sound design | fast comedic timing

A snowy cliffside at dawn. Harsh wind howling. Snow drifting across jagged rocks. In the distance: a perfectly framed Mount Fuji backdrop, slightly too picturesque to be real.

An orange cat in a thin gray daoist robe shivers uncontrollably, arms wrapped around himself.

Beside him stands a black cat with long white eyebrows and beard, wearing a beige daoist robe, hands calmly clasped behind his back, posture confident and unmoving despite the freezing wind.

0–3 seconds:
Wide shot. Wind roars. Snow sweeps sideways.

Orange cat trembling violently. Teeth chattering.

Orange cat (muttering):
"I’m achieving enlightenment… through hypothermia."

Black cat slowly turns his head. Stern gaze.

Black cat (deep, serious):
"Are you ready… to become a Cat Master?"

Dramatic low-angle push-in.

3–6 seconds:
Close on orange cat, confused and suspicious.

Orange cat:
"Okay… what’s the catch?"

Black cat calmly raises one finger… points directly off the cliff.

Black cat:
"Jump."

Beat. Wind intensifies.

Orange cat blinks.

Orange cat:
"…that’s not a test, that’s a resignation letter."

He steps back in panic—
Accidentally gets closer to the cliff edge. Small rocks crumble and fall.

Orange cat (panicking):
"Yeah no—hard pass. I refuse!"

6–10 seconds:
Sudden shift.

Black cat’s eyes widen intensely.
Low bass hum builds.

He levitates slowly into the air, robes fluttering. Arms spread wide like an eagle.

Snow spirals upward unnaturally.

Black cat (cold, echoing):
"Refusal… is not in the curriculum."

He snaps his right hand forward—

A visible shockwave BLASTS toward the orange cat.

Impact hits—
Orange cat slides backward uncontrollably, feet scraping the icy ground.

He flails, right at the edge—
Half his body tipping into the abyss.

Orange cat (screaming):
"THIS FEELS VERY NON-OPTIONAL—"

10–13 seconds:
Suddenly—

LOUD helicopter blades.

From below the cliff, a helicopter rises violently into frame, snow exploding outward from the rotor wash.

Inside: a white cat with exaggerated blue eyeshadow, bright red lipstick, wearing a pink pilot uniform. Calm, focused.

She pilots one-handed—
With the other, she kicks out a rope ladder with precise timing.

White cat (cool, unimpressed):
"Get in. Before your life choices become permanent."

The orange cat instantly grabs the ladder mid-fall—

Now hanging off the side of the helicopter, feet still kicking above the abyss.

He scrambles upward frantically, clinging for dear life.

13–15 seconds:
Mid-air chaos.

Orange cat is still on the rope ladder, half-climbed—
Wind whipping his robe violently.

He twists his body, shouting down toward the still-levitating black cat below—

Orange cat (yelling, smug):
"Maybe next time!!"

The helicopter rapidly ascends and pulls away into the distance, shrinking into the snowy sky.

Cut to wide shot—

The black cat slowly descends from mid-air back onto the cliff.
Robes settle. Snow drifts calmly again.

He stands alone at the edge, watching the helicopter disappear far into the horizon.

Beat.

He turns to camera, expression tired, disappointed.

Black cat (sighing):
"Unteachable."

Soft wind.

Cut to black with classic comedic sting.
