title: Seedance Lip-Sync Prompting Tips
description: |-
  Spent the day diving into Seedance 2.0, focusing on sharp lip-sync without unwanted B-rolls.
  The Challenge: When using reference audio, the model sometimes alters the music or distorts lyrics, turning the song into "magic spell" gibberish.

  What worked for me:
  ✅Strict Prompting: Add to your prompt: RULES: The song is 100 % identical @audio_1. Perfectly lip-syncing. No text.
  ✅Language & Quotes: Specify the language and put your lyrics in double quotes (" ") for better clarity.
  ✅Timing: Use even audio segments (8, 9, 10 seconds, etc.) and match the duration exactly in Seedance 2.0 settings.
  ✅Syllable Trick: If the lip-sync looks "mushy," write out the words by syllables to force better articulation.

  Testing is still ongoing, but these steps significantly improved my results.
