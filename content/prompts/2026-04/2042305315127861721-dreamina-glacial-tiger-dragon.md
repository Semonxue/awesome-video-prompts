**Environment:**

A surreal floating mountain range above a sea of clouds under a pale indigo twilight. Massive ice pillars rise from the floating terrain while fragments of glacier drift slowly through the air as if gravity is weakened. The sky glows with soft aurora ribbons casting cool turquoise and violet diegetic light across the frozen landscape.

**Action:**

15.0s sequence. A colossal white tiger with luminous frost patterns across its fur stalks across the floating ice plateau. Opposing it is a crystalline dragon formed from fractured ice shards and refracting light. They collide in a rapid aerial and ground exchange as the dragon coils around the floating cliffs while the tiger leaps between suspended ice fragments.

Velocity Ramp choreography: the tiger launches into a massive mid-air pounce as time slows dramatically — frost crystals and snow particles freeze in mid-air before snapping back to full speed as the impact shatters a floating glacier slab.

**Camera:**

Low gliding tracking shot across the floating ice surface before tilting upward to follow the tiger's leap through drifting glacier fragments.

**Style & Constraints:**

Photorealistic fur simulation, ray-traced ice refraction, volumetric aurora lighting, cinematic cold atmosphere, 35mm film grain, 8K resolution.
