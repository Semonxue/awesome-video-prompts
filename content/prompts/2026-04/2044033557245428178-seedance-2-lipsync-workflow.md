  Lipsync with SeeDance 2 in Runway.

  At the moment you can't upload audio as a ref for SD2 in Runway but if you convert your audio track to a video with empty visuals, you can use this as a reference. (Just loading mp3 into Capcut and exporting works).

  Note: Even 720p vids get flagged as being too big and 15 secs is labelled as too long so I used a 10 second clip at 480p and it worked fine.

  SD2 did automatically sync to the audio but using a transcript appeared to improved mouth articulation.

  Ref image from Midjourney and audio from Suno.
