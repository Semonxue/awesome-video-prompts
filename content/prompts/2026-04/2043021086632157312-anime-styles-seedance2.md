  10 Anime Styles You Can Plug Into Seedance 2.0 (With Prompts) - Part 2

  Continuing the series. This time with 10 more anime styles. Also updated the style prompts I shared in Part 1.

  Prompts:

  PART II

  1. Seinen Urban Thriller Anime

  Apply a 1990s-to-2010s 2D animated seinen urban thriller anime treatment with restrained color, colder light, grounded anatomy, precise framing, and mature tension.

  2. Josei Slice-of-Life Anime

  Apply a 2000s-to-2010s 2D animated josei slice-of-life anime treatment with grounded adult styling, soft natural color, subtle acting, and emotionally observant realism.

  3. Magical Girl Cosmic Anime

  Apply a 1990s-to-2000s 2D animated magical girl cosmic anime treatment with luminous color, elegant motion flow, jewel-toned accents, clean emblematic shapes, and radiant clarity.

  4. Mecha Military Anime

  Apply a 1980s-to-2010s 2D animated mecha military anime treatment with precise technical linework, hard-surface shading, cool industrial color, tactical framing, and serious operational tone.

  5. Cyberpunk Neon Anime

  Apply a 1980s-to-2020s 2D animated cyberpunk neon anime treatment with electric color separation, glossy reflections, luminous night contrast, dense depth, and sleek alienated mood.

  6. Dark Fantasy Anime

  Apply a 1990s-to-2000s 2D animated dark fantasy anime treatment with cold contrast, ash-muted color, gothic silhouette logic, heavy atmosphere, and tragic severity.

  7. Korean Webtoon Action Drama

  Apply a 2010s-to-2020s 2D animated Korean webtoon action drama treatment with crisp digital linework, gradient-heavy lighting, sharp contrast, high readability, and sleek contemporary polish.

  8. Shojo Romance Anime

  Apply a 1990s-to-2000s 2D animated shojo romance anime treatment with airy pastel color, soft glow, delicate contours, graceful posing, and tender emotional softness.

  9. Iyashikei Healing Anime

  Apply a 2000s-to-2020s 2D animated iyashikei healing anime treatment with calming color, soft light, gentle atmosphere, and restorative stillness.

  10. French Bande Dessinee Animation

  Apply a 2000s-to-2010s hand-drawn 2D animated French bande dessinee animation treatment with graphic ink contour, painterly flat color, elegant album-style composition, controlled stylization, and witty visual rhythm.
