  Beat

  Sound: low supernatural hum

  The white cat's belly becomes semi-transparent—

  Inside: a tiny black bear baby… staring back with a terrifying, fully aware expression

  Its eyes narrow

  It LOCKS gaze with the orange cat

  Beat of silence

  8–9s
  Cut to orange cat's face

  His expression freezes… then shifts into pure awkward discomfort

  9–10s
  He turns slowly to the hyena

  "General… I'm gonna be honest with you."

  "That's… above my pay grade."

  He immediately turns and walks out

  No hesitation
