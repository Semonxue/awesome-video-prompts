Pixar 3D animated. Urban alley, graffiti walls, 
asphalt, sunny day. Hip-hop street battle vibe.

CHARACTER:
- Tiger: "TIGER CREW" black hoodie, baggy jeans, 
  TGR sneakers, intense focused expression, 
  pro breakdancer

SEQUENCE:
0-3s — ENTRY: Tiger walks in slow, 
head nodding to beat. 
Stops center street. 
Looks up. Smirks. 
Drops into stance — 
first hit lands with music.

3-6s — TOPROCK: Sharp arm waves, 
chest pops, shoulder isolations. 
Every move hits the beat exactly. 
Tail swinging as counterweight. 
Camera circles low around him.

6-9s — FOOTWORK: Drops to ground — 
rapid six-step footwork, 
legs blur in perfect rhythm, 
sneakers squeaking on asphalt. 
Freezes suddenly in perfect pose. 
One beat silence. Explodes back up.

9-12s — POWER MOVES: 
Windmill — body spinning 
on shoulders continuously. 
Into headspin — 
stripes blurring into ring. 
Pops up — 
backflip off one hand. 
Lands clean. Doesn't wobble.

12-15s — FINALE FREEZE: 
One-hand handstand, 
legs split perfectly in air. 
Holds it. 
Slowly turns head to camera. 
Raises one eyebrow. 
Drops down. 
Stands. Pulls hoodie straight. 
Walks away. Job done.

CAMERA: Low orbit on toprock, 
ground level on footwork, 
slow-mo on windmill, 
freeze frame on handstand, 
wide cool walk-away finale.

SOUND: Hip-hop beat throughout, 
sneaker squeaks, 
body hits on power moves, 
crowd reaction on freezes, 
beat drop on each power move.
