[0:00–0:02] A dark car bonnet punches through the glass frontage of a small jewelry shop. Glass fractures in a starburst pattern. Camera low, wide angle. Neon purple and cyan light floods through the shattering glass. Slow motion, approx 15–20% speed.

[0:02–0:06] 
@somrat
-2 is launched through the shattering windshield in a dark green jacket. Eyes wide open, expression of raw shock. Head tilted back, one hand braced near the face, body reclined. Glass fragments explode outward in 3D arcs. Gold and silver jewelry chains spiral through the air. Camera begins a slow orbital arc rotating clockwise around the subject — starting from the right side, sweeping to the front. Wide lens, shallow DOF. Hyper slow motion, approx 10–15% speed. Neon cyan and magenta light rakes across the glass debris.

[0:06–0:09] Camera completes the arc, now facing 
@somrat
-2 front-on. His shocked face fills the frame. Glass shards hang suspended mid-air around him. Neon light splits across his face — cyan on one side, hot pink on the other. Camera still rotating slightly, almost settling. Jewelry chains drift in slow motion through the neon-lit air.

[0:09–0:11] Camera locks front-on. Extreme close-up on 
@somrat
-2's face. Eyes wide, frozen in shock. Final glass fragments drift past. Hard cut to black.

Style: hyper-stylized slow-motion crash, orbital arc camera move, neon cyberpunk lighting — cyan, magenta, purple, no warm tones.
