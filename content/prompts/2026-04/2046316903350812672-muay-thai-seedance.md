Audio: Raw spatial audio including loud crowd cheering, heavy breathing, the "thud" of skin-to-skin impacts, and the referee's shouting. 

[IMAGE REFERENCES / LEGEND] :

The exact bald man with the dark beard from the photo [ref], acting as the main fighter. Keep exact facial features and physical build throughout the scene.

[TIMELINE SECOND BY SECOND]

0-3s: Wide handheld shot. the man is in a Muay Thai stance. A muscular Thai opponent lands a powerful roundhouse kick to [ref]'s ribs. [ref]body reacts realistically to the physics of the impact, and he collapses to the canvas.

3-7s: The camera shakes as the spectator moves. The opponent dives onto [ref] , attempting a rear-naked choke (mataleón). Intense ground struggle; [ref]'s face shows strain while maintaining consistency.

7-11s: Continuous shot. [ref] zafs out of the choke, rolls his weight, and locks the opponent's arm in a transition move. The opponent is pinned.

11-15s: The referee steps in quickly to stop the fight. He pulls the fighters apart and raises [ref] 's hand towards the crowd. The camera pans slightly and shakes as the "spectator" cheers.

[STYLE & QUALITY BOOSTERS] Mobile phone recording style,  (amateur look), realistic physics
