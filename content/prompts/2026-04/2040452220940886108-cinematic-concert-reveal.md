A cinematic concert gacha reveal in portrait orientation.

Phase 1 (2s): Dark arena with scattered blue-white penlight dots. Vertical Japanese text of the character's catchphrase reveals rapidly character-by-character with soft glow. Camera orbits above the audience.

Phase 2 (2.5s): Camera swings toward the stage. 8-10 vertical spotlight beams descend from trusses. Steady zoom in reveals spherical footlights along the stage edge. A rainbow-colored ring burst explodes at center with multicolored star-shaped particles scattering outward.

Phase 3 (3.5s): Stage reappears with an elaborate gothic arch structure. Penlights multiply into a vibrant rainbow sea below. On the large screen behind the stage, a long-haired character silhouette slowly materializes. Camera creeps closer. The glow intensifies into a massive four-pointed star flash, then instant cut to total black.

Phase 4 (3s): A blinding white flash erupts. The stage returns dramatically upgraded — golden fireworks shoot upward from both sides of the arch, backdrop transforms into stained-glass geometric patterns in pink, purple, blue and gold. The character materializes from silhouette to full color on center stage. Crossing laser beams sweep from the sides.

Phase 5 (4s): Screen floods white, resolves into a close-up dolly sweeping right-to-left past colorful spotlights with dense rainbow penlights waving below. Camera pulls back to center revealing the full arena — arch stage, stained glass screen, character center, thousands of rainbow penlights. Final frame: character illustration in a card frame with name signature.
