Camera drops from space toward a burning California ridge. Fire burns through the treetops at 80km/h. Below in the forest a herd of elk runs hard, the fire moving through the trees above their heads, gaining on them. Ground level tracking fast beside the elk, hooves hitting dry ground, bodies pushing through bushes, burning embers falling around them from above as they run. The herd reaches the cleared firebreak strip and crosses it fast. Above them the fire jumps the gap, a wall of flame crossing overhead. Wide shot from above, the elk pour out of the trees into an open meadow and spread out. Wide aerial pulling up. The elk herd scattered across the open meadow, safe.
