Duration: 10 Seconds
Setting: An aerial view from a news helicopter hovering above the Hudson River at dusk. The dark, massive metallic structure of the starship dominates the frame, set against the twilight sky and the Lower Manhattan skyline (including the Freedom Tower), where city lights are just beginning to activate.
Atmosphere: Awestruck, ominous, and deeply mysterious.

Action Breakdown:

0:00 - 0:02 (The Dormant Giant): The shot opens on the seemingly lifeless vessel, catching only the last glints of the setting sun on its upper armor. The camera begins a slow, smooth orbit around the bow of the ship.

0:02 - 0:08 (The Awakening): The ship begins an incredibly slow, ponderous forward drift. Simultaneously, cascading waves of warm-yellow window lights activate across the hull, sweeping from bow to stern. A massive hangar bay door on the port side slides open, revealing a brilliantly lit, complex interior.

0:08 - 0:10 (Scale and Motion): The camera completes its orbit, creating a dynamic parallax effect that contrasts the camera's rotational movement with the ship's linear drift, emphasizing its colossal scale as bright floodlights and pulsing navigation beacons fully activate.

Lighting and Design Notes:

The ship features a mix of lights: small, warm-yellow window lights, bright white floodlights, and pulsing red/blue navigational beacons. The domed bridge at the top emits a unique soft blue "command glow."

The overall design is heavily armored, utilitarian, and slightly menacing—resembling a mobile fortress or colony ship.
