Create a cinematic 15-shot video sequence centered around the Eiffel Tower, capturing a full emotional arc from calm elegance to dramatic grandeur.
Style & Tone:
Ultra-realistic, cinematic, rich contrast, soft film grain, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens flares, golden-hour + night transitions, moody Parisian atmosphere.

 Shot List:
Aerial drone shot approaching Paris at sunrise, soft golden light hitting rooftops.
Slow reveal of the Eiffel Tower emerging through morning mist.
Close-up of intricate iron lattice with sunlight piercing through.
Wide shot from Trocadéro with a lone subject walking into frame.
Low-angle hero shot emphasizing scale and dominance.
Time-lapse of clouds drifting behind the tower.
Romantic couple walking under the tower, soft bokeh lights.
POV walking through a Paris street toward the tower in distance.
Rainy scene — wet streets reflecting the tower lights at dusk.
Macro shot of raindrops hitting pavement, tower blurred in background.
Transition to night — tower begins to sparkle.
Cinematic orbit shot around the illuminated tower.
Silhouette of a subject looking up, emotional tone.
Fireworks or light shimmer intensifying the spectacle.
Final wide aerial pull-away into the night skyline of Paris.
 Visual Direction:
Lighting: natural → golden hour → neon/night glow
Camera: slow dolly, crane, drone, smooth tracking
Color grade: warm highlights, teal shadows, cinematic LUT
Mood: romantic, grand, slightly nostalgic
 Settings:
Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (cinematic landscape)
FPS: 24fps
Motion: smooth, stabilized, film-like
Detail: ultra-high, realistic textures
