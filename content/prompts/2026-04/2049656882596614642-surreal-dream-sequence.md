15-second ultra-surreal dream sequence. A small old house drifts slowly through the night, carried on the back of a dark cloud above a forest. Inside and outside blend together. Wide shot: stars hang low around the roof like lanterns. Medium shot: a woman walks through the front door and enters a moonlit meadow instead of a room. Side tracking shot: a man stands by a dining table set in the grass, waiting with one candle burning blue. Close-up: when she touches the flame, it becomes a tiny glowing sun and the tableware begins orbiting gently in the air. Overhead shot: the forest below opens into a spiral pattern like a giant dream symbol. Medium shot: they sit together, but their chairs slowly drift apart across the meadow even as they keep reaching for one another. Final shot: the cloud dissolves, the house becomes a flock of white birds, and the two of them remain suspended in the dark sky under a huge pulsing moon. Tone: emotional, romantic, abstract, eerie yet serene.
