Create a hyper-cinematic, emotionally charged 14-second in ultra-realistic 4K, 24 fps, with a strong slow-motion/time-dilation feel throughout. Cinematic color grading: cool desaturated blues, grays and soft whites, high contrast on the subject's face, subtle film grain, anamorphic lens flares, shallow depth of field, and gentle snowfall bokeh. Heavy atmospheric snow particles falling continuously, wind-blown snow, soft diffused overcast winter light, melancholic and dreamy mood.

A handsome young man in his early-mid 20s in @/image1. He wears a long grey wool overcoat (double-breasted, slightly open), black turtleneck sweater, black slim pants, black combat boots, black gloves, and large black over-ear headphones (JBL-style). He has a quiet, lonely, introspective expression that shifts to quiet intensity.

Precise shot-by-shot sequence with timing (match exactly):

0.00–1.00s  
Extreme close-up, low angle: Black headphones half-buried in fresh powdery snow, snow gently accumulating on them. In the soft background, a black boot is visible. Gentle wind and falling snow. Slow, intimate camera push-in.

1.00–2.00s  
Wide establishing shot of a vast, empty snow-covered field under a pale grey sky with distant snow-laden trees. The man is in the middle distance, bent over at the waist, gloved hand reaching down toward the snow as if picking something up. Slow, majestic camera movement.

2.00–3.00s  
Tight close-up on the man's face. Gloved hands run through his hair, brushing snow away. He looks slightly to the side with a melancholic gaze. Snowflakes land on his face and coat.

3.00–5.00s  
Extreme close-up, handheld intimate style. He slowly places the black headphones over his ears. Snow continues to fall heavily on his face and hair. His eyes blink slowly, expression vulnerable and lost in the music. Subtle slow-motion on the snowflakes and his facial micro-expressions. Camera circles gently around his face.

5.00–6.00s  
Medium full shot. The man stands upright in deep snow, head slightly bowed, hands clasped in front of him. Snow swirls around him. He slowly lifts his head.

6.00–8.00s  
Dramatic wide shot. He suddenly throws his arms wide open in a powerful, wing-like gesture. At the exact moment his arms reach full extension, massive, majestic black feathered angel wings (raven-black, detailed individual feathers with subtle iridescence, slightly damaged/broken aesthetic) erupt and unfurl explosively from his back. A burst of black feathers and dark feather particles scatter violently outward in slow motion, carried by the wind. His coat flares dramatically. He stands tall, legs apart, head held high, eyes open with quiet intensity. Camera slowly pushes in while maintaining the wide heroic framing.

8.00–10.00s  
Smooth 180° camera rotation around him to show his back. Wings fully spread, snow settling on the black feathers. He slowly turns his head and upper body away from camera, looking into the white void. Powerful, lonely silhouette against the bright snowy background.

10.00–11.00s  
Quick cut back to front wide shot. He is still standing with arms outstretched and massive black wings spread wide. Black feathers continue to drift around him. Intense eye contact with camera for a brief moment.

11.00–14.00s  
The environment rapidly changes: thick white fog and heavy blizzard roll in violently. The man becomes a dark, increasingly silhouetted figure in the dense snowstorm. His wings remain dramatically spread as he slowly raises his arms higher in a final, almost sacrificial pose. Visibility drops dramatically until he is almost completely obscured by swirling snow and fog, turning into a ghostly, ethereal silhouette. Final shot fades into pure whiteout with only the faint outline of the winged figure visible. Slow, dreamy camera pull-back.

**Camera style:** Mix of smooth cinematic pushes, gentle orbits, and subtle handheld intimacy. Heavy use of slow-motion (especially on wing emergence, feather scatter, and snow particles). Perfect motion coherence.
Lighting & atmosphere: Soft, diffused natural winter light with high contrast rim lighting on the subject and wings. Volumetric god rays through the fog at the end. Extremely realistic physics on snow, feathers, fabric, and hair movement.
Overall aesthetic references: Cinematic music video style like The Weeknd meets Hozier meets "Black Angel" concept art. High production value, emotionally devastating yet beautiful, perfect for Instagram Reels/TikTok cinematic edits.
