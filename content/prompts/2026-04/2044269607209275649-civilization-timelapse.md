[CINEMATIC SETUP]

Film Style: Photorealistic 8K, 65mm IMAX. One continuous forward dolly — no cuts. Landscape transforms around a steadily advancing camera as if walking through 10,000 years.

Lens: 24mm wide-angle, shallow horizon.

Color Grade: Ochre to blood-red to emerald to gold to steel-blue, each era bleeding into the next.

Lighting: Golden hour shifting to overcast to firelight to electric — the light itself ages with civilization.

Camera: Constant slow forward dolly at chest height. No lateral movement, no tilt.

[TIMELINE — CONTINUOUS FORWARD DOLLY]

0-2s: RIVER VALLEY. Glide over cracked mud beside the Tigris. Mud-brick walls rise on both sides in accelerated construction. An irrigation channel fills with brown water. Grain stalks shoot to full height.

2-4s: WARFARE. Mud walls crumble and reform as stone fortifications. Fields scorch black. Bronze spearheads jut from the ground. A watchtower assembles ahead then fractures as the camera passes through dust.

4-6s: AFRICAN EMPIRES. Dust clears to red laterite soil. Gold trade goods glint in market stalls assembling as camera passes. A Great Zimbabwe stone wall curves into view, dry-stacked blocks fitting together. Baobab trees grow from saplings to full canopy.

6-8s: SOUTH AMERICA. Laterite morphs to grey Andean stone. Inca terraces step upward on both sides greening with crops. Stone steps assemble beneath the path. A temple facade emerges — carved faces forming from rough rock.

8-10s: INDUSTRIAL. Stone becomes cobblestone. Brick factories replace timber buildings. A rail track lays itself down the center path. Smokestacks grow, emitting pale plumes. Gas lamps ignite in sequence.

10-13s: MODERN. Cobblestone becomes asphalt. Glass towers rise on both sides. Headlight streaks blur past. Neon signage illuminates. Contrails scratch across the sky. More glass, more light, more vertical.

13-15s: LAUNCH. Towers fall away. Open concrete ahead. A rocket on a launch pad. Ignition — white exhaust erupts downward. The rocket rises out of frame. Camera advances into dissipating exhaust revealing black sky and stars.

[QUALITY]

Photorealistic 8K. Each era architecturally distinct — mud, stone, brick, steel, glass. Transformations bridge through shared shapes: walls become walls, roads become roads. No duplicates. Consistent forward momentum. Gradual lighting transitions.
