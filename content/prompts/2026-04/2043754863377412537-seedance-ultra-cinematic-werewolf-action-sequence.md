Ultra-cinematic 15-second multi-angle action sequence in a dark city street at night, wet asphalt reflecting streetlights, a massive werewolf rampaging through civilians.

0–2s: Wide establishing shot, empty street with a few pedestrians, distant fog and flickering streetlights, subtle tension.
2–3.5s: Close-up on the werewolf emerging from shadows, glowing eyes, low growl, slight camera push-in.
3.5–5s: Hard cut to handheld medium shot as the creature charges forward, heavy footsteps splashing water, people reacting and turning to run.
5–6.5s: Side tracking shot, the werewolf swings its arm in a wide arc, making powerful kinetic contact — a person is launched into the air across frame.
6.5–8s: Slow-motion shot, body spinning mid-air above wet pavement, reflections stretching, debris and droplets suspended in air.
8–10s: Low angle shot, the creature advancing aggressively, another impact sends multiple people sliding and lifting off the ground from force.
10–11.5s: Overhead drone-style shot, crowd scattering in all directions across the street, cars stopped, doors open, chaos spreading.
11.5–13s: Fast whip pan to close-up impact, strong contact motion, particles, water spray, and debris bursting outward.
13–15s: Dynamic tracking backward shot, the werewolf continues advancing toward camera, breathing heavy, final aggressive step as people flee in the background.

Environment: wet reflective asphalt, streetlights casting long reflections, light fog, scattered trash and objects reacting to motion.

Style: ultra-cinematic realism, high-impact action editing, dramatic cuts, slow-motion accents, heavy motion blur, volumetric atmosphere, no text, no overlays, stable proportions.
