Hyper sleek neon soaked sci-fi city at night after rain, with reflective streets, electric billboards, dense vertical traffic, and sharp cinematic contrast. A fugitive courier carrying a glowing data core sprints across suspended transit bridges while armed drones and black armored interceptors close in from every direction. The camera starts in a tight handheld style close to the courier's shoulder as they push through a crowded market, then snaps into a fast side tracking shot as they leap onto a moving train roof. It races backward in front of the courier while drones open fire behind, sparks exploding from steel beams and glass panels. The camera then drops straight down in a dizzying top shot as the courier slides under rotating turbine vents, then swings outward into a huge aerial orbit as they jump from the train into a neon canyon between skyscrapers and deploy a magnetic grapple. Climax: the camera spins with the courier as they swing through a holographic ad tunnel and crash through a penthouse window. Ending: the camera slowly glides forward through drifting glass shards as the courier places the glowing core on a silent table, city lights flickering across their face.
