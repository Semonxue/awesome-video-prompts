An epic 15-second ancient war field under harsh daylight, with bronze armor flashing, sand and dust storms rolling low, spears colliding, and cavalry breaking through infantry lines. A royal archer stands on a broken stone platform in side silhouette. The camera starts in a wide horizontal track far across the battlefield, passing waves of fighters before reaching him as he pulls and releases. The arrow bursts forward. The camera tracks beside it from a horizontal side angle, close enough to see the shaft trembling as it slices through dust and sunlight. It races toward a wounded commander kneeling in the open, helmet fallen off, unable to move. The arrow closes fast, every frame increasing tension. Final epic ending: just before impact, a massive armored guard lunges into frame and slams his shield in front of the commander. The arrow drives deep into the shield face with violent force, stopping a breath away from the man behind it. The guard drops to one knee under the impact while the saved commander looks up in disbelief.
