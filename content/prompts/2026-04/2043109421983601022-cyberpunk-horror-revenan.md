VISUAL STYLE: Cyberpunk realism, green shadows, reduced saturation, wet reflections, clean digital finish.
CHARACTERS: Armored courier, cracked respirator, low baton. Eight-legged mechanical-organic revenant with fractured porcelain face, cable hair, chrome limbs, wet sinew, red pupils, spider-head inside split shell.
STAGE: Shuttered pachinko hall, mirrored ceiling, ankle-deep water, emerald spill, exit lamps, steam, aisle to the elevator threshold.
EMOTIONAL TARGET: Hush into pursuit terror, then capture.
TIMELINE:
00:00.0: Open on WS dolly down the aisle. Courier wades forward, baton low, hand on machines. SFX: rain.
00:02.0: Cut to 50mm mirrored insert, then snap to the visor. Revenant streaks across the reflection, skims across the glass, then vanishes on the turn. SFX: chatter, static.
00:04.8: Cut to overhead 24mm over cabinet row. Revenant unfolds, taps the aisle, climbs back up, and skitters onto ceiling seams ahead of the courier. SFX: clicks, skitter.
00:07.0: Cut on action to 28mm track. Courier sees it and backpedals while the revenant blocks the aisle above him, then drops from the ceiling seams into his path. SFX: slap, shriek, splash.
00:09.6: Cut to bird's-eye 24mm. Revenant bursts down center line, shatters neon, forces the courier to stumble short, then grinds its porcelain face open as the wet spider-head pushes forward from the mechanical shell. SFX: bulb pops, screech, grind.
00:11.3: Smash cut to 35mm short of the elevator. Courier lunges for the opening; the revenant hooks torso and shoulder, yanks him back. SFX: crack, slam.
00:13.2: Push to medium close 50mm before the elevator. Opened halves frame the spider-head as the revenant clamps the throat, drags him in, shakes him, lunges closer. SFX: breath, armor rattle.
EXCLUDE: Avoid unreadable aisle geography, extra legs beyond eight, early full-face reveal, heroic fight beats, text glitches, daylight contamination, slow safe coverage.
