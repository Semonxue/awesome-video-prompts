SHOT 1 (0:00–0:02) — The Touch
Extreme macro, slow push-in at 70% speed. Two marble cherubs lean toward each other, fingertips nearly meeting. Dark organic forms swirl behind them. Rack focus from background to fingertips. Single cold rim light carves the marble detail from near-black shadow. Faint halo arc pulses at frame-left. Slow upward tilt wipe out.
SHOT 2 (0:02–0:04) — The Inscribed Back
Vertical tilt reveal with 10° orbital creep. A powerful marble figure seen from behind — broad shoulders, cascading hair, intricate text carved across the back. Camera starts low and tilts upward, drifting slightly rightward. Frame rotates approximately 5° clockwise with motion blur on edges. Halo ring glows behind the head. A vertical golden line bisects frame-right. Depth-of-field smear pull out.
SHOT 3 (0:04–0:06) — The Angel and the Elder
Wide pull-back, locked horizontal axis. Small winged angel in foreground; massive bearded elder looms in background right. Scale contrast is extreme — divine hierarchy made physical. Camera begins tight on the angel, pulls back to reveal the elder. Halo on the angel pulses once in soft bloom, then settles. Hard cut on central axis.
SHOT 4 (0:06–0:09) — Golden Geometry Descends (Signature Sequence)
Speed ramp to 20% slowmo. Locked centre axis with slow scale-out. The winged figure sits bathed in a descending vertical light column. Golden geometric lines — cross, radiating spokes, halo ring — draw outward from the figure's core in real time like a divine blueprint materialising. This is the defining visual of the sequence. Light beam intensifies into bloom flash out.
SHOT 5 (0:09–0:11) — The Seated Figure
Gentle push-in, medium to medium-close. Locked vertical axis. The central winged figure — seated, hands clasped, head bowed — catches a single overhead beam on white marble. Halo glows once behind the head, then dims. Residual golden glow from Shot 4 lingers on the figure's chest before fading. Stillness. Slow pull-back out.
SHOT 6 (0:11–0:15) — The Grand Tableau
Ultra-wide reveal. Imperceptible push-in at 10% speed. The full scene: central seated figure flanked by churning masses of marble angels, warriors, and divine forms receding into deep shadow. Golden geometric mandala blooms fully around the figure. Vertical light column holds. Golden lines pulse once in unison, then hold. The world breathes. Slow fade to black.
