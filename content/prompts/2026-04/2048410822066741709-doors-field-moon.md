A child walks through an open field at night under a giant low moon.

Wide shot: hundreds of freestanding doors are scattered across the grass, gently swaying in the wind.

Front tracking shot: each door opens by itself as the child passes, revealing different impossible interiors — a swimming pool, a desert road, a room full of clocks, a snowy balcony.

Top-down shot: the field becomes a grid of moonlit rectangles.

Close-up: the child touches one brass handle and stars spill from the keyhole.

Final wide shot: all the doors rise into the air like lanterns, leaving the child alone in a field now filled with shallow glowing water reflecting the moon.

Tone: innocent, mysterious, celestial, calm
