0-3s (Ink Strike): A heavy brush boldly strikes textured paper. The camera dives straight into the splash, which dynamically splinters into hundreds of fast-weaving wet lines.

9-12s (Parabolic Swoop): The camera swoops in a rapid parabolic arc as the paper backdrop lifts away, revealing the 3D ink-wash sneaker spinning in mid-air.
