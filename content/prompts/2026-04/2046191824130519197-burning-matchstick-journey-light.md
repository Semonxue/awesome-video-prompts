A tiny burning matchstick with a glowing flame stands on a wooden table in a dark room, cinematic macro style. The matchstick immediately starts moving as its flame flickers brightly, casting warm light in darkness. It quickly passes by a glass of water reflecting like a glowing ocean. Suddenly strong wind from a ceiling fan blows, the flame flickers violently creating tension. The matchstick continues and briefly sees burned-out matchsticks lying lifeless. Its flame becomes smaller as it struggles, then it reaches a candle and lights it. Final moment shows the candle burning brightly in the dark room with a warm cinematic glow. Ultra realistic, dramatic lighting, shallow depth of field, volumetric light, emotional storytelling, 4K.
