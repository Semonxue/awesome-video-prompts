[FORMAT] 16:9, cinematic multi-shot scene, slow tension build into chaos

[SCENE] Massive burning castle in the background to the left. undead moving right to left. flames and black smoke rising into the night sky, ash and embers falling. Desperate last stand.

[CHARACTERS] A line of battle-worn human warriors in dark medieval armor, shields raised, swords drawn, exhausted and overwhelmed but holding their ground.

A vast horde of undead soldiers in torn dark armor, blue-grey decomposed flesh, hollow dead eyes, relentless and unstoppable, absorbing every blow without slowing.

[CAMERA SYSTEM] the huge army of undead attacks the army of warriors who are defined the castle. the humans are pulled apart, slaughtered, afraid. they try to escape but can't.
