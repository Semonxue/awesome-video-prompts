Anime cinematic style inspired by Makoto Shinkai, warm sunlight, soft glow, high detail food textures, shallow depth of field, smooth transitions. 15 seconds, multi-angle (top-down + side + close-up), woman’s face and hands always visible.

A young anime woman cooking calmly, expressive face, soft smile, natural movements.

Sequence:
Eggs + sugar whisked fluffy → butter + vanilla added → flour sifted and folded → batter into pan → oven glow → sponge out and cooled → sliced into 3 layers → cream piped → strawberries added → second layer + cream + cherries → final layer → full cream coat → strawberry sauce poured dripping → final decoration with strawberries, cherries, mint.

Camera alternates top view + close-ups of hands + face reactions.
Sounds: whisk, pour, oven hum, slicing, piping, sauce.

Negative: no faceless shots, no only top-down, no low detail, no bad hands, no cartoonish look.
