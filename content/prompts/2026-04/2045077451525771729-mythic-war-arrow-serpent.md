A vast mythic war plain. A warrior-prince stands in side profile atop a stone chariot, calm amid chaos. The camera starts far away in a horizontal battlefield sweep, then closes on him as he releases a glowing blue arrow toward the dark sky. The camera tracks beside the arrow in fast lateral motion as it tears through wind, clouds, and divine thunder. The arrow suddenly expands into a spinning serpent of water. Final epic ending: the serpent-arrow curls across the heavens and bursts open into a river suspended in the sky, pouring down in dozens of giant streams over the battlefield, drowning enemy fire trenches, sweeping away siege towers, and forming a shimmering wall of water between armies as lightning flashes through it.
