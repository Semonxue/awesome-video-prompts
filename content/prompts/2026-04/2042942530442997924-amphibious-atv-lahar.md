Vehicle: Amphibious ATV (Argo style)
Threat: Volcanic mudflow (lahar)
Setting: Amazon foothills of Cotopaxi, rainy dusk, green‑grey light

[0s–1.5s] Wide drone shot skimming above jungle canopy. A 6x6 vehicle plows through a river of grey mud, trees sliding past like floating islands.

[1.5s–3s] Close-up of the driver's hand switching from wheels to propeller. Mud splatters the camera. His eyes wide, checking a side mirror – a wall of debris approaching.

[3s–5s] A bridge collapses behind him – swallowed by the lahar. A mud‑covered boulder the size of a car tumbles past. Camera tracks backward as the flow accelerates.

[5s–7s] The vehicle hits a submerged tree trunk ramp. Slow motion – airborne, propeller spinning, mud tentacles dripping from the chassis, green canopy below.

[7s–9s] It lands hard on a clay riverbank. The ground cracks in mud‑dried patterns. Camera from below the clay looking up at the impact splitting the surface.

[9s–11s] The lahar front rolls over the bank – a slurry of rocks and uprooted trees. He drives blind into the brown fog, only the sound of debris scraping metal.

[11s–13s] Camera cuts to a waterfall cliff. He bursts out of the mud cloud and launches off the cliff into open air above a lower jungle tier.

[13s–15s] Final shot from the jungle floor looking up. Silhouette of the 6x6 against green‑grey storm clouds, falling toward a black river. Cut to black.
