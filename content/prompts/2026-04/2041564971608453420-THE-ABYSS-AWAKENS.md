A forgotten underwater civilization lies buried in the deepest ocean trench — ancient stone-tech ruins, massive pillars, and colossal structures stretching endlessly into darkness.

0–3s:
Wide descending shot, camera slowly sinking into a dark ocean abyss, faint sunlight fading above, thousands of bioluminescent particles drifting like glowing stars.
3–6s:

6–10s:
Smooth forward glide between towering ruins, collapsed arches and broken statues half-buried in sediment, glowing sea creatures drifting through the structures.
10–15s:

15–18s:

22–26s:

26–30s:
Final cinematic moment — the abyss suddenly darkens. A colossal shadow moves deep below the trench, and two enormous glowing shapes slowly ignite in the darkness.
