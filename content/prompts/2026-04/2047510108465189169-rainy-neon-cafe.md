Ultra-cinematic seamless one-take shot, 15 seconds total.
Start with a slow descending aerial shot in a rainy night city. Neon lights glow through light fog. Wet streets reflect blue and purple tones. Camera gently glides downward through rain, soft droplets visible on lens.
At 3 seconds, camera transitions to street level, moving smoothly past blurred cars and glowing shop signs. Reflections shimmer on the wet ground. Ambient rain and distant traffic sounds.
At 6 seconds, camera turns a corner and reveals a neon-lit outdoor café named "LUMOS". The sign glows softly in blue and pink tones. Rain continues lightly, with visible droplets hitting surfaces.
At 9 seconds, camera slows down and pushes toward the café seating area. Tables are slightly wet, soft fog in air, cinematic lighting with strong reflections.
At 11 seconds, camera locks onto a young woman sitting alone under light rain protection. She wears a slightly oversized shirt. A half-eaten burger in her hand, drink placed on the table with droplets on the glass.
At 13 seconds, camera settles into a natural medium close-up shot. She takes a slow bite, eyes calm and slightly distant. Background bokeh lights shimmer softly.
Lighting: deep blue and neon tones, soft fog, volumetric light, wet reflections, cinematic contrast.
Lens: starts wide → transitions to 85mm cinematic look.
Motion: smooth glide, slow deceleration, no cuts.
Audio: soft rain → distant city ambience → subtle café sounds → quiet intimate mood.
Style: photorealistic, ultra-detailed, shallow depth of field, film grain, 8K, moody, emotional.
