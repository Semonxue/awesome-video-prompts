Movie-level Porsche 911 GT3 brand ad, hyper-realistic, first-person aerial dive to ground-level speed, extreme aerodynamic precision, continuous silky camera. Vibe: clinical, precise, German, lethal efficiency. A peregrine falcon's 240mph hunting stoop becomes the GT3's aerodynamic form. No music — only wind, feather, mechanical foley. 15 seconds, visual upgrade every 3 seconds.

【0-3s】ALTITUDE. Camera locked to a peregrine falcon's wing-shoulder in first-person, looking along its body axis. Climbing. Cold mountain air. Then — prey spotted. Wings FOLD. Dive begins. INSTANTLY speed triples. Camera plunges WITH the bird — stomach dropping. Face-close to the falcon's tucked body — feathers pressed tight, perfect aerodynamic teardrop. Wind SCREAMING.

【3-5s】EXTREME close-up falcon's eye during dive — amber, focused, pupil tracking micro-adjustments. Ground rushing up reflected. The eye's curvature, iris ring structure, pupil precision TRANSITIONS into the GT3's headlight lens. Amber eye becomes amber turn signal. Pupil becomes projector beam.

【5-8s】GROUND LEVEL. GT3 in motion — first-person camera racing its flank at ground level. The front fender's curve = falcon's tucked wing. Side intake breathing like lungs. Roofline tapering = falcon's body profile. Signature round headlights — owl-like, predatory.

【8-11s】Camera drops LOW — center-lock wheel spinning, carbon-ceramic disc, suspension geometry through spokes. Then OVER the MASSIVE rear wing — swan-neck mounts, Gurney flap, air flowing over and under. Visible downforce pressing car INTO road.

【11-13s】Full reveal — GT3 in high-speed sweeping turn, perfectly flat, zero roll. The silhouette IS the falcon's stoop — low nose, rising haunch, wing spread. Nothing wasted, nothing decorative, everything FUNCTIONAL.

【13-15s】Hero lock. GT3 low front-three-quarter. Round headlights = falcon's eyes. Rear wing high. Engine ticking as it cools. Text: PORSCHE 911 GT3 "Engineered by nature. Perfected by Porsche."
