## Full Prompt

[Global style: warm cinematic, shallow DOF, golden tones, documentary, soft ambient bakery sounds]

[11s–15s]: Wide shot — slow dolly out. Full bakery revealed, warm and alive, first customers arriving, low chatter, footsteps, light door chime, ambient bakery bustle.
