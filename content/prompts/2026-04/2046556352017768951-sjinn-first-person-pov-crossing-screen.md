First-person POV, handheld cinematic shot of a man at a gaming desk. A curved ultrawide monitor dominates the view, showing a pale woman with silver-white hair, wearing a thorn crown and blindfold, standing among snowy rocks. Deep red ambient lighting fills the room, contrasting with the cool blue-white glow of the screen. Desk details: white-pink mechanical keyboard, pink headphones, small cube decor. Natural hand visible near keyboard.

The woman suddenly turns her head toward the viewer. Subtle camera flinch. She slowly raises her hand. The man reaches toward the screen. Fingers touch—monitor surface ripples like liquid with a soft glow.

Her blindfold cracks and falls, revealing glowing pale eyes. She grabs his hand.

As he pulls back, her arm breaks through the screen—transitioning from flat digital texture into realistic 3D form. Skin gains depth, shadows, and subsurface scattering. Glitch particles and glass shards float around the breach.

She emerges further—shoulders entering the real world. Hair falls naturally, catching red light. The screen edge becomes a shimmering membrane with volumetric rays and distortion.

Hyperrealistic, cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, intense immersion, smooth motion, realistic physics.
