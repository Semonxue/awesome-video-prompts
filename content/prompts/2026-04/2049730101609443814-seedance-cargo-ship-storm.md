A man in a red rescue suit runs along the deck of a huge cargo ship in the midst of a severe storm in the North Atlantic. A large wave is approaching in the distance. Waves crash against the deck, and containers sway dangerously in the wind. At the 2nd second, a giant wave hits the ship, tipping the entire deck to one side. The man slides across the metal surface before grabbing onto the railing. He climbs onto the container and jumps to the rescue helicopter hovering next to the ship.
