Retro 80s Cel Anime:

STYLE: 2D, Retro 80s cel anime: thick cel layers, airbrushed highlights, bold primaries, analog grain, smoky backgrounds, classic VHS-era energy.

SCENE: At night, on a city street under a steady, gentle rain, a lone character walks slowly toward the camera along the sidewalk with a thoughtful, heavy pace. The wet asphalt reflects the faint glow of streetlights, and small puddles ripple softly with each step. The camera remains mostly static, positioned at eye level, holding a frontal view as the character approaches from a short distance. Hair and light fabric move subtly with the wind, while raindrops gather along the shoulders and at the tips of the hair. The character slows for a brief moment, tilts their head slightly upward, and looks ahead, their face lit only by the cool overhead light. There is a quiet sense of fatigue and contemplation in their expression. As they come slightly closer into frame, a faint breath becomes visible in the air, then fades as they continue walking forward. The sound of rain blends with the distant rush of passing cars cutting through water, maintaining a calm, uninterrupted atmosphere throughout the scene.
