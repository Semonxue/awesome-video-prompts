A cinematic wild-animal action sequence in one continuous epic shot. Start with an ultra-wide aerial view above a stormy jungle canyon at dawn, mist pouring through the trees, waterfalls crashing into a river below, golden light cutting through dark clouds. On opposite cliffs, a massive tiger and a giant black gorilla lock eyes across the gap, both tense, powerful, and silent. The camera dives fast between them as thunder rolls. The tiger leaps first, using a fallen tree as a bridge, while the gorilla smashes through vines and rock to meet it. They collide on a narrow cliff ledge in a burst of dust, leaves, and flying stone. The camera circles tightly as they fight with speed and force, slipping near the edge while rain begins falling harder.
