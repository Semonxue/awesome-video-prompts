FORMAT: 15s / 146 BPM / 6 CUTS / 3rd person, pop-up builds, vertical combat

SUBJECTS: Two realistic female raiders with bold silhouettes, styled as high-detail Fortnite characters. One wears a sleek black tactical helmet, a futuristic teal armored vest with glowing accents, bright orange utility straps, and glossy white combat boots. The other wears a stylized sand-toned combat jacket with exaggerated shoulder pads, braided hair with neon threads, magenta highlights across her gear, and vibrant yellow high-top sneakers.
ENVIRONMENT: A stylized suburban zone rendered in the vibrant, slightly exaggerated aesthetic of Fortnite. Detached houses have clean geometric shapes, saturated pastel colors, and simplified textures. Driveways, parked cars, trimmed lawns, and wooden fences are slightly oversized and toy-like, with smooth edges and bright highlights. The sky fades into a colorful dusk gradient beyond soft, rounded hills. Light dust puffs, stylized muzzle flashes, and glowing build seams remain clearly visible between rooftops and structures, with holographic placement outlines and snap-in construction effects emphasizing the fast-paced, game-like building mechanics.
MOOD: Hyper-urgent and aggressive, as suburb erupts into a vertical arena.
COLOR LOGIC: Hyperreal Pop Look
STYLE: ULTRA REALISTIC, LIVE ACTION

SCENE: They fight dynamically while deploying signature Fortnite-style build mechanics: pop-up walls materialize instantly with holographic previews snapping into solid cover, snap-lock ramps unfold with mechanical precision, and floor panels assemble mid-motion into bridges, elevated firing platforms, and defensive structures, creating a fast-paced, strategic combat environment with vibrant energy.

TIMELINE:
0:00-0:03: Over-the-shoulder wide shot behind the black-helmeted raider, third person down the street. She sprints and slams a wall marker onto the asphalt. Three panels pop up into a staircase beside a parked sedan. Fast steadicam follow, 24mm wide. SFX: (wind through yards, boots on asphalt, metallic pop, impact pings, distant gunfire). Hot rim light, cool fill.

0:03-0:05: Cut on action. Low over-the-shoulder shot behind the sand-jacketed raider. Two ramps spring out and a floor tile snaps between them, forming a zigzag climb to a garage roof. Fast handheld chase, 24mm spherical. SFX: (hinge snaps, wood thuds, cloth whip, ricochet ring, debris scatter). Sunset glare flashes through seams.

0:05-0:08: Whip pan transition. Third person trailing shot as both fighters spiral apart. A fresh wall stack punches upward, a side floor flips open, and the build jumps to three levels between roofline and driveway. Steadicam orbit, 28mm. SFX: (rapid ratchet, boots on panels, muffled bursts, debris scatter, build pops). Neon seams glow against the block.

0:08-0:11: Hard cut. Tight over-the-shoulder shot behind the black-helmeted raider at the top tier. She pulls a panel into head cover, peeks over it, and fires through the gaps. Fast dolly push with rack focus, 50mm anamorphic. SFX: (strained breathing, impact thuds, shell tinks, panel creak, brief silence before a violent burst). Rim light catches dust.

0:11-0:13: Smash cut. Bird's eye view. The sand-jacketed raider flips a bridge panel out and chains a floor beneath it, sprinting across as a second lane appears mid-run. Locked overhead then sudden pedestal drop, 28mm wide. SFX: (panel slam, latch click, boot hits, debris scatter, cloth snap). Electric highlights pulse through the seams.

0:13-0:15: Hard cut. Tight over-the-shoulder shot behind the black-helmeted raider as she lands on a platform above a driveway, pulls one last wall into cover, and aims at the rival crossing the bridge. Fast forward creep, 35mm anamorphic. SFX: (boots thud on panels, creaking build joints, final metallic ping, breath held then released). Dusk deepens.
