A fast-paced, high-energy stop-motion animation with a hand-crafted, tactile feel.

0-3s (Machine-Gun Weaving): Extreme macro. The camera shakes as fuzzy cotton yarn shoots out of a needle like a machine gun, rapidly stitching a pattern into empty space.

3-6s (Felt Collision): The camera barrel-rolls backwards. Thick strips of fuzzy felt powerfully snap together in choppy, hyper-fast stop-motion frames to form the upper shoe.
