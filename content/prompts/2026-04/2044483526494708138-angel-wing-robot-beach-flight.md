A futuristic sleek metallic humanoid robot with large mechanical angel wings, painted in light metallic cyan and silver with gold accents, flies dynamically over a beautiful tropical beach. The robot has a smooth helmet head with glowing circular visor eyes, detailed mechanical joints, and feather-like segmented wings that flap powerfully with motion blur.
The scene starts with a dramatic low-angle close-up as the winged robot flies straight toward the camera, wings beating powerfully, over a sunny sandy beach with turquoise ocean waves crashing on the shore. It then pulls back, soaring high into the bright blue sky, performing acrobatic maneuvers — diving, spinning, and gliding gracefully.
It lands dramatically on the soft sand, kicking up sand with its feet, then immediately takes off again in a powerful running jump. The robot continues flying over the open ocean, performing flips and dives, before plunging headfirst into the deep blue water, creating a massive splash with its legs visible kicking up before it disappears beneath the waves.
Cinematic lighting, golden hour sunlight, realistic physics, highly detailed 8K, dynamic camera angles with smooth tracking shots, intense motion blur on wings, epic and majestic atmosphere, photorealistic CGI style.
