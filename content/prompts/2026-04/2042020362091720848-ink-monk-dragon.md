The Ink Monk vs the Paper Dragon — a cinematic supernatural duel.

A silent monk in soaked black robes stands in an abandoned calligraphy hall during torrential rain. He fights using brushstroke sigils, flowing ink whips, and a lacquer-black staff. His enemy is a colossal paper dragon made of torn scrolls, black ink, red seals, and flapping calligraphy sheets.

The setting transitions from flooded script chamber → hanging scroll corridor → rooftop rain garden → giant ink basin courtyard.

0–3 seconds: the paper dragon erupts from the hanging scrolls with a storm of razor-edged pages and snapping paper jaws. The monk plants his staff, flicks two fingers, and black ink surges off the floor into a circular shield. Scrolls rip apart. Close-ups of dripping ink, paper claws, brush bristles, and the monk's calm eyes.

3–7 seconds: the dragon reforms across pillars and ceiling rails, attacking from above and behind with folding wings and scroll-body coils. The monk steps through ankle-deep ink-water, drawing glowing characters in the air with the tip of his staff. Each sigil turns into a lash that cuts through incoming paper limbs. Hanging scrolls ignite with black fire. Close-ups of wet sandals, ink ripples, torn calligraphy, and shaking wooden beams.

7–10 seconds: the duel explodes into the rooftop rain garden under lightning. The dragon circles above him, body widening into a storm-sized halo of paper rings and red stamp seals. The monk spins once and slams the staff down, sending a huge expanding ink mandala across the roof tiles that tears through the dragon's body in concentric waves. Orbit camera, rain, shredded paper everywhere.

10–12 seconds: rapid forward tracking shot as the monk runs over collapsing roof beams while the dragon condenses into a spearheaded paper cyclone. He leaps and drives straight through its throat with the staff, black ink bursting outward like blood.

12–15 seconds: overhead descent into the giant courtyard ink basin. The monk lands at the center of the basin while the dragon collapses around him into thousands of soaked sheets and floating red seals. Ink shockwaves crack stone and overturn bronze censers. Final frame: one white page lands in the rain with a perfect black circle painted on it.

Style: dark calligraphic fantasy, rain-soaked temple atmosphere, black ink physics, torn paper textures, elegant mysticism, thunder-lit contrasts, spiritual brush energy.
