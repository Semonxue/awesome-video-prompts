15-second absurd spectacle, photoreal ceremonial style. Same subject throughout: a tiny fluffy dog in a decorated ceremonial cape marching at the front of an enormous formal parade.

0–3s: low hero shot as the dog leads rows of drummers and banners with total confidence.
3–6s: the dog speeds up and the massive formation behind it struggles to keep perfect rhythm.
6–10s: overhead reveal shows the parade bending around city streets as if organized entirely by this tiny commander.
10–15s: the dog stops on a podium, gives one sharp bark, and the entire formation snaps into stillness with perfect precision.
