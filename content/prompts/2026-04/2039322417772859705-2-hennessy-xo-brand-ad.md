Movie-level Hennessy XO brand ad, hyper-realistic, continuous macro-to-intimate camera, extreme textural richness. Vibe: ancient, warm, patient, powerful — centuries compressed to seconds. An ancient Limousin oak in a thunderstorm becomes the barrel, the liquid, the bottle. No music — only rain, wood, liquid foley. 15 seconds, visual upgrade every 3 seconds.

【0-3s】THE TREE. A 300-year-old Limousin oak. Night. Thunderstorm RAGING. Camera in first-person racing UP the trunk — extreme close-up, bark texture filling frame. Rain cascading down deeply ridged surface — rivers in bark's canyons. Lightning STROBES — illuminating texture in frozen frames.

【3-5s】Camera reaches a lightning scar — exposed HEARTWOOD. Golden. Amber. Cognac-coloured. Rain seeps into tannin-rich grain. EXTREME close-up on wood rings — each a year, visible like geological strata. The grain's curves, density, amber colour through rain-wet surface TRANSITIONS into cognac liquid.

【5-8s】Inside the barrel. Camera floating in cognac — submerged in amber, light filtering from above through oak staves. Barrel's curved interior: char marks, wood grain, spirit-wood interaction at molecular level. Tannins leaching. Colour deepening. Liquid moves with viscosity that speaks of AGE.

【8-11s】Camera rises THROUGH liquid surface — breaking tension with satisfying visual pop. Inside the Hennessy XO bottle — ornate decanter shape, faceted glass refracting light. Being POURED. Camera rides the pour — out of bottle, through air, into crystal glass. Liquid falls in thick amber column — slow, heavy.

【11-13s】Camera pulls back — pour settling, glass a third full. Cognac catching warm light — amber gradients edge to center. In glass's reflection: the oak tree, thunderstorm, lightning flash. Still visible. The cognac REMEMBERS. Aromatic vapour rises from the surface.

【13-15s】Hero lock. Hennessy XO bottle and filled glass. Darkness, warm amber light. Rain on window behind. Text: HENNESSY XO "Three hundred years in the making."
