[STYLE]: high-energy stylized 3D animation, sharp features, strong silhouettes, dark gothic tone, high-contrast moonlit lighting, deep shadows, volumetric fog, cold blue and silver accents, subtle crimson highlights, cinematic depth of field, tense, dramatic, supernatural
