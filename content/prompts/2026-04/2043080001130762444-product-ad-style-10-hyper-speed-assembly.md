A cinematic, high-octane 3D product animation of a futuristic sneaker. Camera starts with a violent, high-speed zoom-in from a dark void into a single glowing white fiber. The camera then crashes through a forest of growing threads that are snapping together with mechanical precision. Cut to a 360-degree orbital "bullet-time" spin as the shoelaces whip through the air like snakes, weaving themselves into the eyelets. The sole of the shoe materializes through a liquid-metal splash effect, merging with the knit upper. Dynamic FPV-style camera movement dives under the arch and spirals up the heel. The background is a dark, atmospheric nebula of floating embers and shattered glass particles. The finale features the shoe suspended in a gravity-defying tilt while a surge of "magma-orange" light pulses through the translucent side-stripe, sending a shockwave of particles toward the lens.
