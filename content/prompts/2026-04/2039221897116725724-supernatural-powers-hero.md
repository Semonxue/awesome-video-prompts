Ultra-realistic live-action, shot like handheld iPhone footage. Natural overcast lighting, cold documentary tone, slight camera shake, occasional soft focus, lens dust/smudges.
Scene: Empty concrete basketball court under grey clouds. Cracked ground, rusty hoop, dust in air. Background: ~12 young players and bystanders, ambient dribbling and chatter.
Action (13s single take):
0–3s: Ground trembles, distant roar. A 3-story monster (dark grey skin, metallic spikes, glowing red eyes) bursts in, cracking concrete. Crowd panics. Camera finds a calm young man (early 20s, sweaty, white T-shirt) stepping forward as others flee.
3–6s: He walks casually, one hand in pocket. Other hand lifts—faint purple glow. Black nano-particles flow from his palm, crawling up his arm forming luminous armor lines.
6–9s: Four rotating particle rings form around his torso. He controls them with a subtle gesture—rings expand, spin, then collapse into dark metallic armor.
9–11s: Full transformation—armor spreads, shirt tears, black patterned robe revealed, leg armor forms. Hair lengthens slightly, arcane facial markings appear.
11–12s: He raises his hand, clenches fist—purple shockwave blasts the monster through the hoop.
12–13s: He turns away casually, walks back past stunned people, whistles. Looks at camera, finger-gun gesture, silently mouths: “{ Text to speech }”
End: Freeze frame, purple particles drifting, audio fades to wind.
