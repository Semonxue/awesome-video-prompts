STORY FORMAT: 15s / 150 BPM / MULTI-CUT / 1970s Hong Kong jiangshi horror-comedy / practical effects style / exaggerated martial arts and slapstick payoff

TONE: eerie tension → sudden panic → explosive taoist combat → stylish comedic finish

SETTING: Nighttime ancient Chinese street, dim lantern lighting, drifting fog, traditional wooden houses, ordinary animals in simple commoner clothing moving naturally

CHARACTERS:
Orange cat taoist: black taoist hat, grey robe, coin sword, calm but theatrical
White cat villager: soft pink traditional outfit, expressive, anxious
Black cat elder: wearing simple commoner clothing, visibly terrified
Jiangshi zombies: pale faces, stiff bodies, long green fingernails, hopping in sync, grotesque expressions

CAMERA STYLE: aggressive snap zooms, ultra-fast cuts, handheld shake, rhythmic impact editing, classic Hong Kong horror-comedy pacing

SCENE

0–1.5s
Wide shot
Orange cat walks through foggy street
Background animals in plain clothing pass by casually

Quick inserts (fast cuts)
Lantern flicker
Animal passerby glance
Footstep close-up

1.5–2.5s
Snap zoom
White cat rushes in, grabs sleeve

Extreme close-ups
White cat eyes (fear)
Trembling mouth
Tight grip on sleeve

White cat (panicked):
“Please! There’s a zombie in my house!”

2.5–4s
Rapid cuts sprint sequence

Feet pounding ground
Robes flapping
Narrow alley rush (handheld shake)
White cat looking back (fear)

Insert flash cuts (very short)
Jiangshi face snarling
Green long fingernails twitch
Dead eyes staring

4–5.5s
They burst into courtyard

Wide shot
Black cat curled in corner

Fast close-ups
Black cat shaking eyes
Sweat drop

Reveal jiangshi circling inward

Insert cuts
Multiple jiangshi faces (aggressive)
Claws flexing

“Save me!!”

Close-up
