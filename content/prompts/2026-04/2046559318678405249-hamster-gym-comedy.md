A tiny golden hamster with a headband and absurdly small tank top flexes in the mirror of a human-sized gym, surrounded by equipment thousands of times his size. High budget 3D animation, quality lighting and fur rendering.

[0–1.5s] The hamster struts into frame at floor level, doing a slow-motion walk. Dramatic gym montage lighting. He cracks his tiny knuckles. A protein shake (a thimble of milk) sits nearby. He downs it. Milk mustache. He nods at his reflection: "Beast mode."

[1.5–3s] He approaches a dumbbell. It's a regular human dumbbell. He wraps his arms around it. Pulls. Strains. His cheeks puff. His eyes bulge. It doesn't move one millimeter. He pretends he was just stretching.

[3–5s] He finds a Cheerio on the floor, rolls it to the bench press, and "lifts" it with maximum effort. Grunting. Veins popping on his tiny forehead. He racks it and flexes triumphantly like he just broke a world record. "LIGHTWEIGHT!"

[5–7s] He spots a cat on the treadmill next door — a bored Persian walking at setting 1. The hamster cranks his own treadmill to maximum. His legs become a blur. He's launched off the back into a stack of yoga mats, creating a hamster-shaped crater.

[7–9s] He climbs a pull-up bar (the towel rack). Does one shaking pull-up. Gets stuck at the top. Hangs there. A gym-goer hangs their towel on him, not noticing. He dangles beneath the towel, whispering: "This counts as a set."

[9–11s] He discovers a hamster wheel someone left as a joke. His eyes light up. He SPRINTS. The wheel spins so fast it detaches from the stand, rolls across the gym floor with him inside, bowling through a yoga class.

[11–13s] The wheel crashes into the smoothie bar. He tumbles out, covered in blended kale. He stands up. A huge bodybuilder dog (a pit bull in a tank top) looks down at him. Silence. The hamster flexes one arm up at him. "You mirin'?"

[13–15s] The pit bull flexes back. The hamster passes out from intimidation, falling backward stiff as a board. Cut to: he's being carried out on a stretcher made of popsicle sticks by two concerned mice paramedics. He gives a thumbs up to camera. Cut to black.

Style: 3D animated, quality fur/lighting, gym comedy, hamster slapstick, motivational parody, cinematic 4K.
