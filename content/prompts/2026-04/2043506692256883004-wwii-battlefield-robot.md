Use the uploaded WWII battlefield image as the exact opening frame and visual reference. Preserve the same tank, soldiers, muddy battlefield, ruined buildings, smoke, and dramatic sun rays. The visual style must remain photorealistic cinematic war footage.

Create a 15-second Hollywood-level VFX sequence during an intense WWII battle.

0:00–0:04 — Battlefield Chaos
The tank fires its cannon while soldiers shoot from trenches. Explosions erupt around the battlefield, mud and debris flying everywhere. Tracer rounds cut through the smoky air. Camera shakes from nearby artillery.

0:04–0:08 — Mechanical Shift
The main tank suddenly stops. Deep metallic sounds begin. Armor plates vibrate and glowing seams appear across the tank. The turret rotates and locks as soldiers nearby look confused.

0:08–0:12 — Transformation
The tank violently unfolds into a giant mechanical war robot. Tracks split into legs, armor plates slide into arms, the turret forms shoulders, and the cannon becomes an arm weapon. Complex mechanical parts rotate and interlock with sparks, steam, and dust falling from the metal.

0:12–0:15 — Robot Reveal
The towering robot rises from the battlefield. Its eyes glow. It raises the arm cannon and fires a massive energy blast that destroys an incoming tank. Smoke and sunlight beams surround the robot in an epic cinematic final shot.
