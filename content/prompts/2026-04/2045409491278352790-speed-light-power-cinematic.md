Reference character consistency: Keep exact face, hairstyle, outfit, and proportions from the reference image. Realistic human appearance, cinematic lighting, grounded physics.

🌇 ENVIRONMENT

A vast floating sky-world at sunset — broken platforms, suspended highways, and ancient ruins drifting above glowing clouds. Warm golden-orange light cuts through deep shadows. Wind flows constantly, carrying dust and light particles through the air.

⚡ ACTION FLOW (15s CONTINUOUS)

0–2s
Wide aerial shot. The character is already moving at extreme speed across floating platforms. Each step creates subtle air shock ripples and trailing particles. Camera tracks from a dynamic side-rear angle.

2–4s
A massive energy phantom forms ahead — a humanoid figure made of shifting light fragments and dark cracks. Its eyes glow gold-white. It glitches forward instantly, closing distance unnaturally fast.

4–6s
She reacts mid-motion — flips backward in the air, narrowly dodging a slicing energy strike. While spinning, she channels light into her arm and strikes forward. Impact releases a sharp burst of glowing particles.

6–9s
She lands and sprints along a narrow floating path at high speed. The phantom splits into multiple copies, surrounding her. She slides under one attack, then jumps between fragments, striking them mid-air.

9–12s (HOOK MOMENT)
Time slows dramatically.
One phantom appears directly behind her — almost touching.
Particles freeze mid-air.
She tilts her head slightly… then vanishes in a flash.

12–15s (CLIMAX)
She reappears above and dives down at full speed with a charged light strike.
A massive energy burst spreads across the sky, shattering all phantom fragments into glowing dust.
The chaos dissolves into calm golden air.

Final frame:
She stands still on a floating platform, wind moving softly, looking forward.
