A lone explorer with a headlamp enters a vast arctic ice cave, crystalline blue walls glittering in the narrow beam of light. Slow push-in follows through the cave entrance, each step crunching on frost, breath visible in the freezing air, the headlamp illuminating intricate ice formations that stretch into darkness. A deep rumble shakes loose ice from the ceiling. The camera shifts to urgent shaky handheld as the explorer spins around, the headlamp catching glimpses of something massive moving in the shadows, sickly pale green bioluminescent veins pulsing along the cave walls. The perspective snaps to a wide static shot as the creature emerges fully, a towering insectoid horror covered in translucent ice-like chitin, green light pulsing from within its body, dwarfing the terrified explorer. Glacial blue ice environment, bioluminescent creature design, horror lighting. 16:9 cinematic.
