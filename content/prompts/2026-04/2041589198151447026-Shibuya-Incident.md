CHARACTERS: Define one figure in a Japanese festival mask with a staff at the curb, recoiling pedestrians at the sidewalk edge, and a colossal serpentine abyssal titan with a ridge-backed silhouette and city-scale mass that rises after the second strike.
STAGE: Set Shibuya Crossing at night after rain with LED billboards, reflective asphalt, moving traffic, and clear geography before the street turns to liquid, keeps swallowing traffic, and opens beneath the masked figure.
EMOTIONAL TARGET: Build from dread into epic ascent.
COLOR LOGIC: Neon Noir

TIMELINE:
00:00.0: Open on a high wide over the curb lanes at Shibuya Crossing. Hold the figure in a Japanese festival mask still against the crowd blur. SFX: crossing beeps, shoe chatter.
00:01.2: Hard cut to a 50mm dolly push on the Japanese mask. Let billboard light crawl across its lacquered surface. SFX: city wash narrows.
00:02.4: Insert cut to the right hand striking the asphalt with the staff for the first hit. Show the impact point clearly. SFX: wood crack, low hum.
00:03.6: Match cut to a ripple blasting through reflected neon on the road surface. Keep the ring centered and fast. SFX: sub-bass pulse.
00:04.8: Smash cut to a 28mm low wide. Let the asphalt soften and turn viscous around the first impact point before the liquid begins to spread. SFX: tar tear, liquid surge.
00:06.0: Hard cut to a front three-quarter wide as Shibuya lane paint slowly sags under the creeping surface. Drive the liquid outward in a heavy crawl. SFX: bubbling drain, horn choke.
00:07.2: Hard cut to moving cars starting to drop nose-first and sink mid-motion as the softened street finally gives way under them. Hold the delayed weight shift long enough to read it. SFX: horn choke, metal groan, glass rattle.
00:08.4: Hard cut wider as pedestrians recoil along the sidewalk edge and more moving cars keep dropping into the black surface behind them. Keep the masked figure centered on an unbroken patch of liquid. SFX: shouts, wave slap, horn choke.
00:09.6: Cut on action to a 35mm push-in. Let the masked figure plant the staff into that black surface for the second strike without breaking calm while cars continue sinking at the edges of frame. SFX: bass hit, rain hiss, metal groan.
00:10.8: Hard cut to a slow 85mm orbit. Reveal the black surface doming under the masked figure as the collapse continues behind them, then split it open in a tight circle around their feet. SFX: deep groan, resonance, distant impacts.
00:12.0: Land on a 24mm crane pullback. Reveal a colossal serpentine abyssal titan rising through that opening directly under the masked figure and lifting them on its head above the liquefied Shibuya Crossing while its long ridge-backed body coils through the flooded intersection below. SFX: rain roar, vertical surge, ominous note.
00:13.2: Continue into a full 360 orbit around the serpentine abyssal titan and the masked figure high above the crossing. Keep the city spinning below them while the titan's body coils through the black street and the ascent holds steady. SFX: rain hiss, deep resonance, distant horns.

EXCLUDE: Avoid drifting facial identity, cartoon liquid physics, unreadable lane continuity, text overlays, and camera HUD.
