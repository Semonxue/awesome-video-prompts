Ultra-cinematic 15-second VFX transformation sequence of a human evolving into a fire-powered superhero at night.

0–2s: Close-up of a normal human standing still, breathing steady, neutral lighting, slight camera push-in.

2–4s: Subtle heat distortion begins around the body, air shimmering, faint orange glow forming beneath the skin, eyes tightening with intensity.

4–6s: First ignition — small flames spark along the arms and shoulders, flickering upward, light reflecting on skin, expression shifting to focus.

6–8s: Energy surge — flames grow stronger, wrapping around the body in controlled streams, clothing begins to morph into a sleek, fire-resistant suit with glowing ember-like textures.

8–10s: Full body transformation — musculature sharpens, posture becomes powerful, eyes ignite with a bright internal glow, flames intensify around hands and back.

10–12s: Peak VFX — a burst of fire expands outward in a circular wave, sparks and embers flying in all directions, heat distortion bending the background.

12–14s: Stabilization — flames settle into a controlled aura, fire flowing upward continuously from shoulders and arms, subtle ember particles drifting.

14–15s: Final hero pose — character stands still, hand slightly raised with fire swirling above the palm, calm and dominant presence.

Camera: slow cinematic push-in with slight handheld micro movement, no cuts, continuous shot.

Lighting: transitions from neutral tones to intense warm fire glow, high contrast with dynamic highlights.

Environment: dark urban or neutral background reacting to heat with light distortion and particle movement.

Style: ultra-cinematic realism, high VFX fire simulation, smooth transformation, volumetric flames, heat distortion, no text, no overlays, stable proportions without stretch.
