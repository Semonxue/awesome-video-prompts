SHOT 1 (0:00–0:02) — DEEP CHAIN ASCENT
Digital zoom scale-out + depth-of-field rack focus. Camera starts extreme close on anchor chain links in deep blue water, silhouetted against diffused surface light. Frame slowly scales back — ship hull shadow looms above. Cold blue grade, volumetric god rays cutting through water. Hard cut upward.
SHOT 2 (0:02–0:04) — SURFACE SPLIT REVEAL ⭐ SIGNATURE
Over-under waterline split + slow push-up. Camera crosses the waterline — bottom half: anchor resting on sandy turquoise seabed, chain rising. Top half: white sailboat hull, open sky, clouds. The split horizon held for 1.5 seconds. Breath moment. Dissolve upward.
SHOT 3 (0:04–0:06) — ANCHOR ON GLITTER DECK
Rack focus snap + bokeh shimmer. Close on the steel anchor resting against a teal sequined cushion aboard a luxury yacht. Background ocean bokeh pulses with diamond light scatter. Camera drifts laterally at 15% speed. Warm silver grade. Hard cut.
SHOT 4 (0:06–0:07) — WATCH + MAP CLOSE ⭐ SIGNATURE
Macro push-in + depth-of-field pull. Extreme close on compass rose watch face — North marker centered. Camera pulls focus to reveal nautical chart beneath, silver compass beside it. Ocean glitter in background, out of focus. Single silver light sweep across the watch crystal. Cut on highlight peak.
SHOT 5 (0:07–0:09) — STORM LIGHTHOUSE STRIKE
Speed ramp deceleration + motion blur crash. Massive wave erupts around lighthouse at full speed — at peak white foam crown, frame drops to 20% speed. Blue-grey teal grade, lighthouse beacon glows cold white through spray. The only dark, forceful moment. Smash cut to silence.
SHOT 6 (0:09–0:11) — HELM IN SPRAY
Slow rotation lock + water splash freeze. Wooden ship's wheel fills frame — wave spray erupts through the spokes from left. At peak splash impact, frame holds at 15% speed, water droplets suspended mid-air around dark oak grain. Camera locked, no movement. Cut on freeze.
SHOT 7 (0:11–0:13) — OPEN OCEAN GLIDE
Lateral drift + lens flare sweep. Wide shot from yacht deck — open deep blue ocean stretching to horizon. Camera glides smoothly right. Sun hits lens at frame edge, triggering a clean silver-white flare. Serene. Low density. The calm after the storm. Slow dissolve to near-black.
SHOT 8 (0:13–0:15) — LOGO ON DEEP BLUE
Fade-up from dark ocean blue. White or silver text rises cleanly on deep navy. Single slow pulse of light across the type — like a lighthouse beam passing once. Settles.
