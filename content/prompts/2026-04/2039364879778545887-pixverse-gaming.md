POV from the perspective of a lone warrior sprinting at extreme speed across a violent desert battlefield in a single continuous take. The shot begins atop a sand dune before the warrior launches downward into a raging dust storm and lands in the chaos below. Instantly, they accelerate forward, racing through a forest of clashing soldiers, squeezing through narrow gaps between swinging weapons that cut through the air like blades. Sunlight flashes across flying sand as the warrior skims over it at insane speed. They dodge a collapsing spear that crashes beside the lens, then climb vertically up a shattered war machine like a towering cliff face. Final moment: the warrior reaches the top and stops, overlooking the entire storm-covered battlefield below.
