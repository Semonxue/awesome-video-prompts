I prefer stories with a twist. Instead of showing a king as a hero, make him a coward.

This video was done using Seedance 2 in @freepik Spaces. Link and prompt example below.

I split everything down into scenes of roughly 10-15 and build these one by one. Often grabbing frames from a previous video and use them as reference images for consistency.

Check the workflow in Freepik Spaces - link in comments.

And here's a Seedance 2 prompt for you.

[STYLE] Hyper-realistic cinematic dark fantasy, dramatic raking light, shallow depth of field, night, smoky battlefield atmosphere.  close ups. emotional. teal moonlight. and a single torch are the only light sources. depth of field. extreme close ups. intense atmosphere.

teal and orange colors.

[FORMAT] 16:9, cinematic multi-shot scene

[CHARACTERS] 

A coward king. he's watching his army loose against the undead.
a women holding a child arguing with the king.

[SOUND] No music. No subtitles. SFX: subtle fighting and screams in the background from the kings army who are loosing.

A cinematic scene.  a dimly lit room. Only moon light and a single torch in the background. the faces are almost all dark with high contrast.

The king is looking out the window/hole with metal bars (no glass) looking of the castle tower overseeing the battlefield high in a towers. 

his men getting slaughtered by the 'undead' army. they can hear distant screams.
a woman behind him tells him that his men are loosing and he should be there with them. she's desperate to save the lives of women and children. they know they will all die if the loose. but he turns to her. a moment of silence as he considers his words. he then argues that no one can win agains the undead.
