CANNONBALL TRACKING SHOT — 15-second cinematic naval masterpiece

A historical naval scene begins on the wooden deck of a battered pirate galleon engulfed in thick morning fog. A soot-covered gunner stands holding a burning match to the fuse of a massive bronze cannon. Tense, damp atmosphere, sea eerily calm.

The fuse burns down, the cannon erupts with a deafening boom and huge black smoke cloud, launching a heavy iron cannonball.

The camera immediately accelerates and locks perfectly behind the cannonball mid-flight. It tracks directly behind the rusted sphere as it skims inches above the ocean surface. Gray water and fog whip past in heavy motion blur, while the pitted iron ball stays razor sharp in center frame. Air roar intensifies.

The British frigate's wooden hull grows rapidly larger ahead. Final seconds: tight close-up as the cannonball slams dead center into the oak planks. Extreme close-up of wood violently splintering inward. Freeze on the chaotic destruction.

Style: photorealistic, dramatic foggy lighting, hyper-realistic cannonball physics and motion tracking, intense naval warfare atmosphere, high detail on wood splintering and water spray.
