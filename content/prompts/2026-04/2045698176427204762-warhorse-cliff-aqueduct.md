Warhorse sprint on a cliff aqueduct (15 seconds, single continuous shot)
A sweeping wide of ancient cliffs and a narrow aqueduct clinging to them. The camera dives to a tiny moving spark: a rider on a warhorse sprinting impossibly fast along the stone channel. Lock-on: hooves strike, water splashes, stone chips fly. The camera slingshots past, whips around, and drops tight to hoof-level: droplets and grit snapping off with each impact. The aqueduct cracks; sections tilt. The rider makes a last-second lateral leap across a broken span, and the camera roll-matches the jump, skimming the water's surface below. A rockslide slams into the far side; the pair thread between falling blocks, then burst through a mist cloud from a cascading spillway. Final reveal: the camera rockets up to show the aqueduct pouring into a sunlit terraced oasis city, suddenly serene.
