Part 1 - 15 seconds prompt

[0-3s] Wide cinematic tracking shot following a weathered, dark-wood exploration ship sailing through a turbulent, ink-black arctic ocean. Beside it looms a colossal, jagged blue iceberg. Lighting is moody and overcast with volumetric fog and deep subsurface scattering penetrating the translucent, frost-covered glacial ice. 

[4-7s] Rapid, handheld camera push-in on the iceberg's cliff face as it violently shifts. A gargantuan, glowing bioluminescent cyan reptilian eye snaps open, fracturing the ice texture into highly detailed, cascading shards of crystalline frost. 

[8-11s] Sweeping crane shot tilting upwards as the disguised leviathan unfurls massive, ice-crusted wings, shattering its camouflage. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting highlights the wet, jagged scales as the colossal beast dives plunging beneath the churning ocean surface, kicking up explosive hyper-realistic seafoam and mist. 

[12-15s] Extreme low-angle dynamic shot from the ship's rocking deck. The dark water abruptly erupts as the towering ice-dragon breaches at high speed, thousands of water droplets cascading off its glacial armor. The camera shakes violently as the beast lunges directly into the lens with jaws unhinged, blocking out the sky.

part 2 - 15 seconds prompt

[0-2s] Extreme low-angle dynamic shot: A roaring wave of bioluminescent blue fire erupts across a dark, rain-slicked wooden ship deck, illuminating the dynamic, terrified movements of 18th-century sailors. [2-3s] Time instantly freezes in a dramatic flash of cyan light; the turbulent fire abruptly solidifies into jagged, glowing geometric ice structures. 

[3-11s] bullet-time continuous pan: The camera slowly and smoothly weaves through the 3D frozen diorama. Sailors are suspended mid-action and mid-air, encased in hyper-realistic, translucent sapphire ice. Highly detailed materiality: intricate frost patterns instantly webbing across wet wool coats, suspended perfectly spherical water droplets catching ambient light like glass beads, and facial expressions locked in crystalline paralysis. Volumetric moonlight and the internal ethereal glow of the frozen blue fire cast deep, dramatic cinematic shadows. 

[11-15s] Macro dolly-in: The camera glides intimately close to a suspended sailor's face, utilizing shallow depth of field to highlight the subsurface scattering of the translucent ice, the sharp refractive edges of the crystallized fire, and microscopic frost spores glittering in the frigid, suspended atmosphere. 8k resolution, photorealistic, cinematic lighting.
