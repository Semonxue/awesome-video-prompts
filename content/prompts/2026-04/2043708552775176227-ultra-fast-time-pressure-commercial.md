An ultra-fast, high-intensity commercial driven by time pressure.

0-2s: Extreme close-up heartbeat pulse → instantly match cut to ticking watch.

2-5s: Rapid cuts: running / climbing / falling / jumping  all synced with ticking sound.

5-8s: Screen flashes metrics: heart rate, speed, calories glitch transitions.

8-12s: Freeze mid-air jump → time slows → only watch UI still moving.

12-15s: Smash cut to product close-up → text: "Own Every Second."
