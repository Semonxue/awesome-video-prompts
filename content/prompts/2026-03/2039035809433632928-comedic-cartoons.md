description: |
    15-second continuous single-shot cartoon sequence.
    No cuts. No scene transitions.

    Stylized 3D cartoon animation, vibrant colors, strong contrast lighting, exaggerated expressions, extreme squash and stretch, elastic motion, dynamic camera, high energy

    Clear motion direction, escalating chaos, readable action, no jitter

    Scene:

    Living room at night, simple layout (sofa, table, lamp), dark environment with strong light contrast

    0–3s — obsession trigger

    Camera low, slowly pushing forward.

    A small cat freezes completely still.

    A tiny red laser dot appears on the floor.

    The cat’s pupils expand instantly to full size.

    Tail stiff. Silence.

    3–6s — ignition

    The dot moves suddenly.

    The cat EXPLODES into motion.

    Instant acceleration:

    – slides across floor
    – claws scraping
    – body stretching forward

    Camera snaps into fast tracking motion.

    6–10s — escalation (controlled madness)

    Laser moves faster and more erratically.

    The cat escalates:

    – runs along the wall horizontally
    – jumps off furniture
    – flips mid-air
    – rebounds between surfaces

    But motion stays readable (clean arcs).

    Objects react:

    – lamp swings
    – cushions fly
    – table slides slightly

    Camera follows in dynamic arcs, not chaotic.

    10–12s — peak absurdity (slow motion)

    Laser suddenly stops mid-air on the wall.

    Time slows.

    The cat launches in a perfect exaggerated jump:

    – limbs fully stretched
    – mouth open
    – eyes locked

    Everything suspended:

    – dust
    – particles
    – fabric mid-motion

    12–15s — punchline

    Time snaps back.

    The cat SLAMS into the wall…

    The laser dot is now on its own nose.

    Instant freeze.

    Eyes go cross-eyed.

    Tail slowly drops.

    Freeze.
