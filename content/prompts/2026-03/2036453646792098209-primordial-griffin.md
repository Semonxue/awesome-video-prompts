FORMAT: 15s / 6 SHOTS / single continuous chase energy / no dialogue STYLE: Primordial giant forest, golden hour god-rays, floating spores, emerald moss vs glowing purple magical runes, epic fantasy realism Shot 01 (0:00-0:02) Swoop down from the canopy of world-trees toward a heavily armored griffin diving alongside a trunk the size of a mountain. Shot 02 (0:02-0:04) Camera paces the griffin's beating wings, slingshots past the rider's gripped reins, and snaps back to the glowing rune-carved tail feathers leaving trails of light. Shot 04 (0:07-0:10) The flight path funnels into a hollowed-out log suspended in vines, packed with glowing mushrooms and darting fairies; the camera threads the glowing obstacles. Shot 05 (0:10-0:13) Bursting from the log into an open grove, a sudden updraft kicks a flurry of dandelion-like seeds across the lens while the griffin's armor flares with protective magic. Shot 06 (0:13-0:15) The camera halts in mid-air as the griffin banks away, revealing an endless ocean of golden treetops surrounding a majestic elven castle in the clouds.
