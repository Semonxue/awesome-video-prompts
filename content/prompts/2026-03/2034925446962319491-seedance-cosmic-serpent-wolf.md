Prompt :

A surreal cosmic desert beneath a gigantic moon hanging impossibly close to the horizon. The ground is composed of smooth white sand dunes reflecting pale blue moonlight while fragments of broken planets float slowly in the sky.

**Action:**

15.0s sequence. A massive celestial serpent with scales glowing like constellations slithers across the dunes. A giant lunar wolf with silver fur and glowing eyes emerges from the shadows of the dunes.

They clash across the surreal landscape as the serpent coils through the air while the wolf leaps between floating planetary fragments.

Velocity Ramp choreography: the wolf's mid-air bite slows dramatically as cosmic dust and starlight particles swirl around the impact before snapping back as the serpent coils and launches them both across the dunes.

**Camera:**

Sweeping cinematic crane shot gliding across the dunes before diving low to track the wolf sprinting across the sand.

**Style & Constraints:**

Photorealistic fur, cosmic volumetric lighting, ray-traced moon reflections on sand, cinematic depth-of-field, 8K resolution.
