Prompt on Kling 3.0 (Image to Video):

Shot 1: Low wide tracking shot from behind the red-cloaked group as they walk slowly toward the glowing tree across the white valley floor. The giant face on the right subtly begins turning toward the left, stone creaking, dust shedding from its surface.

Shot 2: Medium close shot on two walkers stopping abruptly, both turning toward each other in shock, pale reflected light on their faces, distant rumble echoing. One whispers, "Did it move?"

Shot 3: Hard cut to a breathtaking aerial crane-up, rising high above the valley into a godlike overhead view. As the camera ascends, the left giant finally answers in a deep ancient voice that rolls across the landscape: "Dreamers… you have returned."

Shot 4: Elegant descending orbit around the central tree. The white horse walks slowly toward the trunk with unnatural calm. As the horse touches the tree, its body dissolves into pale particles and flowing light, vanishing into the bark as if absorbed into memory. The ground ripples outward in soft concentric distortions.

Shot 5: The world begins collapsing into surreal instability. The red-cloaked figures brace themselves as reality peels open around them. Camera whips upward and backward in a dizzying dream-collapse, beautiful not horrific.

Shot 6: Cut to a sleek sci-fi dream research facility. A small group of people sleep in a circular chamber, connected to futuristic interfaces, sharing dream projections of the exact surreal valley. One wall-sized hologram shows the tree and giant faces. The atmosphere is hushed, awestruck, luminous blue-white. Final camera push inward as the group watches the shared dream together, revealing that the entire vision is a collective dream space.
