title: No Way Back
description: |
    Subject 1: A slim rookie boxer with messy short black curly hair, sweat dripping, visibly trembling; oversized gloves, hunched posture; never attacks, only retreats and pushes backward in panic

    Subject 2: A tall, powerful skilled boxer with slicked-back dark brown hair, sharp jawline; precise, aggressive forward pressure

    Visual distinction:

    Rookie: red trunks
    Skilled boxer: black trunks

    Style: Stylized 3D animation with exaggerated forms, strong silhouettes, squash-and-stretch, elastic motion, and clear physical cause-and-effect

    ENVIRONMENT:

    Professional boxing ring; highly elastic ropes capable of large visible deformation; slightly bouncy canvas; strong overhead spotlight; clear readable space from center to ropes

    MOOD:

    Escalating pressure through continuous retreat; tension builds until physical limit is reached; explosive release driven by stored elastic energy

    COLOR LOGIC:

    High contrast lighting; warm fighters vs cool dark background; red vs black trunks for strong identity

    TIMELINE:
    0:00-0:02 【Beat 1】

    Extreme close-up

    Rookie sweating, trembling

    Cut to wide — skilled boxer advancing

    0:02-0:04 【Beat 2】

    Punch

    Rookie steps backward

    Miss

    0:04-0:07 【Beat 3】

    Continuous retreat

    All punches miss due to distance

    Ropes appear behind rookie

    0:07-0:09 【Beat 4】

    Rookie steps back

    Touches ropes for the first time

    NO deformation yet

    Still trying to move backward

    0:09-0:11 【Beat 5】

    CRITICAL MOMENT

    The skilled boxer drives forward

    The rookie pushes backward strongly in panic

    His body is forced deep into the ropes

    IMPORTANT:
    The ropes undergo large, exaggerated deformation
    They stretch far backward, clearly storing energy

    The rookie’s entire body is compressed into the ropes

    He is still trying to push backward

    Final punch — misses

    0:11-0:15 【Beat 6】

    Release

    The ropes snap back powerfully

    The rookie is launched forward with force

    He stumbles

    His arm swings uncontrollably —
    NOT a trained punch

    The glove crashes into the jaw

    The skilled boxer is still moving forward → impact amplified

    He is knocked down hard onto the canvas

    Crowd explodes

    The rookie stands frozen, completely stunned

    MANDATORY RULES:

    The rookie NEVER attacks intentionally

    The rookie ONLY retreats and pushes backward

    The ropes MUST show large deformation before release

    The deformation must be clearly visible and exaggerated

    The final punch is caused by stored elastic energy + momentum

    Skilled boxer must fall fully

    NEGATIVE PROMPT:

    no small rope movement, no weak rope deformation, no early bounce, no rookie attack, no controlled punch, no soft impact
