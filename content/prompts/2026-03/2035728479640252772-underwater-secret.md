    Subject 1: A dedicated fishing enthusiast, wearing a fishing hat or bucket hat, casual outdoor fishing clothes, loose jacket or vest, and practical shoes; sitting on a small stool while fishing; relaxed but focused posture.
    Subject 2: A mischievous cartoon fish with an oversized, highly expressive mouth, smooth rubber-like surface, large expressive eyes, and a flexible mouth line; lies lazily on a small beach chair underwater.

    Style: Stylized 3D animation, rounded volumes, simplified geometry, soft edges, and expressive motion.

    ENVIRONMENT:

    A warm lakeside wooden dock with soft worn planks above water; a small stool placed on the dock where the fisherman sits; underwater is a clean sandy floor with soft light rays and subtle particles; warm light above transitions to cool tones underwater

    TIMELINE:
    0:00-0:02

    Medium shot, 35mm, slight low angle.
    The fisherman sits on a small stool on the dock, holding the rod steadily; the float rests on the water, calm and still.

    0:02-0:04

    Cut, same framing.
    The line reacts slightly.

    The fisherman lifts the rod in a natural motion.

    The hook rises clearly into frame with no bait attached at all.

    There is no worm, no residue, and nothing hanging from the hook.

    He looks at it and pauses—confused.

    0:04-0:06

    Two more attempts:
    lift → hook enters frame completely empty.

    Each time, the hook is visibly bare with nothing attached.

    His expression tightens: brows pull down, lips press, showing frustration and unwillingness to accept the repeated failure.

    0:06-0:08

    The camera smoothly pushes forward and dips below the surface into the underwater space.

    As the camera settles underwater:
    The fish lies on a beach chair.
    A hook carrying a worm drops vertically into frame.

    The fish performs a precise sequence:
    one pectoral fin reaches out → grips the worm → pulls it off the hook → moves it toward its large mouth → places it fully inside → closes its mouth to eat → then reaches to the fishing line and gently pulls it once.

    After the pull:
    the hook is pulled upward and exits the frame.

    0:08-0:10

    Same underwater shot continues.

    After a short pause:
    the hook re-enters from above with a new worm attached → the fish repeats the full sequence:
    reach → grab → remove → move to mouth → eat → pull line → hook lifts up again.

    0:10-0:12

    Push-in to close-up.
    The fish turns toward the camera, its large mouth subtly stretching into a crooked smirk, then closes one eye in a deliberate single wink.
